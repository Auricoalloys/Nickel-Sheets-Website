Our Products

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nickel, titanium and special steel in every major mill form, from Mumbai. Each form below covers the same twelve alloy families, so the choice is which shape you need first and which grade second.

Material is supplied cut to size against EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates, held in stock or arranged with the manufacturer depending on grade and quantity.

Flat Products

Rolled flat forms, twelve alloy families in each
Form What it is Usually bought for
Sheets Rolled flat stock, the thinner of the two flat gauges Fabricated vessels, linings, ducting and formed components
Plates Rolled flat stock in the heavier gauges Pressure parts, tube sheets, structural and machined blanks
Coil Continuous rolled strip, supplied on the coil Feeding presses, roll-forming and slitting to width
Foil The thinnest rolled gauge, down to a few hundredths of a millimetre Shielding, gaskets, bellows, heat exchange and electronics work

Bar, Wire and Hollow Sections

Long products and sections
Form What it is Usually bought for
Round Bars Solid round section, hot rolled, forged or peeled Turned parts, shafts, fasteners and forging stock
Hex Bars Solid hexagonal section Nuts, fittings and anything turned from across-flats stock
Hollow Bars Bar with a bored centre, so the hole is not machined out Bushes, sleeves and cylinders, saving both stock and cycle time
Wire Drawn round section, supplied on spools or in coil Springs, welding consumables, mesh and fasteners

Tube, Pipe and Fittings

Tubular products
Form What it is Usually bought for
Tubes Sized on outside diameter and wall thickness Heat exchangers, condensers and instrumentation
Pipes Sized on nominal bore and schedule Process piping and pressure systems
Fittings Elbows, tees, reducers, caps, flanges and stub ends Joining and redirecting a piping run in the same alloy
