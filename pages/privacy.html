Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information Aurico Alloys LLP collects through www.nickelsheets.com, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It applies to this website and to enquiries you send us through it.

Who we are

Aurico Alloys LLP, 112, 1st Floor, Aman Grih, Carpenter 1st Street, C.P. Tank, Mumbai-400004, Maharashtra, India. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact info@auricoalloys.com or call +91 22 6743 6611.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit an enquiry or request a quotation we collect your name, company, email address, telephone number, country and the details of your requirement. We collect this only because you chose to send it.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours records standard technical data through analytics: pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, referring site, browser and device type, and time spent on the site.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To process, fulfil and document an order, including export paperwork where applicable.
  • To keep in contact about an active or recent enquiry.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.
  • To meet legal, tax and export-control record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without your agreement.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics sets cookies that record your visit in aggregate; the reports we see do not identify you personally. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking cookies does not stop you using the site.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is necessary: with logistics, freight-forwarding and customs agents to deliver an order; with inspection agencies where you have asked for third-party inspection; with our bank for payment; with professional advisers; and where the law requires it. Service providers act on our instructions and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

Where it is held and for how long

Enquiry and order records are held on systems operated by us and by our service providers, and may be processed outside India where those providers operate. We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and any resulting relationship, and transaction records for as long as tax, customs and export-control rules require.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct anything inaccurate, or to delete information we no longer need to keep. You may also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Write to info@auricoalloys.com and we will respond within a reasonable period. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you send us. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send us sensitive financial details by email.

Other websites

Pages here may link to sites we do not control. This policy does not cover them, and we are not responsible for their content or their handling of your data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
