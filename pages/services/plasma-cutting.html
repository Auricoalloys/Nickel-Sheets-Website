Specs, Product Grades & Compliance

Aurico Alloys LLP’s plasma lines work with stainless (ASTM A240/UNS S31603), duplex (ASTM A789/UNS S32205), nickel alloys (ASTM B265/UNS N06625), and aluminum with thickness up to 100mm and edge accuracy of ±0.2mm. All jobs are certified to retain original mechanical (tensile >75 ksi, elongation ~35%) and chemical (Cr, Ni, Mo ratios) specs.

Every cut is globally traceable under ASTM and Unified Numbering System standards, ensuring critical materials meet project and regulatory needs.
