Material Grades, Standards & Technical Precision

Our stamping capabilities extend across metals engineered to comply with UNS and ASTM standards. Materials like stainless steel (UNS S31603 / ASTM A240), duplex steel (UNS S32205 / ASTM A789), and aluminum (UNS A96061 / ASTM B209) offer proven performance across pressure vessels, structural assemblies, and thermal systems.

Mechanical properties such as yield strengths of 205–290 MPa for stainless steel and elongation up to 40% ensure both strength and ductility under elevated stress. Stamped parts achieve close tolerances of ±0.05 mm suitable for sub-assemblies, gaskets, flanges, and custom connectors critical in high-risk industrial infrastructure.

Efficiency Meets Compliance

Each stamped part undergoes rigorous quality testing, ensuring compliance with ASTM A666 (stainless steel), ASTM B488 (copper plating), and ASTM E527 for UNS numbering consistency.
