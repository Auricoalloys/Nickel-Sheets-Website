Engineering Precision with Global Standards

Aurico Alloys delivers shearing services compliant with key ASTM and UNS standards, ensuring every cut meets both strength and dimensional accuracy requirements. Common materials include stainless steels such as ASTM A240 (UNS S31603) and duplex grades like ASTM A789 (UNS S32205), known for their corrosion resistance and stability under extreme operating conditions. Other materials such as ASTM B209 (Aluminum UNS A96061) and ASTM B171 (Copper UNS C70600) serve marine and heat-transfer applications.

Mechanical properties such as tensile strength between 485 MPa and 620 MPa and elongation of 30–40% ensure toughness without compromising formability. Dimensional accuracy post-shearing achieves tolerances as tight as ±0.1 mm, ideal for sheet thicknesses up to 20 mm. The process ensures no heat-affected zones, preserving metallurgical uniformity a critical factor in sectors like energy and pharmaceuticals where contamination and brittleness are unacceptable.

Applications Across Industrial Markets

  • Chemical Plants: Stainless and duplex sheared sheets for constructing corrosion-resistant reactors, flanges, and tanks.
  • Oil & Gas: Precision shearing for high-strength sheets used in subsea components, refineries, and structural platforms.
  • Marine Industry: Sheared stainless steel and copper-nickel alloys for shipbuilding, bulkheads, hull reinforcements, and seawater pipelines.
  • Pharmaceutical Fabrication: Clean-edged, burr-free sheared 316L sheets for sterile environments and food-grade processing assemblies.
  • Power Generation: Nickel-based alloy sheets per ASTM B168 (UNS N06600) used in boilers, turbines, and high-temperature ducts.
