Specs, Grades & Industry Standards

Aurico Alloys LLP Tooling delivers solutions using tool steels (ASTM A681, A36, D2, H13), high-speed steel (HSS, ASTM A600), hot/cold work alloys, and Inconel/Monel for the most aggressive conditions. All supplied tools, dies, and fixtures are compliant with global standards including the Unified Numbering System (like UNS K11547 for T2 tool steel) and robust test certificates. This supports usage from oilfield punching tools and marine valve seats to pharma stamping and power plant dies.

Typical tool steels feature high hardness (HRC >60), toughness, and wear resistance. HSS grades offer excellent balance for machining hard materials and are favored in high-cycle production. Strict adherence to ASTM and UNS standards ensures interchangeability and project compliance, reducing operational risk.
