Laser Cutting: Specifications & Industry Standards

Modern laser cutting machines operate with precision tolerances of ±0.05 mm and can handle metal plates up to 25 mm thick depending on the alloy type. The technology supports diverse grades including:

  • Stainless Steel: ASTM A240 / UNS S31603 for chemical and marine corrosion resistance.
  • Carbon & Alloy Steels: ASTM A516 Grade 70 for oil storage and heat exchanger components.
  • Aluminum & Brass: ASTM B209 / UNS A96061 for low-weight, high-strength applications.
  • Titanium: ASTM B265 / UNS R50400 for high-performance marine and pharma applications.

These global standards ensure traceability and mechanical consistency tensile strengths above 485 MPa, elongation of 20–30%, and thermal fatigue resistance, making the materials suitable for pressure vessels, turbines, and offshore structures.
