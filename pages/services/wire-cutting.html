Wire EDM Cutting Services

Precision Starts Here: Industrial Wire Cutting for Critical Applications

In the control room of a power station, an engineer checks the fit of a custom machined EDM turbine blade every micron matters. In a marine valve plant, a complex superalloy part emerges burr-free from a wire cutting machine, ready for a corrosive offshore deployment. Stories like these happen daily, driven by the evolution of modern EDM (Electro-Discharge Machining) and precision wire cutting. When stakes are high, certified accuracy and repeatable perfection become an operational necessity for chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma, and power generation sectors.

Wire Cutting Market Growth & Industry Insights

The global wire-cutting EDM market is set to grow from USD 2.68 billion in 2025 to USD 4.57 billion by 2035 at 5.5% CAGR. Automotive, aerospace/defense, medical device, and electronics manufacturing are the largest users, with India showing over 6.9% annual growth as digital automation and high-precision demand rise. In critical sectors, wire cutting unlocks higher yields, tight tolerances (<±2 μm), and surface finishes that eliminate rework in even the most complex parts.​

Specification, Product Grades & Standards

Aurico Alloys LLP Wire Cutting processes compliant grades like stainless (ASTM A240, UNS S31603/S30403), nickel alloys (ASTM B265, UNS N06625), high-temp Inconel, and titanium all per ASTM F2063, F136, and Unified Numbering System requirements. Mechanical and metallurgical integrity, high tensile, corrosion resistant, and heat-stable are retained post-cut with virtually no HAZ (heat-affected zone).​
