Elevate Protection with Precision PVC Dip Coating

Imagine equipment exposed daily to corrosive chemicals, seawater, or harsh cleaning agents components that fail prematurely mean costly downtime and safety hazards. Precision PVC dip coating offers a resilient protective barrier that preserves material integrity without compromising performance. Leading industries like chemical processing, oil & gas, marine, pharmaceuticals, and power generation rely on this versatile coating to extend equipment life and maintain compliance with strict environmental and safety standards.

Industry Standards & Specifications

Aurico Alloys LLP applies PVC dip coatings compliant with ASTM D149 and meets UNS certifications for substrate materials like stainless steels UNS S31603 and nickel alloys UNS N06625. Our coated components are traceable per ASTM A276, guaranteeing chemical composition integrity and mechanical properties before and after coating. This ensures the coating’s durability in chemical, marine, and power generation environments.​

Compatible Materials & Chemical Properties

PVC dip coating is applied effectively on alloys such as duplex stainless steels (UNS S31803, S32205), Inconel 625 (UNS N06625), titanium alloys, and other corrosion-resistant substrates used in critical sectors. This coating provides excellent chemical resistance, mechanical toughness, and thermal stability while retaining substrate tensile strength exceeding 70 ksi and preventing corrosive deterioration.

Applications Across Key Industries

  • Chemical: Protective coatings for piping, tanks, valves to combat aggressive media.
  • Oil & Gas: Coatings for offshore platforms, pipelines, valves, and equipment exposed to harsh elements.
  • Marine: Seawater-resistant protective barriers on ferrous and nonferrous components.
  • Pharmaceutical: Sterilizable, contamination-resistant coatings for cleanroom tools and machinery.
  • Power Generation: Coatings to prolong turbine, condenser, and cooling system component life.
