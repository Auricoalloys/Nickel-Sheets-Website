Precision Metal Bending: Shaping Industry, Shaping Success

In the demanding environment of a refinery or marine yard, the flawless bend of a stainless steel pipe can mean the difference between a perfect installation and costly delays. Bending is an art that integrates precision and material science, especially for industries like chemical processing, oil & gas, marine, pharmaceutical, and power generation — where fabricated components face extreme stress and hazardous conditions. This is why choosing expert bending services is critical for engineering resilience and operational excellence.

Market Insights and Industry Standards

The industrial metal bending market is witnessing steady growth driven by rising demand in critical sectors. Compliance with ASTM material standards (e.g., ASTM A269, A312 for stainless pipes) and UNS alloy designations such as UNS S31603, S32205 for duplex stainless steel ensures material predictability and reliability in fabrication processes. The combination of superior material quality and advanced bending techniques allows fabrication tolerance as fine as ±0.5% of the bend angle and radius.​

Product Grades & Mechanical Properties

Aurico Alloys LLP specializes in bending diverse grades including austenitic stainless steels UNS S31603 (Grade 316L), duplex steels UNS S32205, nickel alloys UNS N06625 (Inconel 625), and titanium ASTM B265 Grade 2. These materials are chosen for their corrosion resistance, strength (yield strength >30 ksi), and elongation (~40%) properties, essential for enduring harsh chemical, marine, and power generation environments without cracking or performance loss.

Applications Across Industries

  • Chemical Industry: Bended seamless pipes, reactors, and corrosion-resistant ductwork.
  • Oil & Gas: Curved pipelines, manifold piping, and pressure vessel components.
  • Marine: Fabrication of ship hull supports, ballast system tubing, and exhaust conduits.
  • Pharmaceutical: Sanitary piping systems requiring pinch-free bends and smooth surface finishes.
  • Power Generation: Pipe bends in heat exchangers, cooling systems, and turbine assemblies.
