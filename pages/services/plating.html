Enhancing Durability Through Precision Metal Plating

Imagine an offshore oil platform constantly battered by saline waves or a pharmaceutical plant’s sensitive equipment exposed to harsh cleaning agents both relying on precise metal plating to ensure longevity and performance. Plating technology is more than a surface treatment; it’s a safeguard against corrosion, wear, and operational failure. Advanced plating services now enable critical industries to extend equipment life, reduce maintenance, and meet stringent regulatory frameworks.

Market Overview & Standards

The market for industrial metal plating in 2025 is rapidly expanding, buoyed by demand in sectors like chemical processing, oil & gas, marine manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, and power generation. Key standards like UNS N10276 for Alloy 276 plating and ASTM B488 for copper and nickel plating ensure tailored protection and performance in harsh conditions. Globally, these standards form the backbone of supplier certification and product traceability.

Typical Product Grades & Chemical Properties

Aurico Alloys LLP offers plating on corrosion-resistant materials including duplex stainless steels (UNS S31803, S32205), Inconel (UNS N06625), and nickel alloys. These coatings help withstand chloride-induced stress corrosion cracking and abrasion common to oil rigs, chemical vessels, and turbine components. The chemical and mechanical integrity of base materials (conforming to ASTM A182, A789) combined with overlay plating assures durability and compliance.​

Applications Across Industries

  • Chemical Industry: Coatings for valves, pumps, and reactor internals against aggressive media.
  • Oil & Gas: Corrosion resistant overlays for pipelines, compressor parts, and offshore drilling equipment.
