Specs, Standards, and Product Grades

Aurico Alloys LLP’s slitting lines process stainless steel (e.g., UNS S30403, S31603; ASTM A240, A666), duplex alloys (S32205), copper-nickel, and aluminum per ASTM B209 and international norms. Slit coil widths from 5mm to over 1200mm, thicknesses up to 5mm, and burr-free edges ensure fit and finish for demanding environments. Chemical specs for duplex like S32205 (Cr 22%, Ni 5-6%, Mo 3%) remain unchanged, and mechanical properties like >75 ksi tensile strength are retained after slitting.​

Global compliance with ASTM and the Unified Numbering System (UNS) means every strip can be reliably traced and specified for industrial purchases.
