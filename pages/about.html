About Us

About Aurico Alloys LLP

Founded in 2023, Aurico Alloys LLP has grown to become a trusted name in the alloy industry, serving clients across multiple continents. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer service sets us apart in the competitive metals market.

With deep stock across more than 20 alloy families, cutting, slitting and forming in-house, and a team of experienced metallurgists, we deliver alloy solutions that meet the most demanding specifications.

Our Vision, Mission, Goal & Values

At Aurico Alloys LLP, we are driven by purpose, powered by integrity, and guided by innovation.

Vision

To become the global leader in supplying high-performance alloys that empower industries with strength, reliability, and sustainability.

Mission

To deliver exceptional quality alloys through innovation, metallurgical expertise, and customer-focused solutions.

Goal

To expand our global presence while maintaining ethical standards, on-time delivery, and uncompromised product quality.

Values

We are guided by integrity, sustainability, and excellence, building long-term partnerships with industries worldwide.

Our Journey

Starting from humble beginnings in Mumbai, India, in 2023, Aurico Alloys LLP has quickly grown into a key player in the alloys industry. We are dedicated to delivering exceptional quality and innovative solutions, and our goal is to become the preferred global supplier for mission-critical applications. While we are a relatively new company, we are focused on expanding our reach and building strong relationships with clients in various countries. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and client satisfaction is the foundation of our growing reputation.

What We Do?

We deliver high-performance alloys and provide end-to-end solutions for industries worldwide — from manufacturing excellence to global logistics.

Manufacturing Excellence

Our state-of-the-art facilities are equipped with advanced technology and testing laboratories to ensure every product meets global standards such as ASTM, DIN, JIS, and ISO.

  • Modern production lines for strips, plates, wires, pipes, and fittings
  • On-site chemical analysis and mechanical testing
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality assurance
  • Custom packaging and export compliance services

Technical Expertise

Backed by decades of metallurgical knowledge, our engineers and specialists assist in material selection, product development, and custom solutions tailored to client needs.

Global Presence

With an extensive global logistics network, we ensure fast and reliable delivery to clients across Europe, North America, South America, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East. Wherever your project is, Aurico Alloys LLP is your trusted partner.

Certifications & Quality Assurance

We follow rigorous quality control processes, backed by international certifications and third-party inspections, ensuring material traceability, compliance, and performance under demanding conditions.
