Haynes® 556

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Haynes® 556 (UNS R30556, W.Nr. 1.4883) as sheet, plate and round bar from Mumbai, India, with EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates. It is an iron-nickel-chromium-cobalt alloy for high-temperature service in sulphidising, carburising and chlorine-bearing atmospheres, where a conventional stainless or nickel alloy would be attacked.

Key Properties

  • Iron base, not nickel. Chromium 22%, nickel 20% and cobalt 18% nominal on an iron balance — the 22-20-18 in its EN designation, X10CrNiCoMoN22-20-18.
  • Molybdenum 3% and tungsten 2.5% nominal, with tantalum 0.6% and nitrogen 0.2% nominal.
  • Corrosion and heat resistance in one grade. It resists sulphidising, carburising and chlorine-bearing atmospheres while keeping its strength and oxidation resistance at temperature, ground that would otherwise need two different grades.
  • The other iron-based Haynes grade is HR-120, which carries more nickel, 37% nominal, and caps cobalt at 3%.

Applications

  • Waste incineration grates and components
  • Chemical process equipment and fixtures in sulphidising, carburising or chlorine-bearing atmospheres
  • Furnace and process fixtures that need corrosion and oxidation resistance from a single grade
