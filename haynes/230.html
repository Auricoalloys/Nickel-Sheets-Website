Haynes® 230

Haynes® 230 is a nickel-chromium-tungsten-molybdenum alloy with creep strength beyond Hastelloy® X to 1150 °C, combined with oxidation resistance and long-term thermal stability. It is used for combustion cans, ducting and retorts.

Key Properties

  • Tungsten 13-15% with molybdenum 1-3% on a nickel base, chromium 20-24%.
  • Lanthanum 0.005-0.05% for oxide scale adherence, as on Haynes 188.
  • Carbon 0.05-0.15% and aluminium 0.20-0.50%.
  • ASTM B435 for plate, sheet and strip; B572 for bar; B619 / B622 for pipe and tube.

Applications

  • Combustion cans and transition ducts
  • Furnace retorts and radiant tubes
  • Chemical process reformers
  • High-temperature ducting and bellows

Available Forms

  • Haynes® 230 Plates
  • Haynes® 230 Round Bars
  • Haynes® 230 Sheets
