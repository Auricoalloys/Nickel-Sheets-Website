Haynes® 282

Haynes® 282 is a gamma-prime strengthened superalloy developed for creep strength to 1700 °F with better fabricability and weldability than the older age-hardenable grades. It is specified for A-USC boiler components, combustors and supercritical CO2 heat exchangers.
