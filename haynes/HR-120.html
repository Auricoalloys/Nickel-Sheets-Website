Haynes® HR-120

Haynes® HR-120 is an iron-nickel-chromium alloy offering carburising and sulfidising resistance to 2000 °F, which makes it a lower-cost answer than the fully nickel-based grades for incinerator internals and radiant tubes.

Available Forms

  • Haynes® HR-120 Plates
  • Haynes® HR-120 Round Bars
  • Haynes® HR-120 Sheets

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Haynes® HR-120 in plates, round bars and sheets, with material test certificates (EN 10204 3.1) and export-ready packaging from Mumbai, India. Contact us for stock availability and pricing.
