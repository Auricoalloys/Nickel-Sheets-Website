Haynes® HR-120

Haynes® HR-120 is an iron-nickel-chromium alloy offering carburising and sulfidising resistance to 2000 °F, which makes it a lower-cost answer than the fully nickel-based grades for incinerator internals and radiant tubes.

Key Properties

  • Iron base, not nickel. Nickel 37% nominal and chromium 25% nominal on an iron balance, which is what makes it the economical answer in this family.
  • Carburising and sulphidising resistance at temperature, the services that attack many nickel alloys.
  • Nitrogen 0.2% and niobium 0.7% nominal for strength.
  • No ASTM standard is recorded for it in the specification table below.

Applications

  • Incinerator internals and grates
  • Radiant tubes and furnace rolls
  • Calciner and kiln components
  • Ore processing hardware

Available Forms

  • Haynes® HR-120 Plates
