Haynes® 188

Haynes® 188 is a cobalt-nickel-chromium-tungsten alloy that holds its oxidation and sulfidation resistance beyond Inconel® 617, which is why it is specified for flame holders, nozzle vanes and combustion hardware in gas turbines.

  • Haynes® 188 Plates
  • Haynes® 188 Round Bars
