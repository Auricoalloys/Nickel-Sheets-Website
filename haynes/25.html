Haynes® 25

Haynes® 25, also known as L-605, is a cobalt-chromium-tungsten-nickel alloy with sulfidation and galling resistance that suits turbine combustion liners, blades and downhole tooling. The same composition is supplied to implant specifications under ISO 5832-5 and ASTM F90.

Available Forms

  • Haynes® 25 Plates
  • Haynes® 25 Round Bars
  • Haynes® 25 Sheets
