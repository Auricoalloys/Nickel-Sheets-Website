17-4 PH Stainless Steel Powder Supplier and Exporter

17-4 PH Stainless Steel powder (S17400) is a argon or nitrogen gas atomised precipitation-hardening stainless steel feedstock for additive manufacturing, supplied to ASTM F3301 (PBF post-processing) in 5-25 µm, 15-53 µm, 45-105 µm, 45-150 µm, 53-150 µm size fractions.

Reaches over 1300 MPa after a simple H900 age, which makes it the default when a printed stainless part has to carry real load.

What Properties Does Printed 17-4 PH Reach?

Printed properties depend on the heat treatment, not just the alloy, so they are given per condition.

Typical mechanical properties of 17-4 PH by condition
ConditionTensile strength Yield strengthElongationHardness
As built900-1100 MPa600-800 MPa10-20%30-35 HRC
H900, aged at 480 C1300-1400 MPa1150-1250 MPa10-15%40-44 HRC
H1150, aged at 620 C950-1050 MPa800-900 MPa16-20%28-33 HRC

Printed 17-4 PH normally needs solution treatment before ageing, because the as-built structure retains austenite that a straight age will not convert.

Should I use 17-4 PH or 316L powder?

Use 17-4 PH where a printed stainless part has to carry real load, since aged to H900 it reaches 1300-1400 MPa. Use 316L where corrosion resistance, ductility and ease of printing matter more than strength, as in chemical, food and marine parts.
