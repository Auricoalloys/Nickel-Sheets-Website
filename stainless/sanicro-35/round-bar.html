Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Sanicro 35 round bar (UNS N08935) cut to length from Mumbai and exported worldwide.

Bar is an Alleima product line — the company owns the SANICRO trademark and lists Sanicro 35 Bar among its solid round bar range. Note what that does and does not mean for the paperwork: Alleima publishes ASTM numbers for the grade in tube, pipe, plate and sheet, and none for bar. Bar is therefore supplied to the mill's own specification, and the certificate states the composition and mechanical properties for the heat rather than conformance to an ASTM document that does not cover the form.

Anyone quoting you Sanicro 35 bar "to ASTM B163" is citing a tube standard. Ask which document the certificate will actually reference.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Alleima characterises Sanicro 35 as having very high mechanical strength, and publishes minima against its tube and pipe standards — 425 MPa proof strength and 750 MPa tensile for tube to ASTM B163 in the solution annealed condition. Those figures are for tube, not for bar, and are given here for the material's general character rather than as an acceptance criterion for a bar order. Bar properties are stated on the mill certificate for the heat supplied.

Good weldability with nickel-alloy consumables; TIG welding at low heat input, with preheat and post-weld heat treatment normally unnecessary.

Industry Applications

Why this grade

Bar in this alloy goes where a fastener, shaft or valve internal has to survive the same environment as the vessel around it. The combination that earns it the job is high strength with chloride stress-corrosion resistance: 6-moly stainless bar has the pitting resistance but not the nickel content, and a nickel alloy costs more than most of these components justify.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on the specification we can add positive material identification, ultrasonic testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM G28 or A262, hardness survey for sour service, and third-party inspection release from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.
