316L Stainless Steel Powder

The workhorse of metal printing. Forgiving to process, weldable, and cheap enough to use for prototyping before committing to a costlier alloy.

Applications: Medical and surgical instruments, food and pharmaceutical process parts, marine fittings, and heat exchangers.

17-4 PH Stainless Steel Powder

Reaches over 1300 MPa after a simple H900 age, which makes it the default when a printed stainless part has to carry real load.

Applications: Tooling, aerospace brackets, valve and pump components, and moulds.

17-4 PH powder details

Should I use 316L or 17-4 PH powder?

Use 316L when corrosion resistance and ductility matter and the part is not highly loaded; it is also the easier of the two to print. Use 17-4 PH when the part needs strength, since a simple H900 age takes it past 1300 MPa. 316L corrodes better, 17-4 PH is far stronger.
