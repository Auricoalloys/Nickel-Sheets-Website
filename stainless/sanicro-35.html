Sanicro® 35 (UNS N08935)

Sanicro® 35 is a nitrogen-alloyed austenitic grade that sits between the super-austenitic stainless steels and the nickel alloys: roughly 27% chromium, 35% nickel and 6.5% molybdenum with 0.3% nitrogen. That chemistry gives it the pitting and crevice resistance of a 6-moly grade with the chloride stress-corrosion resistance the nickel content buys, which is why it is specified for seawater cooling and heating, refinery overhead and sour service where 6-moly stainless is marginal and a nickel alloy is more than the duty needs.

It is a young grade rather than a long-established one, so its identity rests on a single UNS number: no Werkstoff number or EN designation has been assigned. It is covered by ASME Code Cases for Section I and Section VIII, and complies with NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156-3 as a type 4a and 4c material and with ANSI/NACE MR0103 / ISO 17495-1.
