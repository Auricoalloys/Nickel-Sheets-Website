Key Properties

  • Nickel 99.0% minimum, with copper capped at 0.25%, iron at 0.40%, manganese and silicon at 0.35% each and sulphur at 0.01%, in both grades.
  • Carbon is the one difference: 0.15% maximum in Nickel 200 and 0.02% in Nickel 201. Every other limit Special Metals publishes for the two is the same, so an order should name the grade its specification calls for.
  • Magnetic at room temperature. The Curie temperature in the table below is far above it.
