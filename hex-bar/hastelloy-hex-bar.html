Specifications and Standards

Hastelloy hex bars, especially C276, meet ASTM B574/ASME SB574, UNS N10276, DIN/WNr 2.4819, EN NiMo16Cr15W. C22 is UNS N06022/DIN 2.4602, B2 UNS N10665/DIN 2.4617. Sizes span 6mm to 100mm across flats, lengths to 6m, in hot-rolled, annealed, or machined finishes.

ISO 9001-certified with MTRs, ultrasonic testing, and PMI ensure export compliance for global procurement.

Equivalent Grades

Key equivalents: Hastelloy C276 (UNS N10276, DIN 2.4819, EN NiMo16Cr15W), C22 (UNS N06022, 2.4602, NiCr21Mo14W), B2 (UNS N10665, 2.4617). Others include JIS NW 0276, BS NA 45.

Product Forms and Availability

Hex bars available from 1/4" to 4" A/F, weights 0.018 lb/ft up, in straight lengths or custom cuts. Complements include rounds, squares, flats for fabrication.

For marine needs, Monel hex bars provide a cost-effective alternative in seawater.

Chemical Composition

Hastelloy C276: Ni balance (57%), Cr 14.5-16.5%, Mo 15-17%, Fe 4-7%, W 3-4.5%, C 0.01% max, Mn 1% max. High Mo/W boost resistance to reducing acids.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

C276 properties: tensile 690-790 MPa, yield 283-355 MPa, elongation 40% min, hardness ~100 HB, density 8.89 g/cm³, melting 1370°C. Excellent pitting/crevice resistance up to 1040°C.

C22 offers similar strength with enhanced weldability; B2 excels in HCl resistance.

Applications by Industry

Chemical Processing

Hastelloy hex bars build reactors, pumps for H2SO4, HCl, outperforming SS/Inconel in wet chlorides. Used in 70% of flue-gas desulfurization systems.

Oil & Gas

Sour gas components like fasteners resist H2S/acids, extending life 4x over carbon steel.

Marine

Scrubbers, desalination resist chlorides/seawater better than Cu-Ni alloys.

Pharma

Sterile vessels for aggressive cleaners, FDA-compliant purity.

Power Generation

Heat exchangers in FGD plants handle SOx emissions.

OEM, Fabricators, Supply Stores

Machinable for custom parts, weldable without post-weld heat treat.
