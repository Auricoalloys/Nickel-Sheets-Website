Special stainless steel hex bars conform to rigorous standards including ASTM A276/A479 for hot-rolled and cold-finished bars, UNS designations for unified numbering, DIN/EN for European norms, and NACE MR0175 for sour service in oil & gas. Sizes range from 12mm to 65mm across flats (0.47" to 2.55"), with lengths 2-6m, tolerances h10/h11 per EN 10060, and finishes like cold-drawn or polished. h2>Product Forms and Availability

Primarily offered as hot-rolled, cold-drawn, or forged hex bars, special stainless steel hex bars come in standard metric/imperial sizes with chamfered ends (30-60°). Availability spans 303 to duplex grades, with custom cut-to-length (±10mm) and surface finishes for OEM fabricators. Power generation buyers favor 321/347 hex bars for turbine shafts, readily stocked by Mumbai exporters.[web:ambica]

Chemical Composition

Key to performance, 316 hex bars feature ~16-18% Cr, 10-14% Ni, 2-3% Mo (UNS S31600), boosting pitting resistance. Duplex 2205 (UNS S31803) adds 22% Cr, 5% Ni, 3% Mo, 0.18% N for balanced microstructure. 904L (1.4539) includes 20% Cr, 25% Ni, 4.5% Mo, 1.5% Cu for sulfuric acid resistance in chemical plants.
