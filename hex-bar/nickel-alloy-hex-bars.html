Specifications and Standards

Nickel alloy hex bars adhere to rigorous international standards including ASTM B446 for Inconel 625 (UNS N06625), ASTM B164 for Monel 400 (UNS N04400), and ASTM B574 for Hastelloy C276 (UNS N10276).[page:0] DIN specifications such as 2.4856 (EN 2.4856 for Alloy 625) and 2.4360 for Monel 400 ensure compliance in European markets, while EN norms support seamless integration in global supply chains. These standards guarantee tensile testing, chemical analysis, and hardness verification, vital for procurement heads in high-stakes industries.

Chemical Composition

For Inconel 625 (UNS N06625), typical composition includes Ni 58% min, Cr 20-23%, Mo 8-10%, Nb+Ta 3.15-4.15%, Fe 5% max.[page:0] Monel 400 (UNS N04400) features Ni 63% min, Cu 28-34%, Fe 2.5% max, while Hastelloy C276 (UNS N10276) has Ni 57% min, Mo 15-17%, Cr 14.5-16.5%. These optimized alloys meet ASTM, DIN, and EN requirements for corrosion resistance in aggressive media.

Applications by Industry

Chemical Processing

Nickel alloy hex bars excel in handling sulfuric, hydrochloric, and phosphoric acids, where stainless steels pit rapidly; chosen for pumps, valves, and agitator shafts.

Oil & Gas

In downhole tools and sour gas environments, alloys like Monel 400 resist H2S and CO2, extending service life amid high pressures.

Marine

Superior seawater corrosion resistance makes Hastelloy C276 ideal for propeller shafts and fittings, preventing crevice attack common in 316L.

Pharma

Hex bars form sterile equipment components, leveraging low contamination risk and compliance with FDA-grade purity.

Power Generation

Used in turbine fasteners and heat exchangers, enduring steam and flue gas desulfurization.
