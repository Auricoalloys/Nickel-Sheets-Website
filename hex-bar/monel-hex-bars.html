Specifications and Standards

Monel hex bars conform to key international standards including ASTM B164/ ASME SB164 for nickel-copper alloys, UNS N04400 for Monel 400, DIN/EN 2.4360 (Werkstoff Nr.), and EN NiCu30Fe. These ensure compliance for high-stress components in corrosive settings. Sizes typically range from 6mm to 100mm across flats, with lengths up to 6000mm, available in bright, black, or polished finishes[web:3][web:6][web:8]

Certifications like ISO 9001 and material test reports (MTRs) verify traceability, with non-destructive testing (NDT) such as ultrasonic and dye penetrant ensuring defect-free bars for export to global markets

Equivalent Grades

Monel 400 equivalents include UNS N04400, DIN/WNr 2.4360, EN NiCu30Fe, BS NA 13, JIS NW 4400, and AFNOR NU-30M. For age-hardened variants like Monel K500, equivalents are UNS N05500, DIN 2.4375

Product Forms and Availability

Monel hex bars are stocked in diameters from 6mm to 120mm, with common sizes like 10mm, 19mm, and up to 100mm across flats (A/F). Other forms include rounds, squares, flats, and custom profiles, supplied in straight lengths of 1m to 6m or cut-to-length[web:6]

For complementary needs, Inconel hex bars offer high-temperature alternatives in similar sizes.

Chemical Composition

Monel 400's composition features Nickel (Ni) 63% min, Copper (Cu) 28-34%, Iron (Fe) 2.5% max, Manganese (Mn) 2.0% max, Carbon (C) 0.3% max, Silicon (Si) 0.5% max, Sulfur (S) 0.024% max. This balance delivers exceptional corrosion resistance

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Mechanical properties include tensile strength 480-620 MPa, yield strength 172-345 MPa, elongation 20-40%, and hardness Brinell 110-150. Physical traits: density 8.80 g/cm³, melting range 1300-1350°C, thermal conductivity 21.8 W/m·K

Monel K500 adds age-hardening for higher strength: tensile up to 690-827 MPa via Ti/Al precipitation
