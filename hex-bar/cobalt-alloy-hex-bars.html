Specifications and Standards

Cobalt Alloy Hex Bars adhere to AMS 5759 for bars, AMS 5894 for Stellite variants, ASTM F90, DIN 2.4964, and EN ISO 5832-5 (CoCr20W15Ni), guaranteeing high-temperature strength up to 980°C.

These specs include tolerances for hex sizes 18-57mm, with mandatory tests like tensile, hardness, and metallographic analysis for industrial compliance.

ISO 9001 certifications and mill test reports (MTRs) affirm export readiness from Mumbai to stringent markets.

Equivalent Grades

Key equivalents: UNS R30605 (L605), Haynes 25, Udimet L605, DIN 2.4964, EN CoCr20W15Ni, offering consistent performance across aerospace and medical standards.

Stellite 6B (UNS R30016, AMS 5894) provides similar wear properties for abrasion-focused uses.

Product Forms and Availability

Available as hex bars 18-57mm (11/16″ to 2-3/4″) A/F, lengths up to 6m, in black, bright polished, or rough-turned finishes.

Square and flat variants support fabricators, with custom machining for OEM power and marine components.
