Specifications and Standards

Inconel hex bars comply with ASTM B446/ASME SB446 (625), B166 (600), UNS N06625/N06600/N07718, DIN 2.4856 (625)/2.4648 (718)/2.4851 (600), EN NiCr22Mo9Nb (625). Available in annealed or age-hardened conditions, sizes 6-150mm A/F, lengths to 7m.

Full traceability via MTRs, UT/PMI testing, ISO 9001 certification support export to stringent markets.

Product Forms and Availability

Hex bars from 4mm to 100mm A/F, in hot-forged, cold-drawn finishes. Rounds, flats, squares also stocked for OEM needs.

Hastelloy hex bars serve as a strong complement for severe acid environments.

Chemical Composition

Inconel 625: Ni 58% min, Cr 20-23%, Mo 8-10%, Nb+Ta 3.15-4.15%, Fe 5% max, C 0.10% max. Enhances strength via Nb-Mo interaction.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

625: Tensile 820 MPa (annealed), yield 414 MPa, elongation 30-36%, density 8.44 g/cm³, modulus 207 GPa, thermal expansion 12.8 µm/m°C. Retains properties to 980°C.

718 age-hardens to 1275 MPa tensile; 600 offers 550-690 MPa for oxidation.

Applications by Industry

Marine

Exhaust systems, props resist seawater corrosion at elevated temps.

Pharma

Power Generation

OEM, Fabricators, Stores
