In 2010, a major oil & gas platform in the North Sea faced a catastrophic failure when standard austenitic stainless steel bolts corroded under chloride stress, leading to a multi-million-dollar shutdown and safety risks.

This incident highlighted the limitations of conventional materials in harsh marine environments, prompting engineers to turn to Duplex Steel Hex Bars—offering superior strength and corrosion resistance that prevented similar failures worldwide.

Specifications and Standards

Duplex Steel Hex Bars conform to key standards like ASTM A182 for forgings, ASTM A276 for hot-finished bars, DIN 1.4462, and EN 1.4462 (X2CrNiMoN22-5-3), ensuring compliance for high-pressure applications.

These standards specify tolerances such as -0/+0.16mm for 40-45mm diameters, with testing including positive material identification (PMI), ultrasonic testing (UT), and radiography for quality assurance.

Certifications like ISO 9001 and material test reports (MTRs) confirm compliance, vital for export to chemical and power sectors.

Equivalent Grades

Common equivalents include UNS S31803/S32205, EN 1.4462, DIN 1.4462, and AFNOR F44, providing interchangeable options across global specifications.

S32205 offers slightly higher nitrogen for enhanced pitting resistance (PREN 28-38), while S31803 remains widely used for standard duplex needs.

Product Forms and Availability

Hex bars are available in sizes 2-100mm across flats (A/F), lengths 3-7m, with finishes like hot-rolled, peeled, or rough-turned.

Custom cuts support OEM fabricators, with stock readiness for urgent procurement in Mumbai for export worldwide.
