Chemical Composition

Incoloy alloys feature nickel-iron-chromium bases for oxidation and carburization resistance. For Incoloy 800/800H/800HT: Ni 30-35%, Cr 19-23%, Fe 39.5% min, C 0.05-0.10% (higher in H/HT), Al+Ti 0.30-1.20% (restricted to 0.85-1.20% in 800HT). Incoloy 825 adds Mo 2.5-3.5% and Cu 1.5-3% for acid resistance.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Incoloy hex bars deliver tensile strength of 520-600 MPa, yield 205-350 MPa, and 30% elongation at room temperature. High-temperature properties shine: creep-rupture strength up to 1650°F (900°C), density 7.94 g/cm³, thermal conductivity 11.5-31.9 W/m°C. Modulus of elasticity ~196 GPa at 20°C, dropping to 141 GPa at 800°C.

These properties ensure durability under stress, with superior creep resistance in 800H/HT due to controlled grain size (ASTM 5 or coarser).
