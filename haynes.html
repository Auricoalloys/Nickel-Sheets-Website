Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Haynes® alloys from Mumbai in sheet, plate, round bar and foil. Held in stock or arranged with the manufacturer, depending on grade and quantity.

The Haynes grades are high-temperature alloys, chosen where a part has to hold its shape and its strength in a furnace, a combustor or an exhaust path. Two of them, 25 and 188, are cobalt based, which buys resistance to sulphidation and to the sliding wear that nickel alloys tolerate poorly. The rest are nickel based.

Price INR 5,000 – 15,000 per kg. Indicative USD 60 – 175 per kg; quoted against grade, form, size and quantity.

The dividing line inside the nickel grades is again solid solution against age hardening. 230 and 214 are solid solution and are picked for oxidation resistance and stability over long exposures. 242 and 282 are age-hardened and are picked when the part must carry real load while hot.

Haynes

Key Properties

  • Strength and metallurgical stability at temperature over long exposures
  • Oxidation resistance from an alumina scale in 214, and a chromia scale in the rest
  • Sulphidation and sliding-wear resistance in the cobalt grades, 25 and 188
  • Solid-solution grades 230 and 214 for oxidation and stability; age-hardened 242 and 282 for load while hot
  • Supplied as sheet, plate, round bar and foil

Where These Alloys Are Used

  • Gas turbines — combustion liners, transition ducts, seal rings and exhaust components
  • Aerospace — afterburner parts and hot structural sections
  • Industrial heating — furnace fixtures, muffles, retorts and radiant tubes
  • Chemical processing — high-temperature reactor internals
  • Power generation — high-temperature ducting and boiler components
