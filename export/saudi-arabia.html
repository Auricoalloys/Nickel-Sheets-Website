Standards and Conformity

Imports fall under the SABER platform operated by SASO, which requires a Product Certificate of Conformity and a Shipment Certificate of Conformity before customs clearance. Material for Saudi Aramco projects is additionally governed by Aramco engineering standards and its approved manufacturer arrangements.
