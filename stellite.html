Stellite

Stellite Cobalt Alloys for Wear and High-Temperature Service

Stellite is a family of cobalt-chromium alloys, strengthened by hard chromium and tungsten carbides, that are valued for their resistance to wear, galling, cavitation and erosion. The alloys retain hardness and strength at elevated temperature and resist oxidation and corrosion, which makes them a standard choice for valve seats and trim, pump components, wear pads and hardfacing in demanding service.

Key Properties:

  • Outstanding Wear, Galling and Abrasion Resistance
  • Retention of Hardness at High Temperature
  • Corrosion and Oxidation Resistance
  • Cavitation and Erosion Resistance
  • Good Performance in Unlubricated Contact

Applications:

  • Valve Seats, Trim and Sealing Faces
  • Pump and Compressor Components
  • Power Generation
  • Chemical Processing
  • Oil, Gas and Marine Equipment

Grades Supplied:

  • Stellite 6 (UNS R30006) — the general-purpose cobalt-chromium-tungsten wear grade for valve seats, pump parts and hardfacing
  • Stellite 12 (UNS R30012) — higher-carbide grade for cutting edges, knives and severe abrasion
  • Stellite 21 (UNS R30021) — molybdenum-bearing grade (no tungsten) for high-temperature valve trim and biomedical use

Available Forms:

  • Stellite Sheets
  • Stellite Plates
  • Stellite Round Bars
  • Stellite Foil
