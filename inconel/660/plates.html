Inconel 660 (frequently marketed as Alloy 660, A286 or NAS 660) is an iron‑based, precipitation‑hardened austenitic stainless alloy containing approximately 24–27% nickel, 13.5–16% chromium, around 1–1.5% molybdenum and titanium, vanadium and boron additions, which generate a γ′‑strengthened microstructure for high strength at elevated temperature. Producer and specification literature state that it is designed for high‑temperature use up to about 700–704°C with excellent oxidation resistance, high tensile strength and good creep and stress‑rupture properties, making it suitable for gas‑turbine, steam‑turbine, petrochemical, aerospace and high‑temperature fastening applications.
