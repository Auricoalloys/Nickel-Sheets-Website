Why Choose Inconel 660 Sheets?

Inconel 660 (UNS N06660, ASTM B163) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy engineered to withstand aggressive high-temperature oxidation, carburization, and thermal fatigue up to 1150°C. Its superior resistance against scaling and cracking makes it ideal for furnace components, heat exchangers, and reformers.

The alloy's high chromium and nickel content, along with its mechanical robustness, allow it to resist corrosion in oxidizing and reducing environments alike. This makes Inconel 660 sheets a preferred choice over conventional stainless steels or other nickel alloys for sustained industrial performance.
