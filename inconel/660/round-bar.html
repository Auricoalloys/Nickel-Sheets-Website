    Inconel 660 (UNS N06660, ASTM B166) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy with additions of cobalt and aluminum, developed for exceptional resistance to oxidation and carburization at elevated temperatures. It's widely used in turbine components, furnace parts, and chemical processing equipment subject to harsh oxidative media. Its superior high-temperature mechanical properties and corrosion resistance exceed many traditional alloys, offering long service life in severe environments.
