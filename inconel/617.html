Inconel 617

Inconel 617 plates (UNS N06617, W.Nr. 2.4663, ASTM B168 / ASME SB168) offer exceptional creep strength and oxidation resistance up to ~980–1100°C for USC boiler components, gas turbine combustion cans, petrochemical.
