Inconel 600

Inconel 600 plates (UNS N06600, W.Nr. 2.4816, ASTM B168 / ASME SB168) deliver reliable strength and corrosion resistance from cryogenic to 1093°C in chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma and power applications.
