Aurico Alloys LLP supplies inconel 740h (N07740) from stock in Mumbai, in seamless pipe, tube, plate, bar.

Inconel 740H is the ASME code-qualified alloy for 760°C steam, which is what makes advanced ultra-supercritical plant possible.

Chemical Composition

A Ni-Cr-Co alloy with roughly 25% chromium and 20% cobalt, strengthened by gamma-prime through titanium, aluminium and niobium, with carbon controlled for weldability.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

The only nickel alloy code-approved for advanced ultra-supercritical steam service, holding creep-rupture strength at 750°C and 35 MPa for 100,000 hours.
