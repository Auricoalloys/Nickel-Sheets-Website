Chemical Composition

The alloy X-750 composition with aluminium raised to roughly 1.0-1.8%, which increases the volume fraction of gamma-prime and lifts high-temperature strength.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Aged tensile strength above 1200 MPa with rupture strength held to about 870°C, and good resistance to exhaust-gas corrosion.
