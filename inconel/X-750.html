Key Properties

  • Titanium 2.25-2.75%, aluminium 0.40-1.00% and niobium with tantalum 0.70-1.20% on nickel 70% minimum, chromium 14-17% and iron 5-9%.
  • An age-hardened grade. Special Metals publishes how much it contracts in the precipitation treatment from the annealed, hot-rolled and 20% cold-rolled conditions: the figures are in the table below.
  • Essentially non-magnetic at room temperature. Its Curie temperature, also below, is well under zero both hot-rolled and triple-heat-treated, the two conditions Special Metals publishes it for.
