Chemical Composition

An oxidation-resistant low-expansion Ni-Co-Fe alloy with roughly 26-30% cobalt, 24-27% iron and 5.0-6.0% aluminium, strengthened by beta-NiAl precipitation.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Combines a low coefficient of thermal expansion with oxidation resistance that conventional controlled-expansion alloys lack, holding useful strength to about 700°C.

Testing and Certification
