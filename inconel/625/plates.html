Inconel 625 (Alloy 625, UNS N06625) is a nickel–chromium–molybdenum alloy with niobium that strengthens the matrix by solid-solution and precipitation mechanisms without the need for a separate age-hardening heat treatment. Technical documents explain that the high nickel and chromium contents give excellent resistance to a wide range of oxidising and reducing environments, the molybdenum and niobium confer high resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion and chloride-induced stress-corrosion cracking, and the alloy performs well in strong acids, seawater, sour gas, high-purity water and high-temperature oxidising and carburising environments.

FAQ & Search Trends

Alloy 625 combines a high-nickel matrix with chromium, molybdenum and niobium to provide broad-spectrum resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, impingement attack and chloride-induced stress-corrosion cracking in seawater, as well as strong resistance in sour-gas and FGD environments, and is described as almost immune to chloride SCC and highly resistant in seawater and sour service. Duplex and super-austenitic stainless steels can perform well but are more sensitive to localised corrosion and sour-service limits, whereas Alloy 625 offers a wider safe operating envelope across temperature, chloride and H₂S combinations, which is why it is widely used in subsea wellhead accessories, risers, FGD hardware and nuclear and marine systems.
