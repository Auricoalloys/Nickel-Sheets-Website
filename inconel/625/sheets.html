Unmatched Properties of Inconel 625 Sheets

Inconel 625 (UNS N06625, ASTM B443) is a nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy known for exceptional corrosion resistance, outstanding mechanical strength, and outstanding resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking. Its ability to operate reliably at temperatures up to 1093°C combined with excellent weldability makes it the alloy of choice for heat exchangers, reactors, piping, and marine hardware.

The addition of niobium and molybdenum enhances the alloy matrix, stiffening the material without requiring heat treatment. This ensures high strength without compromising ductility. Enhanced resistance to chloride-induced stress corrosion cracking significantly extends equipment service life in marine and sour gas environments.
