Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 625 pipe (N06625, Werkstoff 2.4856) to ASTM B444 from Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

A 625 pipe line is a seawater line, a sour-service line or an acid-condensate line – somewhere a carbon-steel or stainless system would need replacing within a few years. Offshore seawater and firewater systems, sour-gas process piping and flue-gas desulphurisation lines are built in it because 625 resists the pitting and crevice attack those fluids cause, and because it can be welded without losing the properties the line was designed on.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Annealed 625 pipe runs around 830–965 MPa tensile with yield above 415 MPa, so for a given pressure rating the schedule can often come down against a stainless line. It is strengthened in the annealed condition, so field welds keep their properties without post-weld heat treatment.

Industry Applications

Why Inconel 625 pipe

The argument for 625 pipe is that the corrosion allowance in seawater and chloride brines can be kept small, because the alloy is not being eaten. It needs no post-weld heat treatment, which matters on an offshore module where a line is welded in place. It is the general-purpose alloy for this duty; where the service is hot, acid and heavily contaminated with halides, 686 pipe is the step up.
