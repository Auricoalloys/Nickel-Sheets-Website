In a North Sea offshore platform, standard duplex stainless components suffered chloride stress corrosion cracking under cyclic loading, risking catastrophic failure and multimillion-dollar delays. Inconel 625 (UNS N06625, ASTM B443) and 625LCF (N06626) foils mitigate these risks with exceptional pitting resistance and fatigue endurance.

Specifications & Standards

Inconel 625 foil adheres to ASTM B443 (sheet/plate), B446 (rod), ASME SB443/SB446, DIN 2.4856/EN 10095 for seawater and sour service. 625LCF (UNS N06626) follows similar specs with enhanced low-cycle fatigue per AMS 5877.

NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 compliance verified by ASTM G48 (pitting), G36 (SCC), ensuring marine and oil & gas readiness with full MTRs.

Product Forms & Availability

Foils from 0.01 mm to 0.8 mm thick, widths to 1500 mm, solution annealed 1030-1150°C per ASTM B443. LCF variant optimized for bellows forming.

Stock in Mumbai supports custom decoiling/slitting for pharma diaphragms and OEM clamps.
