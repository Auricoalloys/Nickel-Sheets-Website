In a North Sea offshore platform, standard duplex stainless components suffered chloride stress corrosion cracking under cyclic loading, risking catastrophic failure and multimillion-dollar delays. Inconel 625 (UNS N06625, ASTM B443) and 625LCF (N06626) foils mitigate these risks with exceptional pitting resistance and fatigue endurance.

Specifications & Standards

Inconel 625 foil adheres to ASTM B443 (sheet/plate), B446 (rod), ASME SB443/SB446, DIN 2.4856/EN 10095 for seawater and sour service. 625LCF (UNS N06626) follows similar specs with enhanced low-cycle fatigue per AMS 5877.[web:51][web:53]

NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 compliance verified by ASTM G48 (pitting), G36 (SCC), ensuring marine and oil & gas readiness with full MTRs.

Product Forms & Availability

Foils from 0.01 mm to 0.8 mm thick, widths to 1500 mm, solution annealed 1030-1150°C per ASTM B443. LCF variant optimized for bellows forming.

Stock in Mumbai supports custom decoiling/slitting for pharma diaphragms and OEM clamps.

Mechanical & Physical Properties

Density 8.44 g/cm³, UTS 827 MPa min (LCF aged), yield 414 MPa, 40% EL, modulus 208 GPa. PREN ~51 for pitting resistance.

LCF excels in 10^4-10^6 cycle fatigue for bellows; thermal cond. 9.8 W/mK RT.

Frequently Asked Questions

ASTM B443 Gr1/2 sheet/plate, B446 bar, ASME SB443, DIN/EN 2.4856, AMS 5599/5666. NACE MR0175 sour, G48 PREN>40 equiv. MTRs cover PMI, UT, tensile E8 for oil/marine exports.

625LCF (N06626) uses vacuum induction + ESR melting, low C/Nb for 2x fatigue life in bellows (10^6 cycles). Aged 760°C boosts yield 414 MPa vs 625's 276 MPa annealed. Same corrosion, enhanced ductility/formability.
