Inconel 625 Powder Supplier and Exporter

Inconel 625 powder (N06625) is a vacuum induction melted, argon gas atomised nickel superalloy feedstock for additive manufacturing, supplied to ASTM F3056 (PBF) in 5-25 µm, 15-53 µm, 45-105 µm, 45-150 µm, 53-150 µm size fractions.

Strengthened as printed rather than by heat treatment, which is why it is the usual choice for corrosion-critical parts and for cladding worn or damaged components.

What Is Inconel 625 Powder Used For?

Post-processing

Stress relief and HIP; solution annealing where full corrosion performance is required.

Should I use Inconel 625 or Inconel 718 powder?

Use 625 for corrosion-critical parts, seawater and chemical service, and for cladding worn components, because it reaches its properties without ageing. Use 718 when the part is structural and can go through a solution and double-age cycle to roughly 1400 MPa.
