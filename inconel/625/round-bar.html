    Inconel 625 (UNS N06625, ASTM B444) is a nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy known for excellent resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking. Its robust performance under oxidizing and reducing environments, high mechanical strength, and thermal stability make it perfect for heat exchangers, chemical reactors, subsea components, and power generation equipment. It outperforms many stainless steels and nickel alloys in demanding service conditions.

    Aurico Alloys LLP offers Inconel 625 Round Bars in diameters ranging from 6mm to 350mm, with various finishes including bright, ground, and polished.
