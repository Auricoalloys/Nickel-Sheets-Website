Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 693 pipe (UNS N06693) to ASTM B167 from Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

A 693 pipe line carries synthesis gas – the hydrogen and carbon monoxide stream that feeds ammonia, methanol and hydrogen production. In the temperature band where that gas cools, its carbon activity attacks the pipe wall by metal dusting, and 693 was developed by Special Metals to resist it; the alloy has been used successfully in a reactor for syngas production.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Special Metals reports excellent rupture strength for 693 at elevated temperature. The schedule of a syngas line is set by pressure; what 693 adds is a chromium and aluminium scale that resists metal dusting and carburisation where the line would otherwise waste away locally.

Industry Applications

Why Inconel 693 pipe

The expensive thing about metal dusting is that it is local: a line looks sound until it pits through at the section that sat in the wrong temperature band. Replacing that section with more of the same alloy repeats the failure. 693 changes the material at the root of the problem. Where the syngas runs above or below the dusting range, a more conventional alloy is the better value.
