Why Industry Leaders Prefer Inconel 693 Sheets

Inconel 693 (UNS N06693, ASTM B574) is a premium nickel-based alloy formulated with high chromium (24–27%), molybdenum (4–6%), and tungsten (3–4%) contents, conferring outstanding resistance to oxidizing and reducing media at elevated temperatures. Its ability to withstand continuous operation up to 1100°C in aggressive atmospheres makes it the ideal choice for heat exchangers, reactor liners, and piping that must resist thermal fatigue, oxidation, and chloride-induced corrosion in the most demanding environments .

The alloy's tungsten addition significantly enhances its corrosion barrier in chloride-rich and acidic environments, outperforming many traditional alloys like Inconel 625 and Alloy 686. Its outstanding mechanical strength yield above 420 MPa and tensile above 850 MPa and its low thermal expansion rate ensure durability even in cyclic thermal operations.
