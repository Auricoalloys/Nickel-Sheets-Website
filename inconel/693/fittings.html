Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 693 fittings (UNS N06693) to ASTM B366 from Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

A section of synthesis-gas line rebuilt in 693 pipe to stop metal dusting needs its bends and branches in 693 as well. The attack is local and temperature-driven, and an elbow in a lesser alloy sitting in the same band will waste away exactly as the old line did – only now it is the one component the upgrade missed.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Fittings are supplied annealed. The property that matters is the chromium and aluminium scale that resists metal dusting and carburisation in synthesis gas, which the fitting needs as much as the pipe.

Industry Applications

Why Inconel 693 fittings

ASTM B366 names UNS N06693 in its scope, so factory-made 693 fittings are a standard product rather than a special. That matters because a metal-dusting repair only works if every component in the affected band is upgraded together: pipe, fittings and the welds between them. Order the fittings with the pipe, to the same heat traceability, so the section can be built and inspected as one.

Testing and Certification

Every fitting is marked with grade and heat number and supplied with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate. Positive material identification confirms the aluminium-bearing grade, dye-penetrant testing checks bevels and formed surfaces, and dimensions are checked against the ordered schedule. Third-party inspection is available under our ISO 9001:2015 system.
