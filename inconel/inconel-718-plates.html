INCONEL alloy 718 (UNS N07718 / 2.4668) is a precipitation-hardening nickel-chromium-iron alloy strengthened by ordered γ″-Ni₃Nb and γ′-Ni₃(Al,Ti) phases that provide exceptional tensile strength (1275 MPa minimum), yield strength (1034 MPa minimum), creep-rupture resistance to 700°C and corrosion resistance across cryogenic to high-temperature service. Technical data describe it as age-hardenable from solution-annealed condition (typically 980–1065°C) through double-aging cycles (718°C/8h furnace cool to 621°C/8h), achieving combinations of high strength, fatigue resistance and SCC immunity that make it the material of choice for rotating aircraft engine components, oilfield wellhead hardware and nuclear reactor internals.

For procurement heads, design engineers, fabricators, OEMs and hardware stores serving chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma and power-generation sectors, Inconel 718 plates solve the classic dilemma of needing aircraft-grade strength (UTS >185 ksi) with oilfield sour-service compliance (NACE MR0175 HRC ≤35 max) in a single alloy, unlike conventional stainless or lower-strength nickel alloys that sacrifice either mechanical margin or H₂S tolerance.
