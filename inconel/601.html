Inconel 601

Inconel 601 plates (UNS N06601, W.Nr. 2.4851, ASTM B168 / ASME SB168) deliver outstanding oxidation and scaling resistance up to about 1200°C for industrial furnaces, superheater supports, petrochemical heaters, flare.
