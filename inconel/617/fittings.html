Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 617 fittings (N06617, Werkstoff 2.4663) to ASTM B366 from Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

In a high-temperature line the fittings creep first. An elbow or a tee concentrates stress at its intrados and branch, and above 800°C stress plus time is what creep is. A 617 line is specified for its creep-rupture strength, so a fitting in anything weaker quietly becomes the part that sets the line’s life.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Fittings are supplied annealed. Room-temperature tensile is around 740 MPa, but what matters in service is creep-rupture strength between 800 and 1100°C, where 617 leads the solid-solution nickel alloys.

Industry Applications

Why Inconel 617 fittings

Matching the fittings to the pipe matters more in creep service than in almost any other. The pipe wall is calculated for 617’s strength at temperature; drop in a fitting in a weaker alloy at the same schedule and its wall is under-designed by definition. B366 fittings are made from 617 itself, so the stress calculation that sized the line holds at every bend and branch.

Testing and Certification

Every fitting is marked with grade and heat number and supplied with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate. Positive material identification confirms the cobalt-bearing grade fitting by fitting, dye-penetrant testing checks bevels and formed surfaces, and dimensions are checked against the ordered schedule. Third-party inspection is available under our ISO 9001:2015 system.
