Why Inconel 617 Sheets Are Industry Favorites

Inconel 617 (UNS N06617, ASTM B168, DIN 2.4663) is a nickel-chromium-cobalt-molybdenum alloy engineered for exceptional strength and oxidation resistance at elevated temperatures up to 1100°C. It resists creep, thermal fatigue, carburization, and oxidation better than most other high-temperature alloys, ensuring longer equipment life in harsh environments such as gas turbines, heat exchangers, reactors, and boilers.

The alloy’s composition with chromium (21-23%), cobalt (10-15%), and molybdenum (8-10%) coupled with its fabrication versatility, results in robust performance against a variety of corrosive media, including oxidizing and reducing agents found in oil refining and marine atmospheres. This makes it preferred where downtime costs are high and safety is paramount.
