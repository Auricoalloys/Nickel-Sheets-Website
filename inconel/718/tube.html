Aurico Alloys LLP supplies inconel 718 tube (N07718, Werkstoff 2.4668) from ready stock in Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

Tube is specified by outside diameter and wall thickness rather than nominal bore, which makes it the form of choice for heat exchangers, instrumentation and any assembly where the fit into a tubesheet or fitting is what governs.

Inconel 718 age-hardens slowly enough to be welded and then heat treated without strain-age cracking, which is why it is the most-used superalloy in the world.

Chemical Composition

Nickel 50-55% with 17-21% chromium, 4.75-5.5% niobium, 2.8-3.3% molybdenum plus titanium and aluminium. Strengthening comes from gamma-double-prime precipitation on ageing.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Solution treated and aged tensile strength reaches 1275 MPa with yield above 1030 MPa. The sluggish ageing response makes it weldable and resistant to post-weld cracking.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on the specification we can add positive material identification, ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM G28 or A262, hydrostatic testing, and third-party inspection release from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.

Aurico Alloys LLP operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system, and can certify material to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 for sour service where the grade permits.
