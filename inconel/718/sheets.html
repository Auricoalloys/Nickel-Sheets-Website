Why Inconel 718 Sheets Lead in Performance

Inconel 718 (UNS N07718, ASTM B670) is a precipitation-hardened nickel-iron-chromium alloy, notable for its outstanding tensile strength exceeding 1,270 MPa, excellent creep resistance, and superb corrosion resistance. This combination makes it invaluable for components subjected to cyclic loading, high heat, and corrosive environments, such as turbine blades, rocket motors, and chemical plant vessels.

Its capability to maintain strength at cryogenic temperatures and endure temperatures up to 700°C without loss of mechanical properties distinguishes Inconel 718 from alternative alloys. The alloy’s balanced chemistry with additions of niobium, molybdenum, and titanium also enhances weldability and fabrication versatility necessary for complex manufacturing processes.
