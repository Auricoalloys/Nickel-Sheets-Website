Aurico Alloys LLP supplies inconel 718 fittings (N07718, Werkstoff 2.4668) from ready stock in Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

Fittings carry the same corrosion duty as the pipe they join but see higher stress concentration at every change of direction, so grade traceability and wall thickness at the extrados matter more here than anywhere else in a piping system.

Inconel 718 age-hardens slowly enough to be welded and then heat treated without strain-age cracking, which is why it is the most-used superalloy in the world.

Chemical Composition

Nickel 50-55% with 17-21% chromium, 4.75-5.5% niobium, 2.8-3.3% molybdenum plus titanium and aluminium. Strengthening comes from gamma-double-prime precipitation on ageing.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Solution treated and aged tensile strength reaches 1275 MPa with yield above 1030 MPa. The sluggish ageing response makes it weldable and resistant to post-weld cracking.

Industry Applications

Why this grade

Inconel 718 is age-hardenable, strengthened by gamma double-prime precipitation on ageing rather than by solid solution alone. That gives it high strength up to around 700°C together with good weldability, which is the combination that put it into turbine discs, shafts, fasteners and downhole hardware. Above that temperature the strengthening phase coarsens and a solid-solution grade such as 617 takes over.
