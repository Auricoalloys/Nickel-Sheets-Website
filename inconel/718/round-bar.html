    Inconel 718 (UNS N07718, ASTM B637) is a precipitation-hardenable nickel-chromium alloy that provides outstanding corrosion resistance paired with exceptional tensile, fatigue, creep, and rupture strength at temperatures up to 700°C. Its unique microstructure stabilization and good weldability make it ideal for aerospace components, power plants, chemical reactors, and offshore installations where durability and safety are paramount.
