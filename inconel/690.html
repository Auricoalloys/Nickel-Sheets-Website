Inconel 690

Inconel 690 plates (UNS N06690, W.Nr. 2.4642, ASTM B168 / ASME SB168) are high-chromium nickel alloy plates with excellent stress corrosion cracking and oxidation resistance for nuclear steam generators, thermal power.
