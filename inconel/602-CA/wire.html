Aurico Alloys LLP supplies inconel 602-ca wire (N06025, Werkstoff 2.4633) from ready stock in Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

Wire is bought on diameter tolerance and temper, and for welding, spring and mesh work those two properties matter more than anything on the certificate.

Inconel 602-CA combines an alumina scale with carbide strengthening, so it keeps creep strength at temperatures where alloy 601 has already softened.

Chemical Composition

Nickel balance with 24-26% chromium, 8-11% iron, 1.8-2.4% aluminium and a deliberately high 0.15-0.25% carbon, micro-alloyed with yttrium and zirconium.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

The carbide dispersion plus the alumina scale give the highest creep-rupture strength of the alloy 600 family, with oxidation and carburisation resistance to 1200°C.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on the specification we can add positive material identification, ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM G28 or A262, and third-party inspection release from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.

Aurico Alloys LLP operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system, and can certify material to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 for sour service where the grade permits.
