Why Inconel 602 CA Sheets are Essential for Industrial Success

Inconel 602 CA (UNS N06025, ASTM B168) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy with specially enhanced aluminum and titanium contents, designed to provide superior resistance against carburization, oxidation, and thermal fatigue at temperatures up to 1200°C. Compared to other alloys, its unique composition maintains high strength and corrosion resistance in both oxidizing and carburizing atmospheres, making it ideal for heat exchanger tubes, furnace elements, reformer components, and other high-temperature applications.

The addition of aluminum offers remarkable oxidation resistance, while titanium provides structural stability by minimizing carbide formation, which ensures longer component life under aggressive operating conditions. Additionally, Inconel 602 CA exhibits excellent fabricability, weldability, and mechanical traits required for complex industrial systems.
