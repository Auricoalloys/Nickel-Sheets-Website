Why Inconel 690 Sheets Are the Industry Standard

Inconel 690 (UNS N06690, ASTM B167) is a high-nickel, chromium-molybdenum alloy renowned for its exceptional resistance against oxidation, corrosion, and high-temperature environments up to 1000°C. Its dual properties corrosion resistance especially in acidic environments and mechanical stability make it ideal for critical components like furnace linings, heat exchangers, and reactor shells in power plants and chemical industries.

The alloy's corrosion resistance is attributed to its high chromium (24–27%) and nickel (60–67%) content, which fortify its passive oxide layer against acids, chlorides, and oxidizing agents. Its ability to withstand thermal cycling without embrittlement significantly extends the service life of equipment facing sustained high temperatures and corrosive media.
