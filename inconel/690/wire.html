Chemical Composition

Nickel 58% minimum with an unusually high 27-31% chromium and 7-11% iron. The chromium level is what drives its resistance to oxidising acids and high-temperature corrosion.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Annealed tensile strength around 590-760 MPa. Very high resistance to stress-corrosion cracking in pure and caustic water, plus excellent nitric and mixed-acid resistance.

Industry Applications

Why this grade

Inconel 690 is the high-chromium modification of 600, at 27-31% chromium against 600's 14-17%, with nickel down to around 58%. It was developed specifically to resist the stress-corrosion cracking that affected 600 in nuclear steam generators, and is generally regarded as immune to it. That chromium also gives it strong resistance in highly oxidising environments.
