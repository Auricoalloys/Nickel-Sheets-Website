Inconel 693

Inconel 693 plates (UNS N06693, ASTM B168 / ASME SB168) offer superior metal dusting resistance, carburization resistance and oxidation protection up to ~1100°C for ammonia synthesis thermowells, syngas reformers.

Available Forms

  • Inconel 693 Coil
  • Inconel 693 Fittings
  • Inconel 693 Pipe
  • Inconel 693 Plates
  • Inconel 693 Round Bars
  • Inconel 693 Sheets
  • Inconel 693 Tube
  • Inconel 693 Wire

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 693 across all the forms above, with material test certificates (EN 10204 3.1) and export-ready packaging from Mumbai, India. Contact us for stock availability and pricing.
