Why Inconel 686 Sheets Stand Apart

Inconel 686 (UNS N06686, ASTM B575, DIN 2.4606) is a single-phase, austenitic Ni-Cr-Mo-W alloy offering outstanding general, pitting, and crevice corrosion resistance in a wide set of aggressive industrial environments, including hot acid chlorides and mixed halides. With exceptional mechanical strength (tensile 730–850 MPa, yield 360–420 MPa, elongation 55–70%) it performs under stress and temperature extremes that defeat ordinary alloy solutions.

Tungsten and molybdenum additions enhance Inconel 686’s ability to resist highly localized acid attack, while low carbon content maintains corrosion resistance even in weld heat-affected zones. Key certifications include compliance to ASTM B575, ASME SB575, NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for sour gas service, and EN/DIN standards.
