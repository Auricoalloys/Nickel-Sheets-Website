Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 686 fittings (N06686, Werkstoff 2.4606) to ASTM B366 from Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

Nobody specifies 686 fittings for a line that is not already in 686. They are bought because a 686 line exists for a reason – a fluid that pitted the previous alloy – and the elbows and tees are where turbulence, deposits and weld heat-affected zones concentrate that attack. A fitting one alloy down is where the old failure comes back.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Fittings are supplied solution annealed, where 686 runs around 720 MPa tensile. Carbon at 0.01% maximum keeps the heat-affected zones of the butt welds free of the carbide sensitisation that would invite intergranular attack.

Industry Applications

Why Inconel 686 fittings

The corrosion case for the line is only as good as its most exposed component, and in a piping system that is the fitting: flow changes direction, deposits settle in the intrados, and every fitting brings two more welds. B366 fittings in 686 keep that exposure in the same alloy as the pipe, welded with matching 686 filler so neither the fitting nor the weld becomes the weak point.

Testing and Certification

Every fitting is marked with grade and heat number and supplied with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate. Positive material identification confirms the molybdenum-tungsten grade, dye-penetrant testing checks bevels and formed surfaces, and dimensions are checked against the ordered schedule. NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 certification and third-party inspection are available under our ISO 9001:2015 system.
