Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 686 tube (N06686, Werkstoff 2.4606), seamless to ASTM B622 and welded to ASTM B626, from Mumbai to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

686 tube goes into the heat exchanger that has already eaten a 625 or C-276 bundle. Hot acid condensate carrying chlorides or fluorides, mixed oxidising and reducing acids, bleach-plant liquors – these are the process sides where its 15–17% molybdenum and 3.0–4.4% tungsten on a 19–23% chromium base hold out, and where a tube failure means a shutdown rather than a repair.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Annealed 686 tube runs around 720 MPa tensile. It is a solid-solution alloy supplied solution annealed, with carbon held to 0.01% maximum, which limits carbide precipitation in the heat-affected zone – the reason tube-to-tubesheet welds keep their corrosion resistance.

Industry Applications

Why Inconel 686 tube

Specify 686 tube when the exchanger has a failure history in 625 or C-276, or when the process chemistry is known to be beyond them – not as a default, because it costs more. Seamless tube to B622 and welded tube to B626 are both available: B626 tube is made by automatic welding without filler metal and supplied solution annealed, and is intended for heat-exchanger and condenser service, so welded tube is a sound and often cheaper choice for a bundle.
