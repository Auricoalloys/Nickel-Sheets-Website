Why Inconel 625 LCF Sheets Excel in Industrial Applications

Inconel 625 LCF (UNS N06626, AMS 5879) is an advanced variation of Inconel 625 engineered specifically for improved low-cycle fatigue (LCF) performance at high temperatures. It offers outstanding tensile strength, thermal stability, and resistance to pitting and stress corrosion cracking, making it indispensable for cyclically stressed components in marine environments, gas turbines, and chemical reactors.

This advanced alloy retains high ductility and toughness, essential for fluctuating stress conditions that cause premature failure in conventional materials. The enhanced fatigue properties come without sacrificing its excellent corrosion resistance to acidic and saline environments common in oil & gas and power generation.

Aurico Alloys LLP provides Inconel 625 LCF Sheets certified to AMS 5879, and dual-certified to ASTM B443 and ASME SB443 as N06625 material where a project calls for those standards - N06626 is not itself covered by ASTM or ASME.
