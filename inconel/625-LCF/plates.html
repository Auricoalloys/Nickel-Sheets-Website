INCONEL alloy 625LCF (UNS N06626, sometimes referenced with W.Nr. 2.4856 in data sheets) was developed as a fatigue-resistant, bellows-quality version of INCONEL alloy 625, using alloying, melting and processing strategies to optimise low-cycle fatigue performance in components experiencing frequent mechanical and thermal cycling. Literature notes that 625 LCF has essentially the same Ni–Cr–Mo–Nb base as Alloy 625 but with tighter limits on impurity elements and a carefully controlled microstructure that provides enhanced fatigue strength, high resistance to intergranular corrosion and resistance to both oxidising and reducing atmospheres while retaining the general seawater, sour-gas and acid resistance of standard 625.
