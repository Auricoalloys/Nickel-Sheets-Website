Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel 601 coil and slit strip (N06601, Werkstoff 2.4851) to ASTM B168 and AMS 5870 from Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

Strip in 601 becomes furnace hardware: muffles and retort liners, baskets and trays, heat shields and burner parts that are blanked, formed and welded from coil. Those parts share one fate – they heat and cool with every batch – and 601’s aluminium-stabilised oxide is what keeps them from scaling away across hundreds of cycles.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Strip is supplied annealed for forming, at roughly 550–760 MPa tensile, with the ductility to be blanked and bent into baskets and liners. In service the design value is oxidation resistance to about 1200°C through thermal cycling, not room-temperature strength.

Industry Applications

Why Inconel 601 coil

For furnace parts that cycle, 601 strip outlasts 600 because its scale stays on. For parts that also carry load at temperature, the usual answer is a combination: a furnace is often built with 617 in the load-bearing frame and 601 as the skin facing the heat. Buying 601 in coil keeps the cost of that skin down on long production runs.
