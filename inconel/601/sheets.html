Why Choose Inconel 601 Sheets for Demanding Projects?

Inconel 601 (UNS N06601, ASTM B168, DIN 2.4851) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy with an innovative aluminum addition that delivers outstanding resistance to high-temperature oxidation (up to 1200°C), spalling, carburization, and thermal fatigue cycles. This makes it ideal for furnace components, heat treatment muffles, nitric acid production equipment, petrochemical reformers, and power plant superheaters exposed to extreme process atmospheres.

Aurico Alloys LLP offers Inconel 601 Sheets in a wide range of thicknesses and customized finishes, always supplied with full ASTM B168 or ASME SB168 certification, mill test reports, and traceability for mission-critical projects and export requirements. Compared to 310/316 stainless or lower-cost nickel alloys, Inconel 601 delivers proven lifecycle value by minimizing outages and costly metal failure.
