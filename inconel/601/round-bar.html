    Inconel 601 (UNS N06601, ASTM B166) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy designed especially for high-temperature oxidation resistance and moderate corrosion prevention. Its chemistry withstands high heat, chemical attack, and thermal cycling, making it ideal for heat exchangers, furnace components, chemical reactors, and marine hardware. Its balanced composition delivers long life, superior toughness, weldability, and machinability.
