Chemical Composition

Nickel 58-63% with 21-25% chromium, 1.0-1.7% aluminium and the balance iron. The aluminium forms a tightly adherent oxide that resists spalling under thermal cycling.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Annealed tensile strength around 550-760 MPa. Outstanding oxidation resistance to 1200°C and good strength under cyclic heating.

Industry Applications

Why this grade

Inconel 601 adds over 1% aluminium to the 600 chemistry, so it forms a tight alumina scale and resists oxidation to around 1200°C. It is the grade to specify when the part faces radiant heat rather than load: above 1000°C its creep strength falls well short of 617, so a furnace is often built with 617 in the load-bearing frame and 601 as the skin facing the heat.
