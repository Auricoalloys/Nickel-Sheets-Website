Inconel 601 (Alloy 601, UNS N06601) is a nickel‑chromium alloy with an aluminium addition that provides outstanding resistance to oxidation and other forms of high‑temperature corrosion, forming a tenacious oxide scale that remains bonded even during repeated heating and cooling between 1000–1200°C. Technical summaries highlight that its nickel base plus substantial chromium content give resistance to many corrosive media and high‑temperature environments, while aluminium further enhances oxidation resistance and resistance to carburising and carbon‑nitriding conditions in furnace atmospheres.

For procurement heads, engineers, fabricators, OEMs and hardware or industrial supply stores serving chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma and power generation, Alloy 601 plates solve a specific problem: stainless steels and many generic nickel alloys suffer rapid scaling, oxide spalling and distortion when exposed to cyclic high‑temperature oxidising and sulfur‑bearing atmospheres, whereas Alloy 601 maintains a stable oxide film, high‑temperature strength and good ductility under thermal shock. Mechanical data for plate and bar show room‑temperature tensile strengths in the 765–785 MPa range, yield strengths around 410–414 MPa and elongation around 36–41% after solution treatment at about 1200°C, while oxidation studies report “exceptional resistance” to scaling and absence of embrittling sigma phase up to about 2200°F (≈1200°C).
