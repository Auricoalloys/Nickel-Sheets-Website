Chemical Composition

Chemically the same Ni-Cr-Mo-Nb system as alloy 625, but produced to a controlled fine grain size with tightened carbon, nitrogen and silicon limits.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

The controlled microstructure roughly doubles low-cycle-fatigue life against standard 625 while keeping the same corrosion behaviour and annealed strength.
