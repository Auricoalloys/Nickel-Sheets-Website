Why Inconel 600 Sheets Stand Out

Inconel 600 (UNS N06600, ASTM B168) is a versatile nickel-chromium alloy notable for excellent resistance to oxidation, carburization, and corrosion in oxidizing and mildly reducing environments. Its robustness at temperatures up to 1150°C enhances equipment longevity and operational safety, making it ideal for heat exchangers, furnace parts, and nuclear reactor components.

The alloy’s balanced composition delivers high tensile strength, good ductility, and outstanding creep rupture strength under thermal stress. Its fabricability and weldability also make it popular for manufacturing complex components needed in critical industrial processes.
