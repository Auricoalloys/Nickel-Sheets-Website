    Inconel 600 (UNS N06600, ASTM B168) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy known for excellent oxidation resistance and corrosion resistance in highly oxidizing atmospheres and moderate reducing environments. Its resilience against heat, acidic media, and chloride stress corrosion cracking makes it suitable for heat exchangers, furnace components, chemical reactors, and marine parts exposed to seawater and steam applications. Compared to basic stainless steels and other nickel alloys, Inconel 600 offers an optimal balance of strength and corrosion resistance at temperatures up to 1100°C.
