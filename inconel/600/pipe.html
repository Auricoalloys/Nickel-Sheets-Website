Chemical Composition

Nickel 72% minimum with 14-17% chromium and 6-10% iron. The high nickel content gives resistance to chloride stress-corrosion cracking, while chromium handles oxidising conditions.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Annealed tensile strength around 550-690 MPa with yield near 240 MPa and elongation above 35%. Useful in continuous service to roughly 1090°C.

Industry Applications

Why this grade

Inconel 600 carries 14-17% chromium and about 72% nickel. The high nickel is what earns it its place: it resists chloride-ion stress-corrosion cracking and caustic attack, and holds up under carburising atmospheres. Where it is known to be vulnerable is intergranular cracking in high-temperature primary water, which is the reason 690 exists. For chemical and heat-treatment service away from that environment, 600 remains the cheaper and entirely sound choice.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on the specification we can add positive material identification, ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM G28 or A262, hydrostatic testing, and third-party inspection release from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.
