Specifications & Standards

Inconel 600 foil meets ASTM B168 (sheet/strip), ASME SB168, DIN 2.4816 for oxidation up to 1150°C. Inconel 601 (UNS N06601, DIN 2.4851) follows ASTM B166/B168, excelling in carburizing atmospheres. Inconel 617 (N06617, DIN 2.4663) complies with AMS 5887 and ASTM B166 for aeroengine parts.

Testing includes ASTM E8 tensile, G28 corrosion, ensuring compliance for pharma and marine exports with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Product Forms & Availability

Foils range 0.01-0.5 mm thick, widths to 1000 mm, annealed per ASTM specs for stamping in OEM heat shields. 617 foil supports cryogenic to 980°C service.

Mumbai inventory offers custom slitting/decoiling for fabricators in power and chemical sectors.

Mechanical & Physical Properties

Inconel 600: UTS 550 MPa, yield 240 MPa, 35% EL, density 8.47 g/cm³, thermal cond. 14.9 W/mK. 601 retains strength to 1000°C with 45% EL.

617: Creep rupture superior at 900°C, modulus 212 GPa, expansion 11.6 µm/m°C.
