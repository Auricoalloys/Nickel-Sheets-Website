Specifications and Standards

Nickel alloy tubes conform to ASTM B163 (seamless), B165 (seamless pipe/tube), B516 (welded), DIN/EN 17751, and ISO 15156 for sour service. Key grades include UNS N06625 (Inconel 625), N04400 (Monel 400), N02200 (Nickel 200), and N10276 (Hastelloy C276), ensuring compliance with NACE MR0175 for H2S environments.

Additional certifications like ASME SB163 and PED 2014/68/EU support global pressure vessel applications.
