Specifications and Standards

Nickel alloy tubes conform to ASTM B163 (seamless), B165 (seamless pipe/tube), B516 (welded), DIN/EN 17751, and ISO 15156 for sour service. Key grades include UNS N06625 (Inconel 625), N04400 (Monel 400), N02200 (Nickel 200), and N10276 (Hastelloy C276), ensuring compliance with NACE MR0175 for H2S environments.

Additional certifications like ASME SB163 and PED 2014/68/EU support global pressure vessel applications.

Common equivalents: Inconel 625 (W.Nr. 2.4856, NC22DNB4Mo), Monel 400 (2.4360, Nu-30M), Nickel 200 (2.4066, N6), Hastelloy C276 (2.4819). Cross-referencing these standards facilitates international sourcing for industrial supply stores.

Product Forms and Availability

Available as seamless, welded, and ERW tubes; sizes from 1/8" to 12" OD, wall thicknesses 0.020" to 1.0", lengths up to 40ft. Precision tolerances suit heat exchangers and instrumentation; for structural supports, pair with nickel alloy hex bar.
