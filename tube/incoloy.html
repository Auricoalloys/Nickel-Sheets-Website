Aurico Alloys LLP supplies incoloy tube from ready stock in Mumbai to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets. Tube is specified by outside diameter and wall thickness rather than nominal bore, which makes it the form of choice for heat exchangers, instrumentation and any assembly where the fit into a tubesheet or fitting is what governs.

Incoloy covers iron-nickel-chromium alloys built for carburising, oxidising and chloride-bearing service at elevated temperature. U-bending, end facing, tube-to-tubesheet preparation and cut-to-length are handled in house.

Chemical Composition

The 800 series holds 30-35% Ni, 19-23% Cr and the balance iron; 800H and 800HT are controlled to 0.05-0.10% carbon with a coarser grain size for creep strength. Incoloy 825 adds 2.5-3.5% Mo, 1.5-3.0% Cu and titanium stabilisation.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Minimum tensile strength is typically 450-520 MPa. The 800H/800HT grades are the standard choice for continuous service between 600°C and 1100°C where creep-rupture strength governs design.

Industry Applications

How tube is used

Tube is thin-wall hollow section bought for heat transfer and for carrying fluid in small bores. It goes into shell-and-tube heat exchanger and condenser bundles, feedwater heaters and deaerators, U-bends and coiled tubing, and instrumentation and hydraulic lines. Wall thickness, straightness and the ability to bend without thinning the outer wall are what the buyer is specifying.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on grade and specification we can supply positive material identification (PMI), ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM A262 or G28, hydrostatic testing, and third-party inspection release notes from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.

Aurico Alloys LLP operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system. Material for sour service can be supplied certified to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 where the grade permits.
