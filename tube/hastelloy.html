Specifications and Standards

Hastelloy® tubes conform to rigorous standards like ASTM B622 and B619 for seamless and welded forms (UNS N10276 for C-276), ensuring compliance in pressure vessels and piping. DIN 2.4819 and EN equivalents support global procurement, with testing including hydrostatic, non-destructive electric, and tension tests per ASME SB specifications. These standards guarantee reliability for OEM manufacturers and fabricators targeting chemical and marine sectors.

Hastelloy® tubes are available seamless (3.35-101.6mm OD, up to 6m lengths) and welded (up to 1219mm OD, Sch 5-160), in round profiles ideal for heat exchangers and piping. Custom sizes suit fabricators, with complementary forms like Hastelloy hex bar for fittings. Stock supports rapid delivery for power generation and pharma projects.

Chemical Composition

For Hastelloy C-276, nickel balance (min 57%), Mo 15-17%, Cr 14.5-16.5%, Fe 4-7%, W 3-4.5%, with low C (0.02% max), Si (0.08% max). C-22 features higher Cr (22%), while B-2 emphasizes Mo (26-30%) with minimal Cr (<1%). These compositions drive exceptional resistance in chloride-contaminated acids.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

C-276 offers tensile strength 690-827 MPa, yield 283-345 MPa, elongation 40-50%, density 8.89 g/cm³, melting range 1323-1371°C. Thermal expansion averages 11.2-16.0 × 10⁻⁶ m/m-K, conductivity 7.2-28.1 W/m-K. Properties hold up to 1000°C, suiting high-temperature power apps.

Applications by Industry

In chemical processing, Hastelloy® tubes handle hot contaminated mineral acids, hypochlorite, and acetic anhydride, where the global Hastelloy market hit 163,000 tonnes in 2024 led by this sector. Oil & gas favors C-276 for sour gas and seawater injection over SS316 due to pitting resistance.

Marine uses resist brine and crevice corrosion; pharma ensures purity in reactors; power generation tubes withstand flue gas desulfurization. OEMs and industrial stores select for longevity in geothermal and LNG.
