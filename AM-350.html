AM 350 (UNS S35000)

AM 350 (UNS S35000) is a chromium–nickel–molybdenum semi-austenitic stainless that is hardened by transformation and ageing rather than by cold work alone. Supplied in the SCT condition, it combines high strength with useful corrosion resistance, which is why it is used for aerospace structural parts, jet-engine components, springs and diaphragms.
