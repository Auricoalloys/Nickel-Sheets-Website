AlSi10Mg Powder Supplier and Exporter

AlSi10Mg powder (EN AC-43000 equivalent) is a nitrogen or argon gas atomised aluminium casting alloy feedstock for additive manufacturing, supplied to ASTM F3318 (PBF) in 5-25 µm, 15-53 µm, 45-105 µm, 45-150 µm, 53-150 µm size fractions.

The aluminium alloy that actually prints well. Rapid solidification in LPBF produces a much finer structure than casting, so printed properties exceed the cast equivalent.

What Properties Does Printed AlSi10Mg Reach?

Printed properties depend on the heat treatment, not just the alloy, so they are given per condition.

Typical mechanical properties of AlSi10Mg by condition
ConditionTensile strength Yield strengthElongationHardness
As built400-460 MPa220-280 MPa4-8%110-130 HV
Stress relieved at 300 C300-350 MPa180-230 MPa8-12%85-105 HV
T6330-360 MPa230-260 MPa6-10%100-120 HV

Unusually, as-built AlSi10Mg is stronger than the T6 condition. The fine silicon network from rapid solidification is lost on solution treatment, so T6 is chosen for uniformity rather than for strength.

What Is AlSi10Mg Powder Used For?

Lightweight structural parts, heat exchangers and cold plates, automotive and motorsport components, and housings.

Post-processing

Stress relief at approximately 300 degrees C; T6 where higher strength is required.

Characterisation and Packing

Each batch is supplied with chemical analysis, particle size distribution by laser diffraction to ASTM B822, apparent and tap density, and flow rate by Hall or Carney funnel. Morphology imaging, oxygen and nitrogen analysis and reporting to ISO/ASTM 52907 are available where your qualification requires them.

Powder is packed under argon in sealed containers with desiccant, labelled with lot number, alloy, particle size cut and date of atomisation.
