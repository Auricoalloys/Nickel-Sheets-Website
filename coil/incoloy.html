Aurico Alloys LLP supplies incoloy coil from ready stock in Mumbai to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets. Coil is the most economical way to buy sheet-gauge material when parts are blanked, roll-formed or slit to strip, because it removes the cut-length premium and keeps the surface consistent along the whole run.

Incoloy covers iron-nickel-chromium alloys built for carburising, oxidising and chloride-bearing service at elevated temperature. In-house slitting, cut-to-length, levelling and edge conditioning let us ship coil already sized for your press or roll former.

Chemical Composition

The 800 series holds 30-35% Ni, 19-23% Cr and the balance iron; 800H and 800HT are controlled to 0.05-0.10% carbon with a coarser grain size for creep strength. Incoloy 825 adds 2.5-3.5% Mo, 1.5-3.0% Cu and titanium stabilisation.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Minimum tensile strength is typically 450-520 MPa. The 800H/800HT grades are the standard choice for continuous service between 600°C and 1100°C where creep-rupture strength governs design.

Industry Applications

How coil and strip is used

Coil and strip are continuous cold-rolled flat product, bought to be worked rather than installed as they arrive. The common routes are stamping and deep drawing of gaskets, bellows, diaphragms and seals; forming of springs and spring washers; slitting to narrow strip for electrical contacts, battery tabs and current collectors; shim and vessel-lining stock; and as the feedstock from which welded tube and pipe are made. Thickness tolerance and temper matter more here than they do in any other form, because the material has to survive the press.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on grade and specification we can supply positive material identification (PMI), ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM A262 or G28, and third-party inspection release notes from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.

Aurico Alloys LLP operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system. Material for sour service can be supplied certified to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 where the grade permits.
