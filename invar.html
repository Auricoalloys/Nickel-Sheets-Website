Invar 36 (UNS K93600)

Invar 36 (UNS K93600, FeNi36, ASTM F1684) is an iron–nickel alloy of roughly 36% nickel, the composition at which thermal expansion falls to its minimum. Near room temperature it moves less than almost any other metal, which is why it is specified for precision instruments, metrology fixtures, optical benches, composite layup tooling, laser cavities and LNG containment, wherever a dimension has to hold while the temperature does not.
