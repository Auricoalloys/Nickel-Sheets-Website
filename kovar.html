Kovar (UNS K94610)

Kovar (UNS K94610, ASTM F15) is an iron–nickel–cobalt controlled-expansion alloy whose thermal expansion closely follows that of borosilicate glass and alumina ceramics. That match is what makes it the standard choice for hermetic glass-to-metal seals in electronic packages, vacuum tubes, microwave devices, sensor housings and semiconductor headers, where a mismatched seal would craze or leak through thermal cycling.
