    SMO 254 (UNS S31254, 1.4547, F44) is a 6% molybdenum super austenitic stainless rich in nickel (18–18.5%) and chromium (19.5–20.5%), with nitrogen for extra pitting and crevice resistance. This chemistry brings it close to titanium and nickel alloys, yet with cost and machinability similar to high-end stainless. It thrives in seawater, desalination, sour oil/gas, chemical production, pharmaceuticals, and pulp and paper bleach lines.

    With a minimum yield of 300 MPa and tensile over 650–680 MPa, plus 35–50% elongation, SMO 254 bars withstand high pressure, vibration, and repeated thermal cycling. Their resistance to stress corrosion cracking, excellent fabricability, and workability in field conditions gives engineers, OEMs, and fabricators a material trusted for everything from tankage and heat exchangers to electrodes, pump shafts, and even precision battery connector hardware.

    Aurico Alloys LLP provides SMO 254 round bars per ASTM A276, A479, EN 10272, with sizes from 3mm to 500mm+, in rough, turned, ground, bright, or polish finishes. Each bar ships with RoHS/REACH compliance, full MTCs, batch traceability, rapid export, and custom cutting for urgent repairs or project supply backed by specialists who know global marine, energy, and process industry standards.
