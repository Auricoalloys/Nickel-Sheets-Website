Alloy 31 (UNS N08031, DIN 1.4562) is a super-austenitic alloy bridging the gap between high-alloy stainless steels and expensive nickel alloys. Its potent mix of 30–32% nickel, 26–28% chromium, 6–7% molybdenum, plus copper and nitrogen, gives it robust resistance against sulfuric, phosphoric, and hydrochloric acids even in chloride and mixed-acid systems where 904L, Alloy 20, and others struggle.

Supplied by Aurico Alloys LLP as per ASTM B625, Alloy 31 Sheets are available hot/cold rolled, annealed, pickled, or polished; with full mill certificates and complete traceability.
