904L (UNS N08904, ASTM A240) is a high-alloy austenitic stainless steel with elevated levels of nickel, chromium, molybdenum, and copper. It offers exceptional resistance against highly corrosive environments, including oxidizing acids and chloride-bearing media often found in chemical processing, marine systems, and power generation plants. Notably, its low carbon content minimizes carbide precipitation, enhancing weldability and post-weld corrosion resistance, making it ideal for critical fabrications.

FAQ & Insights

904L exhibits significantly higher nickel, molybdenum, and copper content than 304 or 316 grades, granting better resistance to strong acids, chlorides, and stress corrosion cracking.

Thanks to its low carbon and stabilized structure, 904L is weldable with minimal risk of carbide precipitation or sensitization, enabling easier fabrication and repair in the field.
