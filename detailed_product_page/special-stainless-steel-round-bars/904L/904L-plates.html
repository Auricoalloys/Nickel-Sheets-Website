Aurico Alloys LLP supplies alloy 904l plates (N08904, Werkstoff 1.4539) from ready stock at our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai.

Alloy 904L is the grade that copper makes: that addition buys sulphuric acid resistance no ordinary austenitic stainless has, at a fraction of what a nickel alloy costs.

Chemical Composition

A high-alloy austenitic grade carrying 19-23% chromium, 23-28% nickel, 4.0-5.0% molybdenum and - the distinguishing addition - 1.0-2.0% copper, with carbon held to 0.020% maximum.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Minimum tensile strength 490 MPa with 220 MPa yield and 35% elongation. The copper addition is what gives it resistance to dilute sulphuric acid across a wide concentration range, and the low carbon keeps welds free of sensitisation without stabilisation.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on the specification we can add positive material identification, ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM A262 or G28, ferrite measurement, and third-party inspection release from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.
