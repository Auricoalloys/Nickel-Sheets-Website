Alloy 28 (Sanicro 28, UNS N08028, 1.4563) is a highly alloyed austenitic stainless with a powerful blend of 30–32% nickel, 26–28% chromium, 3–4% molybdenum, and added copper and nitrogen. This engineered balance grants resistance to strong acids (including sulfuric and phosphoric acid), seawater, sour gas, chlorides, and stress corrosion cracking satisfying specs for heat exchangers, piping, evaporators, desalinators, digesters, and reactors. Specified as ASTM B709 and EN 10028-7, Alloy 28 delivers what 316L and 904L cannot.
