AL-6XN (UNS N08367, ASTM A240) is a high molybdenum, nitrogen-enhanced super-austenitic stainless steel designed to provide excellent resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking in chloride-rich and acidic conditions. Its robust composition, with high nickel and chromium contents plus copper and nitrogen stabilization, places it above 316L and 904L in corrosion resistance and mechanical properties.

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies AL-6XN Sheets in hot-rolled, cold-rolled, annealed, and pickled conditions with full ASTM A240, UNS, and ROHS certification, ensuring authenticity and traceability. Customized solutions for thickness, finishes, and sizes are readily available, supporting OEM, fabricators, and industrial supply chains.
