Product data list density around 8.06 g/cm³, melting range about 1320–1400 °C, modulus of elasticity around 195 GPa and mechanical properties in plate and strip with typical yield strength 57 ksi (≈393 MPa), tensile strength 112 ksi (≈770 MPa) and elongation about 44% in 2 in.
