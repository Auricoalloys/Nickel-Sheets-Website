    AM 350, identified by UNS S35000 and governed by specifications like ASTM A350, bridges the gap between conventional martensitic steels and high-end corrosion-resistant grades. Its unique chemistry rich in chromium, nickel, molybdenum, and strengthened through precipitation hardening endows it with remarkable mechanical toughness, heat treatability, and resilience even against sour gas, phosphoric acid, and seawater assaults. Procurement heads, engineers, OEM manufacturers, and hardware distributors find in AM 350 a dependable material that balances cost, performance, and certification.

    Applications & Benefits

    • Chemical Industry: Ideal for compressor components, reactors, and valves used in aggressive chemical processes due to excellent corrosion resistance and high strength.
    • Oil & Gas: Downhole equipment, flanges, and fittings benefitting from its sour gas resistance plus high temperature strength.
