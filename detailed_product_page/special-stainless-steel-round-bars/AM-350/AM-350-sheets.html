AM 350 (UNS N08350, ASTM B670) is a high-strength, heat-treated nickel alloy tailored for highly corrosive atmospheres, combining high nickel, chromium, molybdenum, and columbium (niobium) to combat harsh sour gas environments including H2S and chlorides. Its balanced chemistry helps prevent carbide precipitation and provides excellent creep and fatigue resistance, suitable for pressure vessels, heat exchangers, pumps, and marine applications demanding long service life.

Aurico Alloys LLP provides AM 350 Sheets with full ASTM B670 certification, mill test certificates, and global traceability. Our hot-rolled and solution-annealed sheets come in multiple thicknesses and finishes, ready for custom fabrication, urgent repairs, or OEM manufacturing.

Specifying Aurico’s AM 350 Sheets ensures optimal safety, durability, and value for industrial applications where failure is not an option, delivering a cost-effective alternative to more expensive superalloys with proven reliability.
