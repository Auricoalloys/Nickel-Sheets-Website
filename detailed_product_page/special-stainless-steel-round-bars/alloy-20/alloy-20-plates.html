Applications & Benefits

  • Chemical & Oil & Gas: Alloy 20 plates are used in sulfuric‑acid pickling equipment, acid storage tanks, agitator covers, reactor linings, heat‑exchanger plates and piping components in chemical and petrochemical plants, especially where sulfuric acid and mixed chloride/acid environments have defeated conventional stainless steels.
  • Marine & Power Generation: In coastal power plants, offshore topsides and marine chemical dosing systems, UNS N08020 plates provide corrosion resistance against acid condensates, polluted seawater and salt‑laden atmospheres while maintaining adequate mechanical strength for structural and pressure‑retaining parts.
  • Pharma & Food Industries: Alloy 20 plate is used in pharmaceutical and food‑grade chemical systems, including production of certain preservatives and intermediates, owing to its resistance in phosphoric and nitric acid plus chloride‑bearing media and its ability to be supplied with full documentation.

FAQ & Search Trends

Alloy 20 was specifically developed to withstand sulfuric acid, and its Ni–Cr–Mo–Cu balance plus niobium stabilisation gives it very good resistance to general corrosion, pitting, crevice attack and chloride stress‑corrosion cracking in many acid and salt solutions where 304 and 316L fail quickly or require heavy corrosion allowances. Rubber‑lined carbon steel can be cost‑effective initially but is vulnerable to liner damage, under‑film corrosion and difficult repairs, whereas UNS N08020 plate provides a metallic solution that can often operate without post‑weld heat treatment and with better predictability in aggressive chemical service.

Engineers typically specify Alloy 20 as UNS N08020, WNR 2.4660 and cite ASTM B463 / ASME SB‑463 for plate, sheet and strip, along with related product standards such as ASTM B464 for pipe, B473 for bar and B729/B463‑linked tubing specifications.
