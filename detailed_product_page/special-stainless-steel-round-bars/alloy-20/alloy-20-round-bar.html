    Alloy 20 a nickel-iron-chromium austenitic alloy (UNS N08020, WNR 2.4660) is engineered to thrive in aggressive media including sulfuric, phosphoric, and nitric acids, chlorides, caustics, and seawater. Due to a fine balance of nickel (32–38%), chromium (19–21%), and copper (3–4%), with molybdenum and niobium stabilization, these bars deliver both exceptional resistance to pitting/crevice corrosion and robust stress cracking defence even above 500°C. Aurico Alloys LLP offers these bars in a full range of diameters and finishes for every critical OEM, process line, or fabrication need.

    Available under ASTM B473 with full batch traceability, and recommended for pressure vessels, mixers, and heat exchangers, Alloy 20 bars are easily machined, welded, and cut to specification. Each bar is supplied with mill certificates, RoHS/REACH documents, and international packing for reliable use by engineers and fabricators across regulated sectors.

    Unlike standard stainless or commodity alloys, Alloy 20 round bars protect assets from costly process disruptions especially in pharma, fertilizer, marine desalination, and petroleum. No matter the location or corrosive challenge, buyers trust Aurico for affordable, certified, and on-time supply, supporting the efficiency of hardware stores and major plant projects.
