Alloy 926 (UNS N08926, EN 1.4529, ASTM/ASME) was developed as an upgrade to 904L, offering higher molybdenum (6-7%) and nitrogen (0.15-0.25%) content for outstanding corrosion resistance in chloride, sulfuric, and phosphoric acid environments. The addition of copper also improves performance in reducing acids, while superior mechanical strength and weldability make it ideal for pressure vessels, tanks, evaporators, desalination units, seawater systems, and acid process lines where failure is not an option.

At Aurico Alloys LLP, Alloy 926 Sheets come with full ASTM/EN certification, in hot/cold rolled, pickled, polished, and custom-cut forms.
