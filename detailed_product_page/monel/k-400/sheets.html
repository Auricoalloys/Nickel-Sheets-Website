Monel K 400 (UNS N04400, ASTM B164) is a nickel-copper superalloy engineered for outstanding strength, corrosion resistance to seawater, sulfuric acid, and high-stress hydrogen sulfide environments. Its aluminum and titanium microalloying imparts superior resistance to gaseous hydrogen embrittlement and stress corrosion cracking, while retaining ductility and weldability. The alloy offers tensile strength upwards of 900 MPa and excellent performance up to 315°C, making it ideal for valves, pumps, compressors, subsea equipment, and heat exchangers in the harshest industrial settings.
