Inconel® foils, key grades 625 (UNS N06625, ASTM B443 Gr1/2), 600 (UNS N06600, ASTM B166), 718 (UNS N07718), offer ultra-thin (0.01mm-2mm) high-strength barriers in oxidizing/reducing acids, outperforming Incoloy 825 (better in reducing acids) and SS316L (pitting rate >1mm/yr in seawater) with corrosion rates <0.04mm/yr in 0.5M H3PO4 . High Nb/Mo stabilize against SCC, enabling thinner designs that cut material costs by 20-30% for fabricators .

These foils shine as gaskets, liners, shrouds maintaining ≥760 MPa tensile at 650°C, ideal for jet exhausts or flare stacks where alternatives embrittle . OEMs benefit from weldability (GTAW without filler) and traceability .
