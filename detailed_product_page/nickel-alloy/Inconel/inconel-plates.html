Why Choose Inconel Plates for Your Critical Projects?

Inconel plates, typically following UNS N06600 and related grades per ASTM B575 and ASTM B446, are specialized nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloys designed to resist oxidation, pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking in highly aggressive chemical and marine environments. Their exceptional stability at temperatures up to 1100°C makes them indispensable for heat exchangers, reactors, and pollution control equipment.

The unique chemical composition, rich in molybdenum and chromium, synergizes to create passive oxide films that shield components from both acid attacks and chloride-induced damage. Inconel plates provide enhanced weldability and high tensile strength, optimizing fabrication and field repair processes while maintaining integrity under cyclic and thermal stresses.
