Why Choose Inconel Plates for Your Critical Projects?

Inconel is a family of nickel-chromium alloys rather than a single material, and on a plate order the grade is the decision. Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Inconel plate in nine of them: the general-purpose corrosion grades 625 and 600, the aluminium-bearing 601, the creep-resistant 617, the high-molybdenum 686, the high-chromium 690, the metal-dusting-resistant 693, the age-hardened 718 and the spring grade X-750. They resist oxidation, pitting, crevice corrosion and stress corrosion cracking in aggressive chemical and marine service, which is what makes them the material for heat exchangers, reactors and pollution control equipment. Each is rolled to the flat-product standard written for it, and those are listed grade by grade below.

The unique chemical composition, rich in molybdenum and chromium, synergizes to create passive oxide films that shield components from both acid attacks and chloride-induced damage. Inconel plates provide enhanced weldability and high tensile strength, optimizing fabrication and field repair processes while maintaining integrity under cyclic and thermal stresses.

Aurico Alloys LLP guarantees delivery of certified Inconel Plates with comprehensive ASTM, ISO, and NACE MR0175 documentation. Backed by extensive stocking and custom processing capabilities, we ensure that your projects receive authentic, top-quality materials on schedule.

Mechanical Properties

Density and melting range for all nine grades are in the Equivalent Grades & Standards tables above, each read off the mill bulletin named beneath it.

Tensile strength, yield strength and elongation are properties of a grade in a condition rather than of the family – an age-hardened 718 plate and an annealed 600 plate are not comparable numbers, and the same grade differs by thickness and temper – so each grade's own plate page publishes its figures. The mill test certificate supplied with your order states the results for the material you receive.
