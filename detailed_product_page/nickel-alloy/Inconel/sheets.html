Inconel alloys (UNS N06625, N06601) covered under ASTM B168 offer a nickel-chromium base with elements like molybdenum, niobium, and iron, designed to maintain strength and corrosion resistance at elevated temperatures beyond 1000°C. Their ability to resist oxidation, pitting, and stress corrosion cracking under harsh environments surpasses traditional stainless steels and other nickel alloys, making them invaluable for heat exchangers, pressure vessels, reactors, and turbo machinery.

Aurico Alloys LLP stocks Inconel sheets with thickness from 0.1 mm to 100 mm, widths up to 2500 mm, and customizable lengths, available in bright, polished, annealed, or ground finishes. With full mill test certificates including PMI and environmental compliances, our Inconel sheets meet international standards ensuring trustworthy quality for every project.

Beyond physical toughness, Inconel sheets exhibit excellent fabricability and weldability, critical for the dynamic construction of process equipment. Their stability under repeated thermal cycling and corrosive exposure reduces costly downtime and replacement cycles, offering long-term value and operational confidence.
