    Inconel alloys, especially grades 600, 625, 718, and X-750 provide extraordinary resistance to high temperatures, stress corrosion cracking, pitting, and aggressive media including seawater, acids, and oilfield fluids. With nickel, chromium, molybdenum, niobium, and other advanced alloying, these bars excel where standard stainless or lower nickel alloys fail. Their mechanical strength and oxidation stability are retained at temperatures up to 1000°C, making them workhorses for power plant heat exchangers, gas turbines, reactors, marine components, and high-pressure valves.

    Aurico Alloys LLP Inconel Round Bars are supplied with ASTM (B166/B446/B637), ASME, DIN, and EN certifications, plus full project documentation for traceability and compliance. Available in diameters from 6mm up to 300mm and custom finishes, they are trusted by fabricators, OEMs, maintenance shops, and hardware stores for urgent repair and new build projects demanding only the best materials.

    Few products combine toughness in cryogenic and high-heat conditions, corrosion resistance, and weldability like Inconel. With a worldwide supply record and strong technical assistance, procurement heads and engineers increasingly select Aurico’s Inconel bars for environments where nothing less than top-tier performance will do.
