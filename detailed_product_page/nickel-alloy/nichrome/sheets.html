Nichrome, identified as UNS N06020 and standardized under ASTM B163, is a sophisticated nickel-chromium-iron alloy celebrated for exceptional resistance to oxidation, corrosion, and thermal stress. Its unique chemical composition and microstructure enable it to maintain integrity in environments ranging from high-temperature furnace components to aggressive chemical processing lines, far outperforming conventional stainless steels or lower-grade nickel alloys.
