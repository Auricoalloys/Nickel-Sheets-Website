    Kovar (UNS K94610, ASTM F15, Alloy K) is an iron-nickel-cobalt alloy expertly engineered to maintain dimensional stability over varying temperatures. Its coefficient of thermal expansion closely matches glass and ceramic, making it essential for hermetic seals in electronic packages, vacuum tubes, aerospace instrumentation, and medical devices. Kovar also offers excellent corrosion resistance and machinability, supporting durable and precise fabrication.

    Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Kovar round bars in multiple sizes ranging from 3mm to 150mm diameters with finishes including cold drawn, ground, polished, and bright annealed. Each batch is accompanied by comprehensive test certification to ASTM F15, full batch traceability, and compliant with EN, DIN, and ISO standards supporting global projects in aerospace, electronics, and chemical processing industries.

    Compared to other alloys, Kovar’s unique balanced composition minimizes thermal stresses and improves seal integrity in electronic packaging and aerospace components, thereby reducing failure rates and maintenance costs. This proven material helps hardware stores, OEM manufacturers, and engineers confidently select the perfect alloy for critical environments.
