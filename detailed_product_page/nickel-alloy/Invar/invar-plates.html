Applications & Benefits

  • Chemical & Oil & Gas: Invar plates used in gauge frames, instrument supports, reference standards, and cryogenic pipe supports reduce drift and misalignment when systems cycle between ambient and cryogenic or moderately elevated temperatures, outperforming carbon steel and stainless that expand and contract far more.
  • Marine & Power Generation: Invar plate tooling for composite blades, precision fixtures in turbines and generators, and support structures near cryogenic LNG loading arms benefit from Invar’s low expansion, while Nimonic plates can handle nearby hot‑section loads.
  • Pharma & Precision OEMs: In metrology frames, optical benches, and precision dosing or inspection equipment, Invar plates and shims reduce recalibration frequency because the structure moves very little with daily temperature variations, which is not achievable with plain steel.
  • Hardware & Industrial Supply Stores: Stocking Invar 36 plate, sheet and shim allows distributors to address niche but high‑value requests from instrumentation OEMs and LNG or aerospace contractors, while Nimonic 75 sheet cut to size, Nimonic 75 thin gauge sheet, and shim stock meet companion needs for hot‑section components in the same projects.

FAQ & Search Trends

Invar 36 has a coefficient of thermal expansion of roughly 1.2–1.5 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹ up to around 200–260°C, compared with about 11–13 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹ for typical carbon steel and austenitic stainless, meaning its dimensional change with temperature is on the order of one‑tenth that of conventional steels in the same range.

Invar is optimised for low expansion at cryogenic to moderate temperatures and is not a high‑temperature creep‑resistant alloy; for sustained service approaching 900–1000°C, Nimonic 75 or other Nimonic superalloys are preferred because they retain much higher strength and oxidation resistance under load in hot gas paths.

Key checks include UNS K93600 / K93601 and 1.3912 identification, compliance with ASTM F1684 or equivalent, certified CTE and mechanical properties near reference values (UTS ≈ 430–490 MPa, YS ≈ 240–260 MPa, elongation ≥30%), and a supplier capable of providing plate, sheet and shim cut to tight dimensional tolerances with full traceability.
