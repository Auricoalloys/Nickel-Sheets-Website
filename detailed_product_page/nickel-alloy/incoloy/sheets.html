Incoloy grades such as UNS N08800 and N08810 conform to ASTM B409 and EN 10204 standards, composing primarily nickel, chromium, and iron with trace molybdenum and copper to enhance resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and chloride stress. These superalloys endure extremes of temperature and chemical attack, making them ideal for heat exchangers, reactors, condensers, and piping systems in corrosive media.

Aurico Alloys LLP offers Incoloy sheets ranging from thin gauge foils to thick plates, supplied in bright, polished, annealed, or ground finishes, with full material test certificates and RoHS, REACH compliance. Our stock supports timely delivery for critical industrial applications globally, ensuring project reliability and compliance.

Offering excellent weldability, fabrication ease, and high tensile strength (up to 700 MPa) with elongation over 30%, Incoloy sheets outshine standard stainless steels and nickel alloys in tough chemical processing environments. Their capacity to maintain structural integrity under thermal cycling and high chloride stress lowers operational risks and maintenance costs.
