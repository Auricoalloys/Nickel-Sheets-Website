Monel alloy 400 (UNS N04400, ASTM B127) is a nickel-copper solid solution alloy known for its excellent corrosion resistance, high tensile strength, and ductility across a wide temperature range. With nickel content above 63% and copper about 28–34%, Monel resists corrosion from seawater, hydrofluoric acid, chloride salts, and alkaline solutions. Its unique blend supports high-performance applications requiring strength and minimal maintenance in harsh chemical and marine usage.

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Monel sheets with thicknesses ranging from 0.3 mm to 120 mm and widths up to 2500 mm. Available finishes include cold/hot rolled, annealed, polished, and grit blasted. All products are supported with comprehensive mill test certificates, PMI, and environmental compliance with RoHS and REACH, delivering confidence and quality assurance to procurement and technical teams.

Unlike ordinary stainless steel grade sheets, Monel’s superior fabricability, weldability, and toughness make it an unsurpassed choice for heat exchangers, pump components, chemical reactors, and saltwater environments, extending maintenance cycles and reducing downtime in critical installations.
