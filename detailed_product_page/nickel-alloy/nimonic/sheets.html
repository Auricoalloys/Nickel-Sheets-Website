Nimonic alloy 75 (UNS N07001) and similar grades are nickel-based superalloys featuring cobalt, chromium, and molybdenum additions that enhance thermal stability and corrosion resistance. Engineered to maintain mechanical properties at temperatures exceeding 700°C, Nimonic sheets provide dramatic performance improvements over standard stainless steels, particularly in turbine blades, heat exchangers, and high-stress chemical reactors.

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Nimonic sheets ranging from 0.5 mm to 25 mm thickness, wide sheet widths, and customizable lengths offered with bright, annealed, and polished finishes. Comprehensive mill test certificates, PMI testing, and RoHS/REACH compliance accompany each batch, affirming quality and traceability for vital industrial projects.

Compared to alternate alloys, Nimonic sheets deliver a combination of tensile strength (up to 1100 MPa), fatigue resistance, and oxidation resistance unmatched in high-temperature corrosive environments reducing operational risks and unplanned shutdowns.
