Invar 36 (UNS K93603, ASTM F1684) is a nickel-iron alloy with about 36% nickel content, delivering near-zero expansion from cryogenic temperatures up to about 260°C (500°F). This dimensional stability alongside excellent strength and toughness makes Invar sheets favored in applications ranging from precision instruments and aerospace components to cryogenics and scientific equipment.

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies high-quality Invar sheets in customizable thicknesses and dimensions, hot or cold rolled, annealed, or polished with full documentation including EN 10204 certifications and strict traceability. This ensures our clients in specialized sectors access reliable material for critical applications demanding performance without compromise.

Besides dimensional reliability, Invar sheets offer good machinability, weldability, and resistance to fatigue and corrosion in various environments, making them a preferred solution for stable tooling, bimetallic thermostats, metrology devices, and microwave systems.
