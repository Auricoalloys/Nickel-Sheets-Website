A coastal acid plant on the verge of scrapping a four-year-old absorber tower due to aggressive sulfuric acid and chloride attack on 316L cladding saw unplanned downtime soar past 11% and annual repair costs cross seven figures. The turnaround came when the OEM retrofitted with Incoloy 825 Plates (UNS N08825) and upgraded high-temperature ducting to Incoloy 800H Plates (UNS N08810), slashing corrosion rates by more than 60%, extending inspection intervals from 12 to 36 months, and restoring availability above 98% in one operating cycle.

For procurement heads, project engineers, fabricators, OEM manufacturers, and hardware or industrial supply stores serving chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma, and power generation, Incoloy plates close the performance gap between commodity stainless steels and expensive nickel alloys. Incoloy 800/800H/825 combine nickel iron chromium matrices with additions like molybdenum, copper, and titanium to deliver high-temperature strength, resistance to carburization and oxidation, and robust immunity to sulfuric, phosphoric, and chloride-rich environments where 304/316L routinely fail.

Applications & Performance Advantages

  • Chemical & Fertilizer: Incoloy 825 plates in acid production, pollution control, and pickling lines resist sulfuric and phosphoric acids, chloride stress-corrosion cracking, and mixed oxidizing/reducing environments where 316L and even duplex grades pit or crack prematurely.
  • Oil & Gas: Incoloy 825 and 800H plates in downhole tools, clad pipes, and heat exchangers handle sour gas (H₂S/CO₂), brines, and high-temperature service better than standard stainless steels, reducing workover frequency and corrosion monitoring interventions.
  • Marine & Power: Incoloy plate cladding on seawater-cooled condensers, FGD units, and waste-heat boilers resists pitting, crevice corrosion, and high-temperature oxidation in chlorides and flue gas where standard austenitic stainless steels lose wall thickness rapidly.
  • Pharma & OEM Fabrication: For clean but aggressive environments (pharma intermediates, specialty chemicals), Incoloy provides a more stable passive layer than stainless, reducing rouge formation, re-passivation downtime, and surface rework.

FAQ & Search Trends

Incoloy 825 offers nickel levels high enough to resist chloride stress-corrosion cracking, with molybdenum and copper improving resistance to reducing acids like sulfuric and phosphoric, and titanium stabilizing against intergranular attack, giving broader corrosion resistance than 316L or duplex in mixed-acid plus chloride conditions.

Incoloy 800H has controlled carbon and grain size plus higher-temperature annealing, giving improved creep and stress-rupture properties from roughly 600 to 980°C and making it preferred for high-temperature headers, reformer tubesheets, and furnace components vs base 800.

A common strategy is to use Incoloy plates for bulk equipment (vessels, ducts, tube sheets) where corrosion plus temperature are dominant, keeping the overall BOM cost under control.
