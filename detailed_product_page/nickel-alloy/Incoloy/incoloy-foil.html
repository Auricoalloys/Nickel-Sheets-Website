Incoloy® foils, primarily grades like 825 (UNS N08825, W. Nr. 2.4858, ASTM B424), 800 (UNS N08800, ASTM B409), 800H/800HT, deliver thin-gauge (0.01mm to 2mm) protection in aggressive environments where SS304/316L pits rapidly and even Alloy 20 falters under stress corrosion cracking (SCC) . These nickel-iron-chromium alloys with Mo, Cu, Ti additions outperform in sulfuric/phosphoric acids (up to 98% H2SO4 at 100°C), sour gas (H2S per NACE MR0175), and seawater, reducing replacement frequency by 40-60% per industry case studies .

Unlike bulkier sheets, Incoloy foils excel as precision liners, gaskets, and thermal shields lightweight yet tough, with yield strengths ≥310 MPa and elongation ≥30% for fabrication ease . For OEMs and fabricators, they cut weight in heat exchangers while boosting uptime; hardware stores value ready stock for urgent spares .
