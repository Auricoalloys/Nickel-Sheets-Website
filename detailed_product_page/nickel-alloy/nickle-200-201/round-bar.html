    Nickel 200 (UNS N02200) and Nickel 201 (UNS N02201) are commercially pure nickel alloys prized for their excellent corrosion resistance, thermal stability, and good mechanical properties. Nickel 200 offers high purity and good formability, while Nickel 201 has a slightly higher carbon content for increased strength. Both grades are used extensively in chemical processing, marine hardware, and electrical components where chloride or acid exposure is prevalent. They maintain excellent performance even in extreme environments with pH levels that would corrode standard steels.

    Industries demanding high corrosion resistance, such as chemical manufacturing and marine operating environments, prefer Nickel 200 and 201 basal alloys for their outstanding resistance to chlorides, acids, and seawater. The alloy’s low corrosion rate in aggressive environments reduces maintenance costs and extends operational lifetimes, offering true value over common stainless steels. Their electrical conductivity also opens up applications in electronics and instrumentation where stability and durability are paramount.
