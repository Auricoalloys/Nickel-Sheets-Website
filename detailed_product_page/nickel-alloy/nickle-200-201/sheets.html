Nickel 200 (UNS N02200, ASTM B162) and Nickel 201 (UNS N02201, ASTM B162/B168) are commercially pure, wrought nickel alloys (>99.6% Ni) engineered for superlative resistance to acid, alkali, and neutral media. Nickel 201 with lower carbon offers even greater stability against graphitization at high temperatures, making it ideal for thermal processes. Both grades are non-magnetic, easily welded, and show excellent formability and toughness from cryogenic to 600°C and beyond.

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Nickel 200/201 Sheets in thicknesses from 0.1 mm up to 100 mm, widths up to 2500 mm, and custom-cut lengths, with hot or cold-rolled, soft-annealed, polished, satin, or bright finishes. All sheets are fully traceable, and supplied per ASTM B162, B168, and EN/DIN standards with complete test certification and RoHS/REACH compliance.

With high thermal/electrical conductivity, tensile strength, and elongation (~45‑50%), these alloys outperform typical stainless grades where pure chemical resistance, safety, and electrical performance matter most. Typical applications include caustic evaporators, marine cooling, pharma dryers, power plant internals, food processing, and even battery tabs for advanced EVs.
