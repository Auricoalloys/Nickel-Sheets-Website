Designed to meet the rigorous demands of procurement heads, engineers, fabricators, OEM manufacturers, as well as hardware and industrial supply stores, Nickel 200 offers a minimum 99.6% nickel content, excellent thermal conductivity, and high impact toughness, while Nickel 201 adds a slight sulfur content to improve machinability without compromising corrosion resistance. Both alloys comply with ASTM B162 and ASTM B165 standards for plate and sheet products, delivering dependable long-term service where purity and strength are essential.
