Commercially pure wrought nickel, Nickel 200 foil offers exceptional ductility (45% elongation), magnetic permeability and thermal/electrical conductivity (70.2 W/mK), with tensile strength ~462 MPa and yield ~148 MPa at RT, making it ideal for battery tab welding and lithium-ion cells where low resistance and formability prevent hot spots and failures . Nickel 201 foil, with carbon limited to 0.02% max (vs 0.15% in 200), resists graphitization and embrittlement above 315°C, retaining mechanical integrity in chemical reactors operating at temperatures above 300°C, fuel cells and hydrogen electrolysis stacks – outperforming 304/316 stainless foils which lack the reducing-acid resistance and purity needed for caustic soda (NaOH) handling or high-purity lab liners .

In marine and pharma, UNS N02200 pure nickel foil stockist provides unmatched resistance to salt-water cooled heat exchanger gaskets and pharmaceutical high-heat sterilization/distillation units, where thermal conductivity (487 BTU-in/hr-ft²-°F) ensures efficient heat transfer without contamination risks . Compared to copper foils (higher cost, lower corrosion resistance in alkalis) or stainless (poor ductility for deep-drawing battery casings), Nickel 200/201 foils excel in soft-tempered form for precision stamped electronic components, with Nickel 200 vs Nickel 201 foil comparison favoring 200 for room-temp apps and 201 for elevated service due to superior thermal stability .
