    Nickel 200 and 201 bars are commercially pure (≥99.6% Ni), solid-solution strengthened, and provide unmatched resistance in alkali, neutral, and reducing environments. Nickel 201 is specifically processed with lower carbon (<0.02%) to prevent graphitization, ensuring safety above 315°C / 600°F. These bars handle stress, cycling, and exposure in corrosive and high-temp settings, outperforming most stainless steels and many high-nickel alloys. Fabricators prefer them for piping, heat exchangers, electrical contacts, and vessel linings.

    Nickel 200 / 201 round bars are ductile, easily welded, and machined with standard techniques. Their toughness is preserved across cryogenics up to 1300°F (704°C), making them a unique choice for customers who demand real-world longevity under stress and corrosion.
