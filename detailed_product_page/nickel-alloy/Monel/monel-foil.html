Monel® foils, mainly 400 (UNS N04400, ASTM B127) and K-500 (UNS N05500), provide thin-gauge (0.05mm-1mm) nickel-copper armor excelling in reducing acids (HF up to boiling), flowing seawater (no pitting), and alkalis surpassing SS316 (0.5mm/yr erosion in brine) and Inconel 625 (costlier for non-oxidizing media) with 67% Ni for non-magnetic, weldable performance . Age-hardened K-500 boosts strength 2x for subsea tools .

Ideal for gaskets, diaphragms, cladding these foils resist stress corrosion in stagnant/flowing seawater, enabling lighter designs that slash weight 15-25% in shipbuilding . Fabricators appreciate easy forming and ASME code compliance .
