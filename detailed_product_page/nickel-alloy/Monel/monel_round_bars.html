    Monel alloys, particularly Monel 400 (UNS N04400) and Monel K-500 (UNS N05500), are nickel-copper alloys containing approximately 67% nickel and 30% copper, with additions of iron and manganese. Monel 400 provides exceptional resistance to seawater, hydrofluoric acid, sulfuric acid, and alkaline environments, while Monel K-500 adds aluminum and titanium for precipitation hardening, delivering approximately three times the yield strength of Monel 400 with maintained corrosion resistance. These alloys excel in marine hardware, chemical processing equipment, pumps, valves, and propeller shafts where saltwater corrosion and chemical attack are constant threats.

    Monel round bars are supplied globally per ASTM B164, ASME SB164, and EN standards, available in diameters from 3mm to 800mm, with bright, polished, ground, and hot-rolled finishes. Complete mill test certificates, NACE MR0175 compliance, and batch traceability support demanding offshore, marine, and chemical processing projects. These bars offer excellent machinability, weldability (TIG, MIG), and can be hot or cold worked for complex fabrications.
