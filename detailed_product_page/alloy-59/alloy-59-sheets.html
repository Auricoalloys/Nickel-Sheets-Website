Alloy 59 (UNS N06059), standardized under ASTM B637, combines nickel, chromium, molybdenum, copper, and iron with stabilizing elements, delivering robust protection in highly corrosive media including sulfuric, phosphoric, and hydrochloric acids. Its capability to withstand stress corrosion cracking and pitting in seawater and acid environments takes it beyond typical high-performance stainless steels and alloy 20 alternatives.
