Stellite (UNS R30545, ASTM F1058) is a cobalt-chromium alloy with complex carbide microstructures formed by tungsten, molybdenum, carbon, and nickel additions. This unique chemistry enables exceptional resistance to abrasion, erosion, galling, pitting corrosion, and oxidation at temperatures up to 650°C (1200°F). Its superior hardness (typically 38-46 HRC) combined with corrosion resistance makes it ideal for highly stressed components like valve seats, cutting tools, turbine blades, pumps, and marine hardware subject to cavitation and severe wear.
