    Elgiloy (UNS R30003, ASTM F1058, W.Nr 2.4711) is a high-performance cobalt-chromium-nickel alloy stabilized with molybdenum and iron, offering resilience against acids, brine, and oxidizers far surpassing typical stainless steels. Its superalloy composition delivers ultimate resistance to stress corrosion cracking, oxidation, and fatigue even under aggressive cycling and high temperatures (up to 850°F / 454°C). Compared to standard grades that suffer in sour gas, marine, and acid process lines, Elgiloy round bars endure where others fail, enabling safe, reliable, and compliant operations.

    Aurico Alloys LLP stocks Elgiloy round bars in a range of diameters and custom lengths, all supplied with full material test certificates and traceability to ASTM, EN, NACE MR0175 (ISO 15156), and AMS 5833/5834 specs. Their precision machining and robust export packaging mean rapid deployment for urgent spares or project schedules in hardware supplies and OEM installations. Industries benefit from low maintenance, high fatigue strength, and long-term assurance that standard products cannot match.
