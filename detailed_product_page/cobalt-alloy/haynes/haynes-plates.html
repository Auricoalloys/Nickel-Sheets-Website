Haynes 25 (UNS R30605) delivers 125 ksi tensile and 1000°F service with wear resistance unmatched by nickel alloys, while Haynes 230 (UNS N06230) offers premier oxidation resistance to 2100°F 100x longer life than 625 in nitriding service.
