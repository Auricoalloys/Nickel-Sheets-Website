    Haynes alloys, such as 25 (UNS R30605), 230 (UNS N06230), and 188 (UNS R30188), feature advanced nickel-cobalt-chromium-molybdenum compositions. These superalloys deliver superior resistance against oxidation, carburization, sulfidation, and chloride-induced attack at temperatures even up to 1150°C. Compared to stainless and basic nickel alloys, Haynes round bars outperform in flue gas desulfurization, power turbines, oil refinery vessels, marine heat exchangers, and pharma reactors where both chemical and thermal stress persist.
