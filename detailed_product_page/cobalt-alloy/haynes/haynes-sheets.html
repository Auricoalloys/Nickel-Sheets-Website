Haynes Sheets (UNS R30605, ASTM B435) are engineered with balanced cobalt, chromium, nickel, and tungsten content delivering superior oxidation and carburization resistance alongside high tensile strengths that can exceed 1100 MPa at elevated temperatures. This makes Haynes alloys indispensable in environments where thermal stability and resistance to hot corrosion are critical, including gas turbines, aerospace, chemical reactors, and heat exchangers.
