    NiCr 20:25 (UNS C70600, ASTM B111, DIN 2.0886) typically contains about 20% nickel and 25% chromium plus copper, iron, and minor elements. The enhanced chromium content provides excellent corrosion resistance while maintaining good thermal conductivity and mechanical strength. This makes it uniquely suitable for evaporators, desalination equipment, shipboard piping, and process systems subject to harsh chemicals and thermal cycles.

    Stocked in diameters ranging from 6mm to 120mm, NiCr 20:25 round bars can be custom machined for OEMs and engineered solutions with certified mill test reports and batch traceability following ASTM B111, DIN, and ISO standards. Hardware suppliers and industrial fabricators widely prefer this alloy for its consistent supply and compliance with international safety protocols.

    As compared to NiCr 30:20 and 40:20, this alloy's higher chromium content offers superior pitting resistance and mechanical durability under aggressive marine and chemical feed conditions key in maintaining plant safety and extending asset life.
