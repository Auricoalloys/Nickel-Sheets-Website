    NiCr 20:25 contains about 20% nickel and 25% chromium with the balance iron, plus small silicon and manganese additions. The high chromium content is what carries the alloy: it forms a tenacious chromia scale that resists oxidation and carburisation in furnace service far better than a 25Cr stainless. That makes it suitable for reformer and furnace internals, element supports, and process hardware subject to sustained heat and thermal cycling.

    Stocked in diameters ranging from 6mm to 120mm, NiCr 20:25 round bars can be custom machined for OEMs and engineered solutions, with certified mill test reports and batch traceability on every consignment. Hardware suppliers and industrial fabricators widely prefer this alloy for its consistent supply and for the composition being confirmed lot by lot on the certificate.

    As compared to NiCr 30:20 and 40:20, this alloy's higher chromium content offers superior scaling and carburisation resistance under sustained furnace heat key in maintaining plant safety and extending asset life.
