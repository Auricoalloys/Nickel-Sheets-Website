Super Duplex 32760 (UNS S32760, EN 1.4501, ASTM A240, F55) is a precisely engineered super-austenitic duplex stainless steel developed specifically for extreme service conditions where standard austenitic and even regular super duplex 32750 materials fail. With a balanced 50:50 austenite-ferrite microstructure, 25% chromium, 7% nickel, 3.6-5% molybdenum, and critical additions of tungsten (0.5-1.0%) and copper (0.5-1.0%), Super Duplex 32760 achieves a Pitting Resistance Equivalent (PREN) exceeding 40 superior to S32750 (PREN ~42) and dramatically outperforming 316L (PREN ~25). The tungsten addition, unique to 32760, dramatically enhances resistance to localized corrosion in oxidizing acids and chloride environments, while copper provides exceptional resistance to sulfuric acid and organic acids. With yield strength ≥550 MPa and tensile strength 750-930 MPa, Super Duplex 32760 delivers 3-4x the strength of 316L austenitic steel while resisting pitting, crevice corrosion, general corrosion, and critically chloride stress corrosion cracking (SCC) in the most aggressive H2S/CO2 sour gas environments.
