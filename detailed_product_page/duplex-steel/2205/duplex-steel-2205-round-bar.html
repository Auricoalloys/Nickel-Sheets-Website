    Duplex grades like 2205 and 31803, represented by UNS S32205 and ASTM A182 respectively, offer a microstructure balanced between austenite and ferrite phases. This unique structure contributes to enhanced toughness, elevated strength, and superior resistance against stress corrosion cracking a known issue in chloride-rich and sour environments. These properties make Duplex 2205 & 31803 the preferred alloys for fabrication and OEM manufacturing that demand both mechanical integrity and chemical resilience.
