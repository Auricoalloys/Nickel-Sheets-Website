Duplex 2205 (UNS S31803/S32205, ASTM A240, EN 1.4462) represents a breakthrough in stainless steel metallurgy a balanced microstructure of approximately 50% austenite and 50% ferrite phases engineered for extreme service conditions. With 22% chromium, 5-6% nickel, 3% molybdenum, and critical nitrogen enhancement (0.14-0.20%), this alloy achieves a Pitting Resistance Equivalent Number (PREN) of ~35, far exceeding 316L and 317L austenitic steels. The dual-phase structure grants high yield strength (≥450 MPa), nearly double that of standard austenitics, while maintaining excellent toughness, weldability, and fabricability. This unique combination makes Duplex 2205 the workhorse grade for pressure vessels, heat exchangers, chemical processing equipment, marine structures, and oil & gas piping where both strength and corrosion resistance are non-negotiable.

FAQ & Insights

Duplex 2205 offers approximately twice the yield strength (≥450 MPa vs. ~205 MPa for 304), superior chloride stress corrosion cracking resistance, and better pitting/crevice corrosion resistance than both 304 and 316. This allows for thinner walls (30-50% reduction), reduced weight, and extended service life in harsh environments.

Duplex 2205 provides cost-effectiveness through lower nickel content, higher strength enabling thinner construction (reducing material costs), excellent weldability without special heat treatment, and superior resistance to chloride stress corrosion cracking. It matches or exceeds the corrosion performance of 316L and 317L at a more attractive price point.
