Super Duplex 32750 (UNS S32750, EN 1.4410, SAF 2507, ASTM A240) represents the pinnacle of duplex stainless steel technology a precisely balanced microstructure combining ~50% austenite and ~50% ferrite phases engineered for extreme service conditions where both ultra-high strength and aggressive corrosion resistance are mandatory. With 24-26% chromium, 6-8% nickel, 3-5% molybdenum, and 0.24-0.32% nitrogen, Super Duplex 32750 achieves a Pitting Resistance Equivalent Number (PREN) of ~42 substantially exceeding standard duplex 2205 (PREN ~35) and dramatically outperforming 316L austenitic steel (PREN ~25). The dual-phase microstructure delivers minimum tensile strength of 750 MPa (109 ksi) and yield strength of 550 MPa (80 ksi) making it three to four times stronger than 316L while resisting chloride stress corrosion cracking (SCC), pitting, crevice corrosion, erosion corrosion, and corrosion fatigue in the most hostile chemical, marine, and sour gas environments.
