FAQ & Search Trends

Haynes 230 combines higher rupture strength, better oxidation and nitridation resistance up to about 1150°C, and improved structural stability compared with iron–nickel–chromium and many Nimonic grades, leading to significantly longer service life and fewer forced shutdowns.

Technical datasheets describe Haynes 230 as readily formable and weldable using standard nickel-alloy procedures, with good resistance to microstructural degradation in service so that components can be repaired without complete re-solution treatment in many cases.

By minimizing cobalt content while maintaining performance, Haynes 230 offers lifecycle cost advantages versus cobalt-rich alloys, and Aurico Alloys LLP’s Haynes 230 supplier network in India supports competitive price per kg with ready stock for export orders.
