Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Haynes® 230 is a nickel-chromium-tungsten-molybdenum alloy with a lanthanum addition. It is the all-round choice in the range, combining creep strength and oxidation resistance to about 1150°C with the thermal stability and fabricability that older alloys such as Hastelloy X do not have, plus useful resistance to nitriding atmospheres.
