Applications & Why Choose Haynes 188

Why this grade

Haynes® 188 is a cobalt-base alloy with a lanthanum addition that improves scale adherence. It carries oxidation resistance to around 1095°C, appreciably higher than alloy 25, together with better long-term thermal stability — which is why combustor liners and transition ducts that would oxidise in 25 are built in 188.

  • Chemical: Ethylene furnace tubesheets, reformer skins – 50% longer life vs 253MA
  • Oil & Gas: Gas turbine combustors & afterburners – aerospace qualified
  • Marine: Jet engine nozzle flaps, thermal fatigue resistant
  • Pharma: High-temperature retorts with clean surface retention
  • Power: Industrial turbine transition pieces to 1093°C
  • Hardware: Nimonic 75 shim stock hybrid solutions
