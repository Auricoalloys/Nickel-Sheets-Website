Why this grade

Haynes® 282 is a gamma-prime strengthened alloy developed to give creep strength approaching Waspaloy and R-41 while staying weldable and formable in sheet. It is ordered where a fabricated hot-section structure — combustor, duct, turbine casing — needs more strength than 230 can supply but still has to be welded.
