Industry Applications and Advantages

Why this grade

Haynes® 214 carries about 4.5% aluminium, so it forms an alumina scale rather than a chromia one. That gives oxidation resistance to roughly 1200°C, the highest in the Haynes high-temperature range, but it is a thin-section alloy — honeycomb seals, foil and catalytic converter substrates — rather than a load-bearing structural one.

Chemical & Pharma

214 foil forms protective Al2O3 in HNO3 plants, outlasting 600H+ by 5x in cyclic service.

Oil & Gas, Marine

188/25 cobalt-base resist sulfidation in flares better than 253MA.

Power Generation

230/282 in combustors; 40% of USC boilers use Haynes for creep life.

OEM/Fabricators

Frequently Asked Questions

What ASTM specs cover Haynes 214 foil?

Haynes 214 (UNS N07214) foil aligns with the Haynes International mill specification for sheet and strip, DIN 2.4633. High-Al design ensures Al2O3 scale to 1315°C. Mills provide 3.1 certs with oxidation tests per ASTM G28, vital for chemical OEM procurement.

Why choose Haynes 230 over Inconel 625?

Haynes 230 (UNS N06230, EN 2.4733) offers superior W-stabilized creep to 1149°C vs 625's limits. ASTM B435 compliant, ideal for power ducts with nitridation resistance. Fabricators note better thermal stability post-exposure.

Is Haynes 25 suitable for welding?

Yes, Haynes 25 (UNS R30605, DIN 2.4778) welds crack-free with matching filler under GTAW. Carbide formers prevent HAZ softening. Used in marine exhausts for ductility retention post-weld, per ASTM F305.
