Applications & Why Choose Haynes® 214

Why this grade

Haynes® 214 carries about 4.5% aluminium, so it forms an alumina scale rather than a chromia one. That gives oxidation resistance to roughly 1200°C, the highest in the Haynes high-temperature range, but it is a thin-section alloy — honeycomb seals, foil and catalytic converter substrates — rather than a load-bearing structural one.

  • Chemical: Carburization resistance in ethylene crackers 50x better than 600
  • Oil & Gas: Reformer tube sheets, furnace radiant coils – continuous 1260°C
  • Marine: Exhaust heat exchangers – superior to Nimonic 81 oxidation resistance
  • Pharma: Kiln linings, sterile processing – nitriding resistant
  • Power: Boiler superheaters, waste incinerators – 955°C+ service
  • Furnace: Mesh belts, catalyst grids, muffles – industry standard
