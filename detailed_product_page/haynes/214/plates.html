Haynes® 214 plates dominate in chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma, and power sectors requiring unparalleled oxidation resistance above 955°C (1750°F). This Ni-Cr-Al wrought alloy outperforms conventional heat-resisters by prioritizing Al₂O₃ scale formation over Cr₂O₃, thriving in air, combustion gases, or chlorine-contaminated flows up to 1315°C perfect for industrial furnace radiant tubes, muffles, and gas turbine honeycombs.

Why opt for Haynes 214 over alternatives like Inconel 600 or RA85H? Its selective alumina layer delivers <0.5 mg/cm² metal loss in 1000h at 1150°C (vs 5-10x higher for others), plus resistance to carburization, nitriding, and chlorides, cutting maintenance 50-70% for fabricators and OEMs. Chlorination-resistant plates excel in petrochemical piping and hydrocarbon plants.
