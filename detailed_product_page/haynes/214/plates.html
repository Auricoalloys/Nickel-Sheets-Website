Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Haynes® 214 carries about 4.5% aluminium, so it forms an alumina scale rather than a chromia one. That gives oxidation resistance to roughly 1200°C, the highest in the Haynes high-temperature range, but it is a thin-section alloy — honeycomb seals, foil and catalytic converter substrates — rather than a load-bearing structural one.
