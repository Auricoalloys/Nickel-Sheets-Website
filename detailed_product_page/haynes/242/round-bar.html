    Haynes 242 (UNS N10242, ASTM B434, DIN 2.466) is an age-hardenable nickel-molybdenum-chromium superalloy characterized by a unique long-range ordering reaction that nearly doubles its strength without sacrificing ductility. This alloy withstands aggressive fluoride salts and hydrofluoric acid vapors, making it ideal for chemical plants and industrial gas turbine engines. Its low thermal expansion and excellent fatigue resistance enable precision components like seal rings, duct segments, and casings to endure severe thermal cycling.

    Available from refined stock in diameters 10mm to 350mm, finished with bright, polished, or rough-turned surfaces, Haynes 242 round bars meet rigorous AMS 5878, ASTM, and ISO standards. Suppliers provide full traceability, mill test certificates, and custom machining to serve OEMs, hardware suppliers, and maintenance teams requiring quality and fast delivery under global compliance.

    Haynes 242, alongside Haynes 242 Round Bars and Inconel 718 Round Bars, offer industries in chemical, energy, marine, and pharma affordable, authentic, and trusted alloys that extend asset lifecycles and reduce operational risks.
