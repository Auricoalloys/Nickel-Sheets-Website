Applications & Superiority vs Common Alloys

Why this grade

Haynes® 242 is strengthened by long-range ordering of a Ni2(Mo,Cr) phase rather than by gamma prime. It delivers very high strength to about 700°C together with a low coefficient of thermal expansion, and it is that combination — strength plus dimensional stability — that puts it into aircraft engine seal rings, containment rings, ducting and fasteners.

  • Chemical: High-pressure reactor vessels, fluoride service equipment – Mo content resists aggressive chemistries
  • Oil & Gas: Gas turbine transition ducts, high-strength bolting – double creep life vs 625
  • Marine: Propulsion casings where low expansion prevents distortion
  • Pharma: Sterile high-temperature fixtures maintaining ductility post-exposure
  • Power: Gas turbine casings & containment rings – industry standard for OEMs
  • Hardware/OEM: High-temperature fasteners, precision sheet fabrications
