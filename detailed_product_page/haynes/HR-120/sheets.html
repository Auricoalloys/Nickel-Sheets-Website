Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Haynes® HR-120® is an iron-nickel-chromium alloy rather than a nickel-base one, which makes it the lower-cost option in the range. It delivers creep strength competitive with far richer alloys at temperatures around 1090°C, so it is specified for industrial furnace and waste-incineration hardware where alloy cost per tonne is part of the decision.

  • Chemical: High-temperature reactor internals, heat exchangers resisting carburization and sulfidation
  • Oil & Gas: High-strength fasteners, bolting, and flanges requiring creep resistance and corrosion stability
  • Marine: Exhaust gas components and turbine blades exposed to harsh thermal cycling
  • Pharma: Sterilization chambers and kiln components needing thermal stability
  • Power Generation: Combustion liners, gas turbine vanes, and boiler tubing for ultra-supercritical plants
  • Fabrication & Supply: Precision-cut sheets and shim stock for demanding OEM and hardware applications
