Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Haynes® HR-120® is an iron-nickel-chromium alloy rather than a nickel-base one, which makes it the lower-cost option in the range. It delivers creep strength competitive with far richer alloys at temperatures around 1090°C, so it is specified for industrial furnace and waste-incineration hardware where alloy cost per tonne is part of the decision.

  • Chemical & Power: Oxidation resistant Haynes HR-120 plates for carburizing furnaces, high creep strength in supercritical heat exchangers and FGD systems.
  • Oil, Gas, Marine: Corrosion resistant Haynes HR-120 plates for offshore radiant tubes, sulfidizing service in sour gas lines and subsea incinerators.
  • Pharma & Waste: Haynes HR-120 plate stockist Mumbai for pharmaceutical waste incinerators, heat treating baskets with 2x life vs 304/310 SS.
  • Superiority: Haynes HR-120 vs Alloy 600: better carburization (lower weight gain), higher strength at 1800°F (18.6 ksi vs 11 ksi); Nb stabilizes against cracking.
