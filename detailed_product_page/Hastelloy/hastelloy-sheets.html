Hastelloy sheets (ASTM B575: UNS N06022, N10276; DIN 2.4602, 2.4819) are solid-solution, high-purity nickel-chromium-molybdenum superalloys. Grades like C22 and C276 offer exceptional resistance to oxidizing and reducing acids, chloride pitting, and stress corrosion cracking performing far beyond SS316, Alloy 20, or simple duplex grades. With ultra-low carbon, silicon, and iron, Hastelloy sheets retain their toughness and ductility even after years in aggressive chemical and marine settings.
