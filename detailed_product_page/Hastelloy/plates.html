Why Hastelloy Plates are the Preferred Choice for Harsh Applications

Hastelloy (UNS N06600, ASTM B575) is a versatile nickel-molybdenum-chromium alloy designed for outstanding resistance to oxidizing and reducing agents, pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking. Its excellent mechanical properties up to 1100°C make it indispensable for heat exchangers, reactors, and pollution control equipment operating in the harshest chemical and energy environments.

High molybdenum and chromium content form a strong passive oxide film that combats aggressive chloride-based media, while the alloy's toughness and weldability allow for fabrication of complex components without loss of integrity. This combination reduces maintenance costs and extends service life in underwater marine and high-pressure chemical process applications.
