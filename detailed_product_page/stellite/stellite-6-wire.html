Stellite® 6 wire (UNS R30006) is used as a hardfacing filler to deposit a cobalt-chromium-tungsten wear layer by TIG, MIG or oxy-acetylene welding. The deposit resists abrasion, galling and cavitation to about 500 °C at a typical 36–45 HRC, so a base component in steel or stainless can be given a Stellite 6 running surface exactly where it wears. It is the most widely applied hardfacing grade for valve seats, tool edges and erosion faces.

Applications & Benefits

  • Weld overlay of valve seats, gates and seal faces on steel and stainless bodies.
  • Hardfacing of tool edges, erosion shields and rolling couples.
  • Repair and rebuild of worn wear surfaces to original dimension.
  • TIG, MIG and oxy-acetylene deposition; supplied spooled or in cut lengths.
