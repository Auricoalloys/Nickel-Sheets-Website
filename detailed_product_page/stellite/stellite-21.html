Stellite 21

Stellite® 21 (UNS R30021) breaks the family pattern: carbon is capped at 0.35% rather than run at 1-2%, and molybdenum 4.5-6.0% replaces tungsten as the strengthener. Far less carbide means it is tougher rather than harder, which is what impact and cavitation service needs.

Key Properties

  • Carbon 0.35% maximum — against 0.9-1.4% on Stellite 6 and 1.4-2.0% on Stellite 12. That is the defining difference.
  • Molybdenum 4.5-6.0% in place of tungsten, strengthening the matrix rather than forming coarse carbides.
  • Nickel to 3.0% with chromium 26-29% on a cobalt balance.
  • Tough, not hard. It is the grade for impact, thermal shock and cavitation, where the high-carbon grades chip.

Applications

  • Hot-work and forging dies
  • Valve components subject to impact
  • Pump and turbine parts in cavitation service
  • Surgical implants and prosthetics
