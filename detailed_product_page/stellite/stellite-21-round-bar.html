Stellite® 21 round bar (UNS R30021) is turned into valve trim, forging and hot-stamping dies, and high-temperature wear parts, and its long-established CoCrMo chemistry is also machined into implant and dental components. With molybdenum in place of tungsten, the tough cobalt matrix gives excellent thermal and mechanical shock resistance and cavitation and galling resistance; it work-hardens in service from about 27–40 HRC and holds up in oxidising conditions to about 1150 °C.
