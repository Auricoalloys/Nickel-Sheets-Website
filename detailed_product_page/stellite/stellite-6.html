Stellite 6

Stellite® 6 (UNS R30006) is the general-purpose member of the cobalt–chromium wear family — chromium 27-32%, tungsten 3.0-6.0% and carbon 0.9-1.4%. The carbon and tungsten form the hard carbides that carry the wear resistance, and at this level they balance hardness against enough toughness to survive impact.

Key Properties

  • Carbon 0.9-1.4% with tungsten 3.0-6.0% — the carbide-forming pair, at the level that balances hardness against toughness.
  • Chromium 27-32% on a cobalt balance, giving corrosion and oxidation resistance alongside the wear.
  • Retains hardness at temperature, where hardened steels temper back.
  • Stellite 12 is harder and Stellite 21 tougher — the three differ mainly in carbon.

Applications

  • Valve seats and trim
  • Cutting tools and saw tips
  • Pump sleeves and wear rings
  • Hardfacing deposits
