    What is 32140 Honeycomb Nickel Strip?

    Battery pack manufacturers face critical challenges of heat, corrosion, and reliable interconnections. The 32140 Honeycomb Nickel Strip offers an engineered honeycomb pattern that enhances weld quality, current distribution, and thermal dissipation, increasing pack safety and lifecycle. Recognized globally, these strips meet ASTM B162 and UNS N02200 standards ensuring consistent performance and regulatory compliance. Aurico Alloys LLP provides authentic, certified 32140 honeycomb strips, simplifying sourcing for OEMs, assemblers, and supply chains worldwide.
