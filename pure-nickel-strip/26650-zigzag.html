    What is 26650 Zig Zag Nickel Strip? (Definition & Overview)

    26650 Zig Zag Nickel Strips are precision-cut, pure nickel interconnects designed to link cylindrical lithium-ion cells in high-performance battery packs. The zigzag pattern increases the contact area for superior spot welding strength, while maintaining low resistivity and thermal stability under repeated cycling. Unlike plated steel alternatives, genuine nickel strips resist rust, heat, and performance drift, even in humid or high-drain environments.

    As packs scale in complexity for e-mobility or grid storage, the consequences of weld failure or rising resistance become exponentially more severe. This is where the mechanical properties of pure nickel high tensile strength, excellent corrosion resistance, and a proven ASTM B162/UNS N02200/201 pedigree are crucial for a safe, warranty-ready solution.
