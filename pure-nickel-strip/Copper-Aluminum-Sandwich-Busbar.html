    What is a Copper-Aluminum Sandwich Busbar?

    These are engineered bimetallic connectors consisting of an aluminum core cladded by an outer copper shell. Used across modern prismatic, pouch, and cylindrical lithium-ion battery packs, this design enables high current capacity and weight savings while preserving reliable weldability and corrosion resistance.

    Copper-Aluminum Sandwich (CCA or bimetal) busbars feature a high-purity aluminum core clad with a thick copper layer (typically 10–35% by volume), delivering 65–74% IACS conductivity with around half the weight of a pure copper bar. Extensive applications across battery modules, energy storage systems, and compact power banks make these busbars a favorite for projects prioritizing pack energy density and manufacturing economy. Their composite structure ensures reliable welding, low resistance, and long-term corrosion performance at critical joints.

    Aurico Alloys LLP’s Copper-Aluminum Sandwich Busbars are batch-certified to GB/T 30586-2014 and ASTM B1005 standards, ensuring consistent bonding strength and mechanical properties. Design flexibility allows specification of length, thickness, copper/aluminum ratios, and finishing supporting both high-volume and bespoke assemblies for battery, EV, or power distribution hardware.

    These hybrid busbars offer weight savings of up to 48% compared to copper, reducing installation burden and materials cost for pack assemblers and hardware suppliers. With Aurico’s established supply chain and technical documentation, every project is optimized for efficiency without compromise on global standards or verified purity.
