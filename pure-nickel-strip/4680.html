4680 Cell Nickel Strip Busbar Manufacturer and Exporter

Aurico Alloys LLP manufactures pure nickel strip busbars for 4680 cells - the 46 mm by 80 mm cylindrical format used in current-generation electric vehicle packs.

The 4680 format concentrates far more current into each interconnect than 18650 or 21700 designs, so busbar cross-section, weld area and thermal path all have to be sized deliberately rather than carried over from a smaller-format pack.

Why Pure Nickel

Pure nickel spot-welds consistently to steel cell cans, resists electrolyte and humidity inside the pack, and holds a stable low contact resistance across thousands of cycles. Plated steel loses that stability as the plating wears through, and bare copper will not weld reliably to a cell can without a nickel interlayer.
