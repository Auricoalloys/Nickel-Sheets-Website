    Why 32140 Cells Create Strip Hell for Pack Engineers

    A 32140 cell is 32 mm across and 140 mm long, so it sheds heat differently from an 18650. At the currents these cells carry, the strip and its welds decide how hot each parallel group runs.

    When Nickel Strips Are Wrong Choice

    High Continuous Current

    Use copper busbars. Nickel's higher electrical resistance makes it run hotter than copper at the same current.

    Budget Packs

    Nickel-plated steel strip costs less than pure nickel. It is supplied on request and not stocked.

    Submerged Use

    Ni corrodes in saltwater. SS 316L better.
