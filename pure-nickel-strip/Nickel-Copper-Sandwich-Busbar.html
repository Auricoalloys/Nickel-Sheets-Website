    Unlike plain copper or nickel strips, Nickel-Copper Sandwich Busbars are tailored for battery pack makers, hardware integrators, and supply chains demanding higher reliability and universal weldability. The copper core provides industry-leading conductivity, critical for EV, ESS, and high-drain power tools, while the nickel exterior shields against oxidation, acidic fumes, and salt spray reducing energy loss and preventing long-term performance drops. This design is especially valuable in high-current, fast-charging, or outdoor deployments where even a milliohm of resistance or a spot of rust can mean capacity fade or warranty complaints.

    Aurico Alloys LLP offers Nickel-Copper Sandwich Busbar with full batch certifications, RoHS/REACH compliance, and dimensions tailored to every lithium-ion, LFP, or prismatic cell layout. Users see fewer weld reworks, more consistent cycle life, and lower pack temperatures even in extremely humid, marine, or industrial sites.

    For battery integrators and supply stores, these hybrid busbars enable rapid spot-welding while resisting both chemical attack and deformation. The result: simplified assembly, improved project ROI, and strong project warranties.
