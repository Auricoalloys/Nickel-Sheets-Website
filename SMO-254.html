SMO 254 (UNS S31254, ASTM A240) combines high molybdenum, nitrogen, and chromium with a duplex microstructure that delivers outstanding resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking far beyond 316L or 904L stainless steels. Its alloy chemistry and balanced duplex phase yield provide both strength and corrosion resistance in harsh acids, seawater, and sour gas environments.

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies SMO 254 Sheets to stringent ASTM A240 and UNS S31254 standards, offering hot and cold-rolled sheets in diverse thickness and finish options. Every batch is supplied with full documentation, including mill test certificates, RoHS and REACH compliance, ensuring global project traceability and peace of mind.
