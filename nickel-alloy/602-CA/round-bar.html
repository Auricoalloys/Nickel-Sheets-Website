    Alloy 602 CA (UNS N06025, ASTM B166) is a nickel-chromium alloy with high aluminum and titanium for excellent resistance to high-temperature oxidation, carburization, and corrosion. It excels in aggressive atmospheres involving gas turbines, heat exchangers, chemical reactors, and furnace parts with long-term durability. Compared to standard Inconel 600 and 601, this grade offers superior protection against thermal degradation at temperatures exceeding 1100°C, making it indispensable in critical industrial applications.
