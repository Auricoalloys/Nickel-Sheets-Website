Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Incoloy 825 wire used for?

Incoloy 825 wire (ERNiFeCr-1, UNS N08825) overlays carbon steel tanks handling 93% sulfuric acid, sour gas separators, phosphoric reactors. ASTM B425 chemistry delivers 300x corrosion resistance vs. 309L where stainless dissolves rapidly. Economic alternative to Inconel for moderate acid service. (59 words)

Can Incoloy wire weld carbon steel to stainless?

Excellent transition alloy—825 wire joins CS to SS with controlled dilution producing 90 ksi weld strength. Resists solidification cracking in 10-25% dilution range. ASME IX qualified PQR demonstrates no cracking welding A516-70 to 304L plate. Industry standard for mixed metallurgy piping. (58 words)

What welding processes use Incoloy wire?

GTAW preferred (0.035-0.125"), GMAW short arc (0.045"), SMAW electrodes available. Pure argon shielding gas; no CO₂ additions. Interpass temperature 250°F maximum. AWS A5.14 chemistry control ensures weld metal corrosion matches Incoloy 825 plate specifications exactly. (54 words)

Is Incoloy 825 wire NACE approved for sour service?

Yes—meets NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 hardness ≤ 28 HRC as-welded. Proven in 0.05 psia H2S partial pressure at 300°F. Superior to 22Cr duplex fillers for deep sour gas wells. Weld hardness verified per NACE TM0177 Method A5 testing protocol. (53 words)

What quality assurance comes with Incoloy welding wire?

EN 10204 3.1 chemistry certificates, diameter/mass tolerance verification, surface defect inspection 100% per AWS A5.14. Heat traceability from AOD melt through drawing dies. Welding procedure qualification records (PQR/WPS) available demonstrating corrosion matching performance vs. base plate. (55 words)
