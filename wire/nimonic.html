During a Middle East gas turbine overhaul, standard alloy blades fatigued prematurely under 800°C cyclic loads, risking blackout in a power plant serving millions.[web:24][web:32] Such failures underscore the demand for Nimonic wire, a precipitation-hardened nickel superalloy designed for prolonged high-temperature service. Engineers in power generation, oil & gas, and OEM sectors specify Nimonic 80A wire (UNS N07080) to ensure reliability and longevity.

Specifications and Standards

Nimonic wire meets UNS N07080 (80A), ASTM B637 for bars/wire/forgings, DIN/EN 2.4631/2.4952 (NiCr20TiAl), BS HR601/3076NA20, and AECMA standards. These ensure creep resistance and oxidation protection up to 815°C for turbine and furnace components.

Fabricators benefit from compliance with MSRR 7095 and NACE for sour environments in oil & gas.

Equivalent Grades

Key equivalents include Nimonic 75 (UNS N06075, 2.4951), Nimonic 90 (UNS N07090, 2.4632), Nimonic 263 (UNS N07263, 2.4650), with AFNOR NC20TA and Werkstoff 2.4952. Higher grades like Nimonic 105 (2.4634) offer enhanced creep strength.

Product Forms and Availability

Available as round wire (0.5mm to 6mm+ dia), coils, straight lengths, spring, and shaped wire for blades and fasteners. Precision tolerances support OEM prototyping; for load-bearing parts, pair with Nimonic hex bar.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

In age-hardened condition: UTS 960 MPa, yield 760 MPa, elongation 20%, hardness 300 HV. Density 8.19 g/cm³, melting point ~1340°C, thermal conductivity 11.2 W/m·K, expansion 12.7 µm/m°C.

Maintains properties to 815°C, outperforming standard alloys in creep-fatigue.

Frequently Asked Questions

What standards apply to Nimonic wire?

Nimonic 80A wire follows UNS N07080, ASTM B637, DIN 2.4631, BS HR601/3076, AECMA PrEn2190. These validate high-temp strength, oxidation resistance for gas turbines, power plants, ensuring OEM reliability in extreme conditions. (51 words)

What are Nimonic wire's main properties?

Age-hardened: UTS 960 MPa, yield 760 MPa, 20% elongation, 8.19 g/cm³ density, service to 815°C. Excels in creep, thermal fatigue for oil & gas tools, turbine components over conventional alloys. (47 words)

Which industries use Nimonic wire?

Power generation (turbines), oil & gas (drilling), chemical (furnaces), marine/pharma (valves). Preferred for superior creep/oxidation vs SS; demand surges with energy transitions. (45 words)

Can Nimonic wire be welded?

Yes, with matching fillers (e.g., 2.4648); age after welding. Suits fabricators for turbine repairs; solution anneal 1050-1100°C, age 700-750°C. (42 words)
