Specifications and Standards

Nickel alloy wires adhere to rigorous global standards ensuring quality and interoperability. ASTM B166 covers nickel-chromium-iron alloys such as UNS N06600 (Inconel 600) as rod, bar and wire, while UNS N04400 (Monel 400) is supplied to B164 and UNS N10276 (Hastelloy C276) to B574 — each of which covers wire. DIN EN specifications, such as 2.4816 for Inconel 600 (NiCr15Fe), and AMS or ISO equivalents guarantee performance in demanding applications.

These standards specify tolerances down to ±0.01 mm for fine wires, bright annealed finishes, and testing for tensile strength and corrosion resistance. Compliance with UNS, ASTM, DIN, and EN ensures traceability for OEM manufacturers and fabricators.

Product Forms and Availability

Nickel alloy wires are available in round diameters from 0.05 mm to 12 mm, flat wires (0.5 x 0.1 mm to 6.0 x 0.8 mm), and custom profiles. Supply conditions include drawn, bright, bright annealed, or oxidized finishes for welding and resistance applications.

For structural needs, complementary products like nickel alloy hex bar provide precision in hexagonal sections for fittings and fasteners.

Chemical Composition

Inconel 600 (UNS N06600): Ni+Co 72%, Cr 14-17%, Fe 6-10%, C max 0.15%, Mn <1%, Si <0.5%. Monel 400 (UNS N04400): Ni 63% min, Cu 28-34%, Fe 2.5% max, Mn 2% max. Hastelloy C276 (UNS N10276): Ni balance, Cr 14.5-16.5%, Mo 15-17%, W 3-4.5%, Fe 4-7%.

Nickel 200 (UNS N02200) is high-purity: Ni 99% min, with low C, Fe, S. These compositions optimize corrosion resistance and high-temperature stability.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Inconel 600 offers tensile strength 550-830 MPa (hot-finished), yield 170-620 MPa, elongation 30-55%, density 8.42 g/cm³, melting range 1354-1413°C. Monel 400 provides ultimate tensile 55 KSI min (380 MPa), yield 15 KSI (100 MPa), elongation 35-40%.

Hastelloy C276: Tensile 700-1000 MPa (sheet), yield 255-280 MPa, excellent in oxidizing/reducing media. Properties like low resistivity (1.03 μΩ·m for Inconel 600) suit electrical and thermal applications.

Applications by Industry

Chemical Processing

Nickel alloy wire excels in reactors, evaporators, and heat exchangers handling acids, sulfides, and alkalis, outperforming stainless steel due to superior resistance to pitting and stress-corrosion cracking.

Oil & Gas

In sour gas wells, pollution control, and offshore rigs, alloys like Hastelloy C276 resist H2S and chlorides; used in scrubbers, valves, and flare systems—critical as corrosion causes 30% of pipeline failures.

Marine

Monel 400 wire dominates for seawater-exposed fasteners and propulsion components, offering unmatched resistance over copper-nickels in flowing seawater.

Pharma

Sterile processing equipment uses Inconel 600 for its hot alkali and nitrogen resistance, ensuring compliance in vessels and tubing .

Power Generation

Turbine components, nuclear reactor parts (Inconel 600T), and heat exchangers rely on creep resistance; prevents failures like seal ring cracks from microstructural degradation.
