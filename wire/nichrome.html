Specifications and Standards

Nichrome wire aligns with ASTM B344/B267 for nickel-chromium resistance alloys, DIN 17471/17470, EN 10095, and ISO 9723. Grades like NiCr 80/20 (UNS N06003 equiv.), NiCr 60/15 ensure oxidation resistance to 1200°C.

Compliance supports furnace and heater fabrication worldwide.

Product Forms and Availability

Round wire 0.018-10mm dia., ribbon/strip (0.01-2mm thick), mesh (e.g., 12x12 0.025" wire), coils/spools up to 1000ft. For supports, consider nichrome hex bar.
