Procurement teams and OEMs in power generation, chemical processing, and pharma rely on NiCr 80/20 wire to maintain process integrity.

Specifications and Standards

Nichrome wire aligns with ASTM B344 for nickel-chromium resistance alloys, DIN 17471/17470, and ISO 9723. Grades like NiCr 80/20 (UNS N06023 equiv.), NiCr 60/15 ensure oxidation resistance to 1200°C.

Compliance supports furnace and heater fabrication worldwide.
