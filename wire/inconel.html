Specifications and Standards for Inconel Wire

Inconel Wire meets global welding filler and resistance wire specifications:

  • UNS N06625: ERNiCrMo-3 filler wire composition for 625 base metal.
  • ASTM B166: Nickel-chromium alloy wire; B167 for seamless pipe equivalents.
  • DIN 2.4856 / EN 2.4856: NiCr22Mo9Nb welding wire classification.
  • Welding: AWS A5.14 ERNiCrMo-3, ASME IX QW462 filler metal spec.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Inconel 625 wire used for?

Inconel 625 wire (ERNiCrMo-3, UNS N06625) welds 625/825 base metals and overlays carbon steel in chemical reactors, oilfield valves, marine propellers. ASTM B166 precision chemistry delivers pitting resistance >180°F seawater where 309MoL fails immediately. Single-pass GTAW overlays match base corrosion perfectly. (61 words)

Can Inconel wire weld dissimilar metals?

Excellent—625 wire joins carbon steel to Ni-alloys with controlled dilution <25%. Resists solidification cracking via Nb Laves phase. ASME IX qualified PQR shows no defects welding A516 Gr70 to 625 plate. Industry standard for pressure vessel cladding in mixed acid service. (55 words)

What welding processes use Inconel wire?

GTAW primary (0.035–0.125"); GMAW spray transfer (0.045–1/16"); PAW for overlays. Pure argon shielding; no flux required. Preheat unnecessary; interpass <350°F. AWS A5.14 specifies chemistry tolerances ensuring weld metal matches base metal corrosion resistance across full chemistry range. (57 words)

Does Inconel 625 wire require post-weld heat treatment?

Typically no—as-welded properties meet ASME VIII requirements. Optional 1600–1800°F stress relief reduces residual stresses 50% for thick sections. PWHT restores ductility lost during multi-pass welding; not required for GTAW single-layer overlays. NACE sour service approves as-welded condition. (56 words)

What certifications come with Inconel welding wire?

EN 10204 3.1 chemistry certificates, heat number traceability, AMS 2371 chemistry verification, tensile properties per AWS A5.14. Lot acceptance testing includes ferrite number, gas content, surface defects. Full welding procedure qualification records (PQR/WPS) available for critical fabrication approvals. (58 words)
