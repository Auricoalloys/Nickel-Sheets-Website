Specifications and Standards for Cobalt Alloy Wire

Cobalt Alloy Wire adheres to rigorous global standards ensuring traceability and performance. Key specifications include:

  • UNS R30035: Primary designation for Cobalt-28Chromium-6Molybdenum alloys with superior corrosion resistance.
  • ASTM F1058: Covers wrought Cobalt-28Cr-6Mo wire for surgical implants and industrial use, emphasizing biocompatibility and strength.
  • DIN 2.4979 / EN 2.4979: European norms for high-temperature, corrosion-resistant cobalt wires.
  • Additional: AMS 5796 for aerospace-grade wire; ISO 5832-12 for medical applications.

These standards guarantee minimum tensile strength of 120 ksi, elongation over 12%, and hardness up to 40 HRC, certified via independent testing labs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is UNS R30035 Cobalt Alloy Wire used for?

UNS R30035 Cobalt Alloy Wire excels in chemical processing, oil & gas, marine, pharma, and power generation for valve trim, springs, and weld overlays. Its ASTM F1058 compliance ensures superior galling resistance and corrosion protection in chloride-rich environments, outperforming stainless by 300% in wear tests. Ideal for OEMs needing durable, high-temp components.

How does Cobalt Alloy Wire compare to Inconel?

Cobalt R30035 offers better abrasion and galling resistance than Inconel 625, especially in slurries or valves, while matching oxidation limits to 1900°F. Inconel edges in pure oxidation; Cobalt wins combined wear-corrosion. Per ASTM specs, choose based on service—stats show 25% longer life in marine apps.

What are the standard sizes for Cobalt Alloy Wire?

Available from 0.5mm to 20mm diameter in straight lengths or coils up to 500kg. DIN 2.4979 grades support custom draws; common for GTAW welding wire at 1.6mm. Suppliers offer annealed or spring temper, with tolerances per ASTM F1058 ensuring precision for fabricators.

Is Cobalt Alloy Wire suitable for sour gas service?

Yes, UNS R30035 meets NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for H2S resistance in oil & gas. Its high Cr/Mo content prevents sulfide stress cracking, unlike 316SS. Tested to 30% H2S at 10,000 psi, it's proven in downhole tools and valves. (51 words)

What certifications come with Cobalt Alloy Wire?

Expect EN 10204 3.1 mill certs, PMI, UT, and hardness tests per ASTM F1058. ISO 9001 suppliers provide full traceability, PED compliance for EU, and biocompatibility docs for pharma. Ensures procurement confidence for global projects.
