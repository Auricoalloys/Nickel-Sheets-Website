Specifications and Standards for Cobalt Alloy Wire

Cobalt Alloy Wire adheres to rigorous global standards ensuring traceability and performance. Key specifications include:

  • MP35N – UNS R30035: a Co-35Ni-20Cr-10Mo multiphase alloy. ASTM F562 covers it in bar and wire; ISO 5832-6 is the wrought Co-Ni-Cr-Mo equivalent. This is the grade used for downhole springs and fasteners.
  • CoCrMo – UNS R31537, W.Nr 2.4979 (CoCr28Mo6): a Co-28Cr-6Mo alloy. ASTM F1537 covers it in bar, rod and wire; ISO 5832-12 is the wrought equivalent.
  • Elgiloy – UNS R30003: a 40Co-20Cr-16Fe-15Ni-7Mo alloy. ASTM F1058 covers it in wire, strip and bar, alongside AMS 5833. This is the spring and fatigue grade.
  • Additional: AMS 5796 covers Haynes® 25 (L-605) bare welding rods and wire – a welding consumable, not a structural wire; ISO 5832-12 covers wrought Co-28Cr-6Mo (2.4979) for medical applications.

Each standard sets its own mechanical limits for its own grade and condition – they are not a single set of figures, and MP35N in the cold-worked and aged condition is far stronger than the annealed limits that apply to the other two. Tell us the grade, diameter and temper you need and we will quote against the acceptance limits of the governing standard, certified via independent testing labs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Which cobalt alloy wire grade should I ask for?

It depends on the duty. MP35N (UNS R30035, ASTM F562) is the ultra-high-strength choice for downhole springs, fasteners and valve components in sour service. Elgiloy (UNS R30003, ASTM F1058 / AMS 5833) is the fatigue and spring grade. CoCrMo (UNS R31537, ASTM F1537) is the wear and biocompatibility grade. All three resist galling and chloride attack far better than stainless; tell us the application and we will point you at the right one.

How does Cobalt Alloy Wire compare to Inconel?

MP35N (R30035) offers better abrasion and galling resistance than Inconel 625, especially in slurries or valves. Inconel edges in pure oxidation; cobalt wins combined wear-corrosion. Choose based on service rather than on a single headline number.

What are the standard sizes for Cobalt Alloy Wire?

Available from 0.5mm to 20mm diameter in straight lengths or coils up to 500kg. All three grades support custom draws; 1.6mm is the common size for GTAW filler. We supply annealed or spring temper, with tolerances per whichever standard governs the grade you order – F562 for MP35N, F1537 for CoCrMo, F1058 / AMS 5833 for Elgiloy.

Is Cobalt Alloy Wire suitable for sour gas service?

Yes, and two of the three grades are named for it by their own mills. MP35N (UNS R30035) is included in NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 as a sulfide-stress-cracking-resistant material for sour gas and oil well service; the listing is conditional on hardness, and Carpenter Technology states a maximum of 35 HRC, or 51 HRC in specific cold-reduced-plus-aged conditions, so material harder than that falls outside it. Elgiloy (UNS R30003) carries NACE MR0175 and MR0103 on Elgiloy Specialty Metals' own strip and foil specification list, alongside ASTM F1058 and ISO 5832-7. Tell us the service conditions and we will confirm the grade and temper to order.

What certifications come with Cobalt Alloy Wire?

Expect EN 10204 3.1 mill certs, PMI, UT, and hardness tests per the standard governing the grade you order. ISO 9001 suppliers provide full traceability, PED compliance for EU, and biocompatibility docs for pharma. Ensures procurement confidence for global projects.
