Specifications and Standards for Hastelloy Wire

Hastelloy Wire meets global specifications for extreme corrosion service:

  • UNS N10276: Nickel alloy optimized for wet chlorine, hypochlorite, and mixed acids.
  • ASTM B575: Plate/sheet/wire specification; B574 for bars.
  • DIN 2.4819 / EN 2.4819: NiMo16Cr15W for European chemical plants.
  • Additional: ASME SB-575, NACE MR0103/MR0175 for sour service.

Standards require solution annealing at 2100°F with rapid cooling to restore corrosion resistance post-welding.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Hastelloy C276 wire used for?

Hastelloy C276 wire (UNS N10276) serves weld overlays, springs, and anodes in chemical reactors, flue gas desulfurization, and pharma vessels. ASTM B575 specs ensure resistance to mixed acids, chlorides where stainless/Inconel fail completely. Fabricators value its crack-resistant welds on carbon steel. (58 words)

How does Hastelloy C276 compare to Inconel 625?

C276 excels in reducing acids (HCl, H2SO4) where 625 corrodes rapidly, though 625 handles higher oxidation. C276's 16% Mo content provides broader pH resistance 0-14. Corrosion data shows C276 at 0.1 MPY vs 625's 25+ MPY in 93% H2SO4. (55 words)

What welding processes use Hastelloy wire?

GTAW (TIG), PAW, GMAW (MIG) with matching ERNiCrMo-3 filler. Post-weld solution anneal at 2100°F mandatory. No preheat required; interpass < 200°F. Produces ductile overlays with corrosion matching base metal per ASTM B575 chemistry control. (51 words)

Is Hastelloy wire suitable for sour service?

Yes, UNS N10276 meets NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 hardness limits (≤ 90 HRB). Proven in H2S + CO2 environments to 30,000 psi. Preferred over duplex stainless for deep sour gas wells due to superior pitting resistance. (48 words)

What certifications accompany Hastelloy wire?

EN 10204 3.1/3.2 mill certs, PMI analysis, solution anneal verification, IGC testing per ASTM A262 Practice E. Full chemistry, mechanical properties, NACE compliance docs. Traceability from VIM melt to spool ensures procurement confidence. (50 words)
