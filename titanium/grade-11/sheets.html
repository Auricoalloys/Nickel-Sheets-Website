Titanium Grade 11 (UNS R52250), ASTM B265 certified, is commercially pure titanium with a small palladium addition, combining the corrosion resistance of Grade 1 with markedly better performance in reducing acids. It keeps the formability and weldability of unalloyed titanium and corrosion resistance in oxidizing and mildly reducing media such as seawater and process chemicals.
