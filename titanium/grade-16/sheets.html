Titanium Grade 16 (UNS R58152), certified to ASTM B265, is a nitrogen-strengthened beta titanium alloy offering exceptional corrosion resistance, mechanical strength, and excellent weldability. It combines superior resistance to chloride stress corrosion cracking, crevice corrosion, and oxidation, making it suitable for highly aggressive chemical environments and marine applications exceeding the capabilities of commercially pure titanium and many stainless steels.
