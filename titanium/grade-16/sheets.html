Titanium Grade 16 (UNS R52402), certified to ASTM B265, is commercially pure titanium with a small palladium addition, offering the strength of Grade 2 with far better resistance to reducing acids, and the same excellent weldability. It combines superior resistance to chloride stress corrosion cracking, crevice corrosion, and oxidation, making it suitable for highly aggressive chemical environments and marine applications exceeding the capabilities of commercially pure titanium and many stainless steels.
