Titanium Grade 16 (Ti-0.05Pd, UNS R52402) represents a strategic evolution in titanium technology, delivering Grade 7-level corrosion resistance at significantly lower cost. The precise 0.04-0.08% palladium addition creates a synergistic effect that dramatically accelerates oxide film repair in reducing acids, chlorides, and crevice environments critical for chemical reactors, desalination condensers, and subsea oil & gas valves. Unlike standard CP titanium (Grade 2), Grade 16 maintains full pitting resistance equivalent number (PREN) performance in sulfuric, hydrochloric, and wet chlorine service up to 80°C.
