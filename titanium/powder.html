Titanium Grade 2 Powder

Commercially pure titanium, unalloyed. The most formable and weldable of the CP grades, with the corrosion resistance of titanium at a lower cost than Ti-6Al-4V, and biocompatible for implant work.

Applications: Chemical process equipment, heat exchangers, marine hardware, anodes and non-load-bearing medical components.

Titanium Grade 5 Powder (Ti-6Al-4V)

The most used titanium alloy in manufacturing, and the reason printed titanium parts exist at all: roughly the strength of steel at 60% of the weight.

Applications: Aerospace structure and brackets, motorsport components, marine hardware and industrial tooling.

Should I use Titanium Grade 5 or Grade 23 powder?

They share a composition; Grade 23 simply holds oxygen, iron and carbon to tighter limits. Grade 5 suits aerospace and industrial parts where strength governs. Grade 23 is for implants and damage-tolerant parts, because the lower interstitials buy fracture toughness and ductility.
