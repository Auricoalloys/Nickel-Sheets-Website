A coastal acid‑manufacturing plant had already moved its scrubber shells and ducts from 316L to commercially pure titanium, yet every annual shutdown the inspection reports came back with the same bad news: deep crevice attack and local thinning in the tight gasket faces of the hydrochloric acid absorber and hot brine headers. When the corrosion team rebuilt the most critical liners, tube sheets and flange rings in Titanium Grade 11 Plates (Ti‑0.15Pd, UNS R52250), corrosion logs showed that where plain titanium had suffered measurable loss, the palladium‑modified plates remained essentially intact in reducing acids and chloride‑rich pockets, and after several campaigns the plant had cut unplanned repairs in those circuits to almost zero.

Titanium Grade 11 is a commercially pure titanium alloyed with a small addition of palladium—typically 0.12–0.25% by weight—designated Ti‑0.15Pd and classified under UNS R52250. This grade retains the very high ductility, impact toughness and useful strength of low‑strength CP titanium (similar to Grade 1 and Grade 2), but the noble‑metal palladium addition promotes rapid repassivation of the oxide film, giving significantly improved resistance to reducing acids and to crevice and pitting corrosion in chloride‑rich media.

For procurement heads, corrosion engineers, fabricators, OEMs and hardware or industrial supply stores serving chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma and power generation, Titanium Grade 11 plates offer a way to keep the formability and weldability of very soft CP titanium while opening up applications where Grade 2 alone is marginal. Industry data on titanium in water and seawater show that unalloyed grades already provide negligible corrosion rates in most natural waters and steam, and work comparing titanium alloys notes that palladium‑bearing grades such as 7 and 11 push useful ranges in hydrochloric and phosphoric acids much further than non‑alloyed grades, especially in crevices and at higher temperatures.

Applications & Benefits

  • Chemical & Oil & Gas: Titanium Grade 11 plates are used in scrubbers, absorbers, column internals, tube sheets, channel covers and linings in systems handling hydrochloric, sulphuric and mixed acids, where Pd‑modified titanium outperforms Grade 2 and many stainless steels in crevices and reducing conditions.
  • Marine & Power Generation: In hot seawater, brine, flue‑gas desulfurization and once‑through cooling circuits, Ti‑0.15Pd plate maintains negligible corrosion and resists crevice attack at higher chloride contents and temperatures than unalloyed titanium, extending life of tube sheets, waterboxes and structural plates.
  • Pharma & High‑Purity Plant: Grade 11 is selected for high‑purity systems, waste‑treatment and scrubber equipment where reducing acid cleaning cycles, chlorides and tight crevices must be handled without compromising low contamination or weldability.
