Titanium Grade 12 (UNS R53400), with ASTM B265 certification, refines commercially pure titanium’s corrosion resistance with the addition of palladium, a known performance enhancer against reducing acids such as phosphoric and sulfuric acid, and in marine environments. Its balanced chemical composition offers enhanced resistance to crevice corrosion, pitting, and stress corrosion cracking, making it a preferred choice for highly corrosive and critical applications.
