Titanium Grade 16 UNS R52402 ASTM B265

Titanium Grade 16 plates (Ti‑0.05Pd, UNS R52402) combine the strength and ductility of Grade 2 titanium with palladium-boosted resistance to chlorides, acids and seawater.

Key Properties

  • Palladium 0.04-0.08% — roughly a third of Grade 7's 0.12-0.25%, which is what brings the cost down.
  • Grade 2's base chemistry, oxygen 0.25% and iron 0.30% maximum.
  • Most of Grade 7's crevice resistance for a fraction of the palladium.
  • Specified where Grade 7 is justified on performance but not on price.

Applications

  • Crevice-prone chemical process equipment
  • Hot chloride and brine service
  • Heat exchanger tubing and tubesheets

TIMET states that the 0.05% palladium grades, Grade 16 among them, show nearly identical corrosion resistance to Grade 7, so Grade 7’s reducing-acid limits in our acid and alkali service guide apply to this grade too.
