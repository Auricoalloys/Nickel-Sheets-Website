Titanium Grade 6

Aurico Alloys LLP Titanium Grade 6 round bars (UNS R54520, ASTM B348) Ti-5Al-2.5Sn alloy round bars for aircraft gas turbine components, high-temperature steam turbine blades.

Key Properties

  • Aluminium 4.0-6.0% with tin 2.0-3.0% — an alpha alloy, so it is not heat treatable but keeps strength at temperature.
  • Good weldability and stability, both consequences of the single-phase alpha structure.
  • Retains toughness at cryogenic temperature, which is where the low-interstitial version is used.
  • Oxygen 0.20% and iron 0.50% maximum.

Applications

  • Aircraft engine casings and rings
  • Cryogenic vessels and tankage
  • Airframe components at elevated temperature

Available Forms

  • Titanium Grade 6 Foil
  • Titanium Grade 6 Round Bars
  • Titanium Grade 6 Plates
