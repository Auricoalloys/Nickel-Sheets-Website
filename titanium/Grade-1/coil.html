Aurico Alloys LLP supplies titanium grade 1 coil (R50250, Werkstoff 3.7025) from ready stock in Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

Coil is the most economical way to buy sheet-gauge material when parts are blanked, roll-formed or slit to strip, because it removes the cut-length premium and keeps the surface consistent along the whole run.

Titanium Grade 1 is the most formable titanium grade, so it takes deep draws and tight bends that Grade 2 will crack on.

Chemical Composition

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Minimum tensile strength 240 MPa with elongation of 24% or more, the softest and most formable of the CP grades. Density is 4.51 g/cm3.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on the specification we can add positive material identification, ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM G28 or A262, and third-party inspection release from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.

Aurico Alloys LLP operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system, and can certify material to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 for sour service where the grade permits.
