Titanium Grade 3 (UNS R50430), certified under ASTM B265, strikes a balance between purity and strength, offering better tensile and yield strength compared to pure titanium Grade 1 and 2 while maintaining excellent corrosion resistance. It is particularly favored for applications involving oxidizing and mildly reducing chemicals, high temperatures, and where weldability and formability are essential.
