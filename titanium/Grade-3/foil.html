Titanium Grade 3 foil (UNS R50550, ASTM B265) stands out as a commercially pure titanium with elevated oxygen content, offering 20-30% higher yield strength than Grade 2 typically 380 MPa vs 275-345 MPa while retaining excellent formability and corrosion resistance in seawater, acids, and caustics . Available in thin gauges from 0.1mm to 1mm, it's perfect for high-pressure chemical reactor liners, marine heat exchangers, and pharma distillation components where Grade 2 deforms under load .

Unlike softer Grade 1 or 2 foils prone to distortion in pressurized systems, Grade 3 provides superior resilience: tensile strength ≥450 MPa, elongation ≥18%, and density of 4.51 g/cm³ for lightweight efficiency .
