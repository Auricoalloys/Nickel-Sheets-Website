    Material Identity and Standards

    Titanium Grade 5 is the widely used alpha-beta titanium alloy commonly known as Ti-6Al-4V. In international specifications, it is designated as UNS R56400, commonly supplied as round bar to ASTM B348 Grade 5, and cross-referenced to DIN TiAl6V4 and EN/W. Nr. 3.7165. It is recognized across aerospace, offshore, power, and industrial engineering because it combines low density with high mechanical performance and useful corrosion resistance in many service environments.

    Why Industries Choose Titanium Grade 5

    Chemical processing

    Chemical processors use Titanium Grade 5 round bar for components where weight reduction, strength, and good resistance in selected corrosive media are important. It is especially useful when a plant needs stronger engineered bar stock than commercially pure titanium, while still retaining good corrosion performance in many oxidizing environments.

    Oil and gas

    In offshore and subsea service, Grade 5 is valued for its excellent strength-to-weight ratio and resistance in many marine and offshore environments. This helps reduce load in critical assemblies while supporting long service life in demanding conditions where conventional steels may create handling, corrosion, or maintenance penalties.

    Marine

    Marine equipment benefits from Titanium Grade 5 because salt-bearing environments quickly expose weaknesses in many common alloys. Grade 5 provides a combination of mechanical strength and corrosion resistance that makes it suitable for shafts, fasteners, connectors, and fabricated hardware where reliability matters.

    Pharma and clean-process equipment

    Pharmaceutical and high-purity systems often require materials that support clean fabrication, dimensional stability, and dependable service. Grade 5 is considered where mechanical performance must exceed lower-strength grades, especially for custom machined parts, support structures, and precision OEM hardware.

    Power generation and OEM manufacturing

    Power generation and OEM manufacturers use Titanium Grade 5 for highly stressed components because it offers strong mechanical properties with lower mass than many competitive alloys. That balance is useful in equipment where rotating performance, thermal exposure, structural efficiency, or installation weight directly affect lifecycle cost.

    Comparison with Common Grades

    Buyers often compare Titanium Grade 5 with Grade 2 titanium and stainless steels such as 316/316L. The decision usually comes down to whether the application prioritizes maximum corrosion resistance, lower raw material cost, or a stronger strength-to-weight balance.

    FAQs

    What is Titanium Grade 5 Round Bar?

    Titanium Grade 5 Round Bar is a titanium alloy bar made from Ti-6Al-4V, identified as UNS R56400 and commonly supplied to ASTM B348. It is known for combining high strength, low density, and reliable corrosion resistance, making it one of the most specified titanium bar products for industrial, offshore, OEM, and precision engineering use.

    What standards apply to Grade 5 titanium round bar?

    The most common specification for round bar is ASTM B348 Grade 5. International buyers also recognize the alloy under UNS R56400, DIN TiAl6V4, and EN 3.7165. Using these standard references helps engineering, procurement, and quality teams align drawings, inspection documents, and cross-border sourcing requirements more efficiently.

    Why choose Grade 5 instead of commercially pure titanium?

    Grade 5 is usually selected when the application needs much higher strength than commercially pure titanium grades can provide. While CP titanium may offer excellent corrosion resistance and formability, Grade 5 gives a stronger performance profile for load-bearing shafts, machined components, structural hardware, and engineered assemblies where weight reduction also matters.

    Is Titanium Grade 5 suitable for marine and offshore service?

    Yes, Titanium Grade 5 is frequently used in marine and offshore equipment because it combines strong mechanical properties with excellent resistance in many marine and offshore environments. It is often considered for high-performance parts where corrosion, weight, and long-term reliability all influence the final material selection.

    What should buyers check before ordering Titanium Grade 5 Round Bar?

    Buyers should confirm standard, size range, tolerance, quantity, end use, certification, and inspection expectations before placing an order. It is also important to ask for mill test certificates, traceability, bar condition, cut length requirements, export packing readiness, and whether machining or project-specific testing support is needed for the application.
