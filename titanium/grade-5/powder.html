Titanium Grade 5 Powder (Ti-6Al-4V) Supplier and Exporter

Titanium Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) powder (R56400) is a plasma atomised or plasma rotating electrode, for sphericity and low satellite content alpha-beta titanium alloy feedstock for additive manufacturing, supplied to ASTM F2924 (PBF) in 5-25 µm, 15-45 µm, 15-53 µm, 45-105 µm, 53-105 µm, 45-150 µm, 53-150 µm size fractions.

The most used titanium alloy in manufacturing, and the reason printed titanium parts exist at all: roughly the strength of steel at 60% of the weight.

What Properties Does Printed Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 Reach?

Printed properties depend on the heat treatment, not just the alloy, so they are given per condition.

As-built titanium is strong but low in ductility, because of the martensitic alpha-prime structure. Stress relief converts it; HIP additionally closes porosity for fatigue-critical parts.

Should I use Titanium Grade 5 or Grade 23 powder?

They share a composition; Grade 23 simply holds oxygen, iron and carbon to tighter limits. Grade 5 suits aerospace and industrial parts where strength governs. Grade 23 is for implants and damage-tolerant parts, because the lower interstitials buy fracture toughness and ductility.
