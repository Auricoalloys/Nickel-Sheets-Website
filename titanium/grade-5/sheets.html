Titanium Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) UNS R56400, ASTM B265 is renowned for its combination of high strength, low density, and superior corrosion resistance. Its unique 6% aluminum and 4% vanadium alloying contribute to outstanding weldability, fatigue resistance, and durability at extreme temperatures. Where grades 1-4 might be chosen for corrosion alone, Grade 5’s boosted mechanical performance enables thinner designs, lighter components, and higher pressure ratings.

Supplied with mill certificates, RoHS, REACH, and global standards compliance, Titanium Grade 5 Sheets from Aurico Alloys LLP guarantee reliability and flexibility.
