    Titanium Grade 6 (UNS R54520), an alpha titanium alloy with 5% aluminum and 2.5% tin, delivers superior creep resistance up to 425°C and oxidation resistance to 650°C, outperforming Ti Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) in long-term thermal stability without heat treatment needs. Unlike beta alloys prone to embrittlement, its stable microstructure ensures weldability and fatigue strength in aircraft gas turbine components and power plant exhaust systems.
