Ti-6Al-7Nb (ATI 6-7, UNS R56700) is an alpha-beta titanium alloy developed for medical and surgical implants, with properties close to Ti-6Al-4V but substituting niobium for vanadium. Specified to ASTM F1295 and ISO 5832-11, it has been in clinical use since 1986 and is widely used for orthopaedic implants: total hip-replacement stems, fracture-fixation plates, intramedullary rods and nails, spinal devices, screws and wires. Its elastic modulus is about 105 GPa in the solution-annealed condition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Ti-6Al-7Nb replaces vanadium with niobium for use in the body, keeping mechanical properties close to Ti-6Al-4V while removing vanadium from the implant. It is specified to ASTM F1295 and ISO 5832-11.

To ASTM F1295, annealed bar has a minimum tensile strength of 900 MPa, a minimum 0.2% yield of 800 MPa, 10% minimum elongation and 25% minimum reduction of area.
