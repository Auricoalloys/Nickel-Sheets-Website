Key Properties

  • Oxygen 0.18% and iron 0.20% maximum — the lowest of the commercially pure grades, and the reason it is the most formable.
  • Oxygen is the strengthener in CP titanium. Grades 1 to 4 are the same metal with progressively more of it: 0.18, 0.25, 0.35 and 0.40%.
  • Deep drawing and severe forming are what it is chosen for, not strength.
  • Grade 11 is Grade 1 with palladium added for crevice corrosion resistance.

Applications

  • Deep-drawn and severely formed components
  • Anodes and electrolytic cell hardware
  • Heat exchanger plates and tubing
  • Chemical process linings and vessels
