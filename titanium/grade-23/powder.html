Titanium Grade 23 Powder (Ti-6Al-4V ELI) Supplier and Exporter

Titanium Grade 23 (Ti-6Al-4V ELI) powder (R56407) is a plasma atomised or plasma rotating electrode alpha-beta titanium alloy, extra low interstitial feedstock for additive manufacturing, supplied to ASTM F3001 (PBF) in 5-25 µm, 15-53 µm, 45-105 µm, 45-150 µm, 53-150 µm size fractions.

Those tighter interstitial limits buy fracture toughness and ductility, which is what makes it the implant grade rather than Grade 5.

Should I use Titanium Grade 23 or Grade 5 powder?

Grade 23 is the extra-low-interstitial version of Grade 5 and is what implant standards call for: orthopaedic and spinal devices, trauma fixation, fracture-critical structure. Where the part is not implanted and strength matters more than toughness, Grade 5 is cheaper and slightly stronger.
