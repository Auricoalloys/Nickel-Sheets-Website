Titanium Grade 23 (UNS R56407), ASTM B265 and ISO 5832-3 certified, is the Extra Low Interstitial (ELI) variant of Ti-6Al-4V alloy, offering superior fracture toughness and lower oxygen and nitrogen levels compared to Grade 5. Its enhanced purity makes it ideal for biomedical applications and critical aerospace components, while also serving demanding industrial processes requiring high strength, low weight, and excellent corrosion resistance.
