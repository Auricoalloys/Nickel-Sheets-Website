Titanium Grade 23 (Ti-6Al-4V ELI, UNS R56407) is the extra-low interstitial (ELI) version of Grade 5, with reduced oxygen (max 0.13%) and iron for superior ductility and fracture toughness. ASTM F136 and ASTM B265 certified, it excels in biocompatible surgical staples, mesh, dental implants, and orthodontic components where notch sensitivity could spell failure. Unlike standard Grade 5, Grade 23 maintains impact strength down to -253°C, making it ideal for cryogenic pressure vessels and liquefied gas storage in power generation and oil & gas.
