Titanium Grade 7 (UNS R52404), ASTM B265 certified, enhances commercially pure titanium’s excellent corrosion resistance with a minute addition of palladium, drastically improving performance in aggressive reducing media including strong acids and marine environments. It is highly resistant to crevice corrosion, pitting, and stress corrosion cracking, outperforming typical titanium grades in demanding conditions.
