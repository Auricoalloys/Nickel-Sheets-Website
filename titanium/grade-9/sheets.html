Titanium Grade 9 (Ti-3Al-2.5V, UNS R56320) sits between commercially pure and high-alloyed titanium grades, pairing excellent weldability and formability with impressive mechanical strength. Unlike Grades 2 or 5, Grade 9 provides engineered balance: higher pressure ratings and fatigue resistance than CP grades, yet still easy to fabricate and much lighter than stainless steel alternatives. Specified for heat exchangers, tubing, and structures, it's an industry favorite for environments that punish weaker metals.

Every Aurico Alloys LLP Titanium Grade 9 Sheet is delivered with full ASTM B265, UNS R56320 certification and mill test reports, providing assurance for procurement heads, engineers, fabricators, and hardware supply stores.
