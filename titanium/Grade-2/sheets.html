Titanium Grade 2 (UNS R50400, ASTM B265) balances exceptional corrosion resistance, moderate strength, and everyday fabricability. Its commercially pure composition means fewer impurities, lightweight construction, and a proven record resisting oxidizing/chloride-rich media where stainless steels and basic alloys routinely fail. Many industries use Titanium Grade 2 as the workhorse of all commercially pure titanium grades for pressure vessels, exchangers, marine hardware, and more.
