What makes Monel 400 hollow bars ideal for chemical processing?

Can Monel hollow bars handle marine and oil & gas environments?

Yes, Monel 400 hollow bars resist seawater biofouling and H2S sour service per NACE MR0175. Used in riser pipes and pump barrels, they offer 8-10 year life vs. 2 years for duplex SS, vital for procurement in offshore operations. (47 words)

What are standard sizes for Monel hollow bars?

How does Monel compare to titanium for power generation?
