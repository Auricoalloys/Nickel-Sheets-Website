Frequently Asked Questions

What standards for special stainless hollow bars?

ASTM A276/A479 (S31803 F51), DIN 1.4462 (2205), EN 10088 tolerances. NACE for sour service; Sanmac® machining optimized. Verify ferrite 35-65%.

Properties of duplex 2205 hollow bars?

Yield 450 MPa, tensile 620-860 MPa, 25% elong., PREN 35. Density 7.8 g/cm³; 2x strength 316L. 40% faster machining.

Why duplex hollow bars for oil & gas?

SCC/pitting resistance in H2S/chlorides; thinner walls save weight. Near-net-shape cuts waste 40% vs solid.

Size range for stainless hollow bars?

OD 32-420mm, ID +0/-2%; 2205, 254SMO stock. Duplex hex bars for solid; custom walls.
