How does Haynes 230 compare to Inconel 617 oxidation resistance?

230 loses 0.15 vs 617's 0.85 mg/cm² after 500h at 2000°F due to La additions improving Cr₂O₃ adherence. Haynes maintains 25 ksi strength where 617 softens to 18 ksi. Cyclic tests show 230 surviving 2000 cycles vs 617's 800 before spallation.

What heat treatment produces optimal Haynes hollow bars?

Solution anneal 2150°F (1175°C)/1h WQ + stabilize 1825°F (1000°C)/1h AC. Critical second stabilization develops beneficial M₆C/M₂₃C₆ carbides. AMS 5878 requires Class 2 furnace uniformity ±15°F, preventing sigma phase embrittlement. Dual cycle essential for cyclic oxidation resistance.
