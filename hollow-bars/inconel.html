Specifications and Standards for Inconel Hollow Bars

Inconel Hollow Bars meet stringent international specs for seamless, high-integrity components:

  • UNS N06625: Nickel alloy with optimized Mo/Nb for pitting and crevice corrosion resistance.
  • ASTM B564: forged or rolled bars, with B446 for rod and bar and B444 for seamless pipe and tube equivalents.
  • DIN 2.4856 / EN 2.4856: NiCr22Mo9Nb for European pressure vessels.
  • Others: ASME SB-564, NACE MR0175 for sour service.

Standards mandate hydrostatic testing to 10,000 psi and grain size control for fatigue resistance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are Inconel Hollow Bars used for?

Inconel Hollow Bars (UNS N06625) serve as seamless tubing substitutes in heat exchangers, subsea risers, and pharma vessels. ASTM B564 specs ensure pressure integrity up to 10,000 psi, with superior resistance to pitting in chlorides and acids chosen over welded pipe for zero-defect reliability in oil & gas and chemical apps.

How do Inconel 625 Hollow Bars compare to Hastelloy?

Inconel 625 offers broader corrosion resistance (oxidizing/reducing) at lower cost than Hastelloy C276, which excels in strong acids. Both handle 1800°F, but Inconel machines easier for hollow forms. Industry data shows 20% savings in marine/power use without sacrificing longevity.

What sizes are available for Inconel Hollow Bars?

Standard OD 20–300mm, ID 10–250mm, walls 3–20mm, lengths to 12m. Custom per ASTM B564/B751; bright-drawn or annealed. Ideal for fabricators needing trepanned or HF acid hollows—stock from Mumbai exporters supports urgent OEM needs. (52 words)

Are Inconel Hollow Bars NACE compliant?

Yes, UNS N06625 meets NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for sour service in oil & gas. Hardness ≤ 35 HRC prevents SSC; tested in H2S up to 30% at high pressure. Preferred for flowlines over duplex SS.

What tests are performed on Inconel Hollow Bars?

PMI, ultrasonic (ASTM A388), hydrostatic (B564), grain size, and intergranular corrosion (ASTM A262). Full 3.2 certs include chemistry and mechanicals, ensuring compliance for PED/ASME code vessels. Critical for procurement in pharma/power.
