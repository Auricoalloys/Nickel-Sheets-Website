Specifications and Standards for Duplex Steel Hollow Bars

Duplex Steel Hollow Bars adhere to rigorous global standards, ensuring consistency for OEM manufacturers and fabricators. Key specs include:

  • ASTM: A182 F51 (UNS S31803), F60 (UNS S32205), A479, A789 for seamless/welded forms.
  • UNS: S31803 (2205), S32205 (super duplex).
  • DIN/EN: 1.4462 (X2CrNiMoN22-5-3), 1.4410 (X2CrNiMoN25-7-4).
  • Dimensions: OD 20-300mm, ID 10-250mm, lengths up to 12m.
  • Certifications: ISO 9001, PED 2014/68/EU compliant, with NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for sour service.

These standards guarantee material traceability, vital for procurement heads in power generation and industrial supply stores.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are the key standards for Duplex Steel Hollow Bars?

Duplex hollow bars conform to ASTM A182 F51/F60 (UNS S31803/S32205), A479, and EN 1.4462. These cover seamless forms for pressures up to 10,000 psi, with NACE MR0175 for oilfield use. Ideal for chemical and marine buyers seeking certified stock.

How do Duplex Hollow Bars compare to 316 Stainless in corrosion resistance?

Duplex (PREN 35-40) resists pitting 4x better than 316L (PREN 24) in chlorides, with higher strength (450 MPa yield vs 205 MPa). Perfect for oil & gas, saving 30-50% on lifecycle costs versus frequent replacements.

Are Duplex Steel Hollow Bars available in custom sizes?

Yes, from OD 20-300mm and custom IDs/walls, in seamless or welded. Annealed and pickled finishes ready for machining. Mumbai suppliers offer quick exports with PMI testing for OEMs and fabricators.

What industries benefit most from Duplex Hollow Bars?

Chemical processing, oil & gas, marine, pharma, and power gen choose Duplex for its balance of strength, corrosion resistance, and cost. Stats show 25% of offshore platforms now use Duplex grades.
