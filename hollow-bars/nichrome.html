Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are Nichrome hollow bars used for?

Nichrome 80/20 hollow bars (UNS N06004) build furnace muffles, retorts, pyrolysis tubes serving to 2200°F where stainless rapidly oxidizes. ASTM B344 specs ensure reliable scale formation protects base metal 36,000+ hours continuous. Cost-effective for OEM furnace manufacturers vs. expensive superalloys.

How does Nichrome compare to stainless 310 for furnaces?

Nichrome oxidizes at 0.8 vs 310's 4.2 mg/ft²/hr at 2000°F, delivering 4.5x service life. Maintains 18 ksi strength where 310 falls to 12 ksi. Nichrome's Ni-rich matrix prevents chromium depletion; 310 requires frequent replacement in continuous service.

What is the maximum temperature for Nichrome hollow bars?

2200°F (1205°C) continuous in oxidizing atmospheres; intermittent to 2300°F. ASTM B344 defines safe limits based on 0.5% creep in 10,000 hours. Excellent for most industrial heat treating; superalloys required only above 2400°F or reducing conditions.

Can Nichrome hollow bars be welded?

Yes, GTAW with matching NiCr 80/20 filler using full argon shielding. No preheat required; interpass <400°F. Post-weld anneal 2050°F recommended for heavy sections. Excellent weld ductility matches base metal; preferred over 310SS prone to hot cracking.

What quality documentation ships with Nichrome hollow bars?

EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates include chemistry analysis, annealing verification, tensile properties per ASTM B344, oxide scale thickness measurement. Heat number traceability from AOD melt to finished bar ensures full accountability for furnace-critical applications.
