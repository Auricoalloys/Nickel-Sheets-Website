During deepwater oil exploration, a Ti-6Al-4V OCTG string experienced crevice corrosion in chloride-rich brine, leading to localized pitting and operational failure issues plaguing alloys without stable passivation. Titanium hollow bars excel here with self-healing oxide films and superior strength-to-weight for subsea and chemical demands.

Specifications and Standards

Titanium hollow bars meet ASTM B348/ASME SB348 (bars/billets), B861 (seamless pipe extended), AMS 4928, UNS R50400 (Gr2), R56407 (Gr5), DIN 3.7035 (Gr2), 3.7164 (Gr5), EN 10204-3.1 certs. OD 20-300mm, ID 10-250mm, tolerances per ASTM for machining stock.

NACE MR0175 compliant for sour service in oil & gas.

Product Forms and Availability

Hollow (trepanned/extruded), seamless pipe forms; OD/ID 20x10 to 300x250mm, straights 2-6m. Annealed ASTM 5 alpha structure. Nickel alloy hollow bars complement for higher-temp corrosion.

Chemical Composition

Gr2: Ti bal., O 0.25% max, Fe 0.30% max, C 0.08% max. Gr5: Al 5.5-6.75%, V 3.5-4.5%, O 0.20% max, Fe 0.40% max. Low interstitials ensure ductility.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Gr5: Tensile 1170 MPa, yield 1100 MPa, 10% elong., hardness 379 HB, modulus 114 GPa. Density 4.43 g/cm³ (60% steel), CTE 8.6 µm/m°C, cond. 6.7 W/mK. Hollow cuts weight 40% vs solid.

Bio-compatible Gr23 variant boosts fatigue toughness.

Frequently Asked Questions

What standards for titanium hollow bars?

ASTM B348 (Gr2 R50400, Gr5 R56407), DIN 3.7035/3.7164, AMS 4928. Cover bars/billets; tolerances for machining. NACE for sour gas.

Properties of Ti-6Al-4V Gr5 hollow bars?

Tensile 1170 MPa, yield 1100 MPa, 10% elong., density 4.43 g/cm³, modulus 114 GPa. 60% lighter than steel; oxide passivation.

Why titanium hollow bars for oil & gas?

Resist crevice/SCC in brine/H2S; hollow saves 40% weight/machining. Beats SS316 in marine; Gr5 strength rivals alloys.

Sizes of titanium alloy hollow bars?

OD 20-300mm, ID 10-250mm, 2-6m lengths. Gr2/5 stock; titanium hex bars for solid. Trepanned seamless.
