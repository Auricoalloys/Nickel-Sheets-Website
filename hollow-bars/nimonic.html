Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are Nimonic hollow bars used for?

Nimonic 80A hollow bars (UNS N07080) build high-pressure turbine shafts, gas turbine casings, pyrolysis tubes. ASTM B637 specs deliver exceptional creep resistance at 1300°F where Inconel 718 deforms 5x faster. OEMs specify for rotating components requiring dimensional stability 30,000+ hours.

How does Nimonic 80A compare to Inconel 718?

80A offers superior creep rupture life (100h vs 20h at 1300°F/50ksi) due to optimized γ' phase, though 718 welds are easier. Nimonic preferred for static high-temp shafts; 718 for complex fabrications. Both excellent oxidation resistance through 1600°F.

What heat treatment is required for Nimonic hollow bars?

Solution 1080°C (1975°F)/8h WQ + precipitation 700°C (1290°F)/16h FC. Critical stabilization 900°C prevents σ-phase embrittlement. ASTM B637 mandates furnace class 3 uniformity, delta 14C verification. Age cycle builds 15% γ' volume for creep strength.

What sizes are available for Nimonic hollow bars?

Standard OD 30-400mm, ID 20-350mm, walls 5-30mm from forged/trepanned stock. Custom heat treated per ASTM B637. Aerospace tolerances ±0.5mm; power gen ±1mm typical. Mumbai precision suppliers stock common turbine shaft dimensions.

What testing certifies Nimonic hollow bars?

100% ultrasonic AMS 2631 Level 3, tensile/creep per AMS 2371, metallography ASTM E112 grain size, γ' extraction analysis. EN 10204 3.2 includes heat treatment charts, chemistry certificate, non-conformance zero acceptance. Full traceability melt-to-part.
