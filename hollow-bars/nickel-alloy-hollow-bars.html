In a North Sea oil platform, a standard carbon steel hollow bar valve body suffered stress corrosion cracking from chloride-laden brine, fracturing under pressure and causing costly shutdown failures common in 20% of oilfield tubular incidents. Nickel alloy hollow bars overcome this with inherent corrosion resistance and machining efficiency for critical components.

Specifications and Standards

Nickel alloy hollow bars conform to ASTM B446 (Inconel 625), B425 (Incoloy 825), AMS 5666, UNS N06625 (625), N08825 (825), DIN/Werkstoff 2.4856 (625), 2.4858 (825), EN 10294-1 adapted for alloys. OD 32-250mm, ID 16-200mm, tolerances H8-H11 for free-machining.

These standards support NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for sour service in oil & gas.

Product Forms and Availability

Hollow bars (trepanned or extruded), alongside solid hex bars for hybrid designs; sizes OD/ID 32x16 to 250x200mm, lengths 3-6m. Annealed for optimal machinability. Inconel hex bars provide solid alternatives for high-stress fittings.

Chemical Composition

Inconel 625: Ni 58% min, Cr 20-23%, Mo 8-10%, Nb+Ta 3.15-4.15%, Fe 5% max. Incoloy 825: Ni 38-46%, Cr 19.5-23.5%, Mo 2.5-3.5%, Cu 1.5-3%, Ti 0.6-1.2%. Tight controls ensure pitting resistance.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Inconel 625 annealed: Tensile 827 MPa, yield 414 MPa, 30% elong., hardness ≤287 HB. Density 8.44 g/cm³, thermal cond. 9.8 W/m·K. Hollow design reduces weight 30-50% vs solid while retaining stiffness.

Superior to carbon steel in SCC-prone environments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What standards apply to nickel alloy hollow bars?

ASTM B446 (Inconel 625 UNS N06625), B425 (825 N08825), DIN 2.4856; EN 10294-1 for dims. Ensure corrosion resistance via PMI/MTRs for oil & gas. Tolerances H8-H11.

Properties of Inconel 625 hollow bars?

Tensile 827 MPa, yield 414 MPa, 30% elong.; density 8.44 g/cm³. Hollow reduces machining time 40%, weight vs solid. PREN 51 for pitting.

Advantages of hollow over solid bars?

Superior machinability (longer chips), 30-50% weight reduction, near-net-shape for valves/shafts. Cost savings in high-volume fab; retains strength.

Available sizes for nickel hollow bars?

OD 32-250mm, ID 16-200mm, lengths 3-6m. Grades 625, 825 stock; Inconel hex bars for solid needs. Custom trepanned.
