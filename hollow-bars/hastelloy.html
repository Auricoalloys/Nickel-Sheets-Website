Standards and Specifications for Hastelloy Hollow Bars

Hastelloy® C-276 hollow bars meet stringent codes for universal corrosion resistance:

  • UNS N10276: Nickel-molybdenum-chromium alloy 276.
  • ASTM B619 / B622: Seamless pipe and tube standards.
  • DIN/EN 2.4819 (NiMo16Cr15W): German/EU designation.
  • Additional: ASME SB-619, ISO 6207, NACE MR0175/ISO 15156.

Dimensions: 21-406mm OD, 2.5-50mm walls, lengths to 10m. Non-destructive testing: ET, UT (ASTM E213), hydrotest to 150% yield.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose Hastelloy C-276 hollow bars for chemical processing?

UNS N10276 (ASTM B619) delivers unparalleled resistance to H2SO4, HCl, and wet Cl2 up to boiling points. With 16% Mo + 16% Cr, PREN >60 prevents pitting where Alloy 22 or 625 fail, saving OEMs 70% on lifetime costs in phosphoric acid plants. DIN 2.4819 compliant.

Are Hastelloy hollow bars suitable for oil & gas sour service?

Yes, Hastelloy C-276 meets NACE MR0175 for H2S partial pressure up to 100 psi at 232°C. Ideal for tubulars and valves in HPHT wells, offering better SCC resistance than duplex grades for fabricators specifying long-life sour components.

What sizes of Hastelloy hollow bars are stocked?

Standard: 21-406mm OD, 2.5-50mm walls, 6-10m lengths per ASTM B619. Seamless from Mumbai for immediate dispatch; custom per DIN 2.4819 for EU procurement heads.

How does Hastelloy C-276 perform in power generation FGD systems?

Excels in SO2/SO3 slurries and Cl- contaminated flue gas, with corrosion rates <0.1 mm/y vs. 1-5 mm/y for SS. Preferred over titanium for cost in scrubber internals, per industry standards.
