Standards and Specifications for Cobalt Alloy Hollow Bars

Cobalt alloy hollow bars conform to rigorous international standards ensuring traceability and performance. Key designations include:

  • UNS R30035: Primary US specification for cobalt-chromium-molybdenum alloys.
  • ASTM F75: Casting alloy standard, extended to wrought hollow bar forms.
  • DIN/EN 2.4778: European norm for high-carbon cobalt alloys (CoCrMo).
  • Additional: AMS 5894 (wrought bar), ISO 5832-4 (surgical grade variants), BS 7282.

Available sizes range from 20mm to 400mm OD, with wall thicknesses 3-50mm, in lengths up to 6m. All bars undergo 100% PMI testing, ultrasonic flaw detection (UT Level 2), and hydrostatic pressure testing per ASTM A999.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the key advantages of UNS R30035 cobalt alloy hollow bars in chemical processing?

UNS R30035 (ASTM F75) offers exceptional resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and erosion in acidic environments like HCl and H2SO4. With 27-30% Cr and 5-7% Mo, it outperforms 316L stainless by 5x in wear life, reducing maintenance in pumps and valves for procurement heads seeking long-term ROI.

Are cobalt alloy hollow bars suitable for oil & gas sour service?

Yes, DIN 2.4778 hollow bars meet NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for H2S environments up to 10% concentration. Their high hardness (HRC 36-45) prevents sulfide stress cracking, making them ideal for downhole tubing over Inconel alternatives in high-pressure wells.

What sizes are available for cobalt chrome hollow bars?

Standard range: 20-400mm OD, 3-50mm wall thickness, lengths to 6m. Custom trepanned IDs from Mumbai stock for OEM fabricators. Complies with ASTM F75 and EN 2.4778 for seamless precision.

How do cobalt alloy hollow bars compare to Hastelloy for marine use?

Cobalt R30035 provides superior cavitation erosion resistance (10x better than Hastelloy C276) for propeller shafts and seawater pumps. While Hastelloy edges in uniform corrosion, cobalt's galling resistance suits dynamic marine loads per ASTM standards.
