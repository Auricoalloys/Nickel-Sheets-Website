Aurico Alloys LLP supplies titanium and titanium alloys from Mumbai in sheet, plate, round bar, foil and powder. Held in stock or arranged with the manufacturer, depending on grade and quantity.

Titanium divides into commercially pure grades and alloyed grades, and the two are bought for different reasons. The CP grades — 1 through 4 — are essentially the same metal with progressively more oxygen, which raises strength and lowers ductility as the number goes up. They are chosen for corrosion resistance and formability, not for strength.

The alloyed grades trade some of that formability for a strength-to-weight ratio no steel matches. Grade 5, Ti-6Al-4V, accounts for most titanium used anywhere. Grade 23 is the same alloy with the interstitials held low for fracture toughness and implant use. A third group, the palladium-bearing grades, exists purely to survive reducing acids that would attack CP titanium.

Where These Alloys Are Used

  • Chemical processing — heat exchangers, vessels and piping in chloride service
  • Marine and offshore — seawater systems, risers and heat exchange
  • Medical — implants and instruments, in Grade 2 and Grade 23
  • Aerospace — airframe and engine components, mostly Grade 5
  • Desalination — plate and tubular heat exchangers
  • Additive manufacturing — gas and plasma atomised powder in Grades 2, 5 and 23

Certification and Cutting

Material is supplied against EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates, with chemical composition and mechanical properties for the batch. Cutting to size is done in house.
