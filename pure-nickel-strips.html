Pure Nickel Strips & Busbars

  1. Home
  2. Pure Nickel Strips & Busbars

Aurico Alloys LLP manufactures pure nickel strip and busbars at 99.6% nickel to ASTM B162, laser cut to your battery pack layout. Strip is supplied plain, punched, H-type, honeycomb and zig-zag, in the cell pitches below, with mill test certificates to EN 10204 3.1 and export packing from Mumbai.

Nickel is used for cell interconnects because it spot welds cleanly to steel cell cans, resists corrosion without plating, and holds its conductivity through the heat of welding. Thickness, width and pitch all follow the pack design, so tell us the cell format and the current each joint has to carry and we will confirm the gauge and lead time.
