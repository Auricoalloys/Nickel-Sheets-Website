Chemical Composition

C-276 carries 15-17% Mo and 14.5-16.5% Cr with tungsten and low carbon to resist sensitisation; C-22 raises chromium to 20-22.5% for oxidising media; the B-series runs 26-30% Mo with minimal chromium for hydrochloric acid.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Typical annealed tensile strength is 690-790 MPa with elongation above 40%. The C-series resists pitting, crevice attack and chloride stress-corrosion cracking in both oxidising and reducing conditions.

Industry Applications

How pipe is used

Pipe is pressure-carrying hollow section for moving process fluid at scale. It goes into transfer lines, headers and risers, and into sour gas and offshore service where the line has to resist the fluid as well as hold the pressure. Schedule, end preparation and whether the pipe is seamless or welded are the decisions that drive the specification.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on grade and specification we can supply positive material identification (PMI), ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM A262 or G28, hydrostatic testing, and third-party inspection release notes from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.

Aurico Alloys LLP operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system. Material for sour service can be supplied certified to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 where the grade permits.
