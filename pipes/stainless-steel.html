Specifications and Standards

Special stainless steel pipes adhere to rigorous standards like ASTM A312 for seamless and welded pipes (e.g., TP904L), ASTM A240 for plates, and DIN/EN 1.4539 (X1-NiCrMoCu25-20-5). These ensure compliance for high-pressure, high-temperature applications in corrosive settings. UNS designations such as N08904 classify these super-austenitic grades.

Certifications including NACE MR0175 for sour service and ISO 9001 verify material integrity, with mill test reports (MTRs) confirming adherence.

Chemical Composition

For 904L: C ≤0.02%, Cr 19-23%, Ni 23-28%, Mo 4-5%, Cu 1-2%. Higher Mo and Ni in 6Mo grades (Cr 20%, Ni 18-20%, Mo 6.0-6.5%) boost pitting resistance equivalent (PREN >40). Duplex 2205 features Cr 22%, Ni 5%, Mo 3%, N 0.14-0.20%.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

904L offers tensile strength 490 MPa min, yield 220 MPa, elongation 35%. Duplex 2205 doubles this with 620 MPa tensile, 450 MPa yield. Density ~8 g/cm³, thermal conductivity 11.5 W/m·K for 904L; superior fatigue resistance suits cyclic loading.

Applications by Industry

Chemical Processing

Chosen for sulfuric/phosphoric acid handling over 316L due to higher PREN (>35 vs 25), reducing pitting. Oil & gas uses duplex for sour service pipelines, resisting H2S.

Oil & Gas and Marine

Super-austenitic grades excel in seawater desalination, offshore platforms; oil & gas consumes 28% of stainless pipes globally. Marine apps favor 6Mo for crevice corrosion resistance.

Pharma and Power Generation

Pharma selects low-carbon grades for sterility, cleanability; power gen uses in flue-gas desulfurization (FGD). OEMs/fabricators prefer for heat exchangers, boilers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What standards cover special stainless steel pipes?

ASTM A312/A269 for pipes/tubes (TP904L, TP254SMO), A240 for plates; DIN/EN 1.4539 (904L), 1.4462 (2205); UNS N08904/N08367. These ensure pressure ratings up to 2500 psi, corrosion resistance in chlorides/acids. Compliance verified via MTRs.

Why choose 904L over 316L for chemical processing?

904L's higher Ni/Mo/Cu (PREN>35) resists pitting/crevice corrosion in sulfuric acid, unlike 316L (PREN~25). Reduces failures by 70% in aggressive environments; justified for 20+ year life despite 20-30% higher cost.

Are these pipes suitable for oil & gas sour service?

Yes, duplex 2205 (UNS S32205) and 904L meet NACE MR0175 for H2S; high yield strength (450 MPa) handles HP/HT. Used in 60% of new Asian refineries' hydrocarbon units. (45 words)

What testing ensures quality?

Hydrostatic (min 2x design pressure), dye penetrant/RT for welds, PMI for composition, impact testing per ASTM A370. Certifications: ISO 17025, EN 10204 3.1/3.2.
