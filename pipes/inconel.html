Aerospace Applications

Inconel 718 pipes excel in jet engine exhausts and turbine components due to their high-temperature strength (up to 700°C) and creep resistance, outperforming stainless alternatives in cyclic fatigue.

Chemical Processing Applications

Inconel® 625 pipes resist pitting and crevice corrosion in sulfuric/phosphoric acids, ideal for reactors and scrubbers-offering 4x better corrosion resistance than 316L in chloride environments.

Oil & Gas Applications

Inconel® 825 pipes handle sour gas (H2S) service per NACE MR0175, with superior stress-corrosion cracking resistance for downhole tubing and umbilicals in high-pressure offshore wells.
