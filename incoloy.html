Incoloy Alloys

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Incoloy® from Mumbai in a full range of forms — sheet, plate, coil, strip, foil, bar, wire and tube. Held in stock or arranged with the manufacturer, depending on grade and quantity.

Incoloy is the iron-nickel-chromium end of the nickel alloy range. Adding iron dilutes the nickel, which costs some corrosion resistance against a full nickel alloy but costs a good deal less — so Incoloy is where you land when stainless is not enough and Inconel is more than the duty needs.

The family splits three ways by what it was designed to survive. The 800 series resists heat, oxidation and carburisation in furnace and process service. 825 and its relatives resist wet corrosion, sulphuric and phosphoric acid in particular. The 900 series is different again: 903 and 909 are controlled-expansion alloys for aerospace, while 925 and 945 are age-hardenable grades for sour oil and gas service.

Key Properties

  • Heat, oxidation and carburisation resistance in the 800 series
  • Wet corrosion resistance to sulphuric and phosphoric acid in 825
  • Controlled thermal expansion in 903 and 909, for aerospace rings and seals
  • Age-hardenable strength for sour oil and gas service in 925 and 945
  • Lower cost than a full nickel alloy, from the iron content

Where These Alloys Are Used

  • Heat treatment — furnace retorts, muffles, radiant tubes and fixtures
  • Petrochemical — process heaters, pigtails and transfer lines
  • Oil and gas — sour service tubulars, hangers and downhole tools in 925 and 945
  • Chemical processing — acid service in 825, particularly pickling and fertiliser plant
  • Aerospace — controlled-expansion rings and seals in 903 and 909
  • Power generation — superheater and reheater components
