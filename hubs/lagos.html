Nickel Alloy Supplier to Lagos, Nigeria

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nickel alloys, titanium, duplex and super duplex stainless and cobalt alloys into Lagos, shipped from our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai with full export documentation.

Lagos is Nigeria's commercial centre and its main import gateway through Apapa and Tin Can Island, with the Lekki free zone to the east hosting a large refining, petrochemical and fertiliser complex.

What Lagos Service Demands

Refinery and fertiliser plant calls for Inconel 625, Incoloy 825 and 800HT and Hastelloy C-276, while offshore and sour service supplied through Lagos draws on super duplex and Inconel 718.

Specification and Conformity

Imports are subject to the Standards Organisation of Nigeria Conformity Assessment Programme, which requires certification against the applicable standard before shipment.
