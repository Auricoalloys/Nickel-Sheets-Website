Nickel Alloy Supplier to Houston, United States

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nickel alloys, titanium, duplex and super duplex stainless and cobalt alloys into Houston, shipped from our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai with full export documentation.

Houston and the upper Texas Gulf Coast form the largest concentration of refining and petrochemical capacity in the Americas, with the Ship Channel complex, offshore engineering and fabrication, and the supply chain serving Permian and deepwater production.

What Houston Service Demands

Refinery and petrochemical service draws heavily on Hastelloy C-276 and C-22, Inconel 625 and Incoloy 825; upstream and offshore work on super duplex, Inconel 718 and 725 to NACE MR0175; and cooling systems on 6Mo austenitics.

Specification and Conformity

Material is specified to ASTM with ASME Section II governing pressure applications and NACE MR0175 applying to sour service. Importers should note that Section 232 measures apply to certain steel products.
