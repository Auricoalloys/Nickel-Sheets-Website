Nickel Alloy Supplier to Ras Laffan, Qatar

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nickel alloys, titanium, duplex and super duplex stainless and cobalt alloys into Ras Laffan, shipped from our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai with full export documentation.

Ras Laffan Industrial City on Qatar's north-east coast is the centre of the country's LNG and gas-to-liquids production, with liquefaction trains, condensate refining, helium recovery and a dedicated export port.

What Ras Laffan Service Demands

Cryogenic LNG service and acid gas removal define the material requirement: nickel 200/201 and 9% nickel steel in cold service, Inconel 625 and 718 in process and rotating equipment, and duplex and super duplex through the utilities and seawater systems.

Specification and Conformity

Project material follows the purchaser engineering specification, with impact testing at low temperature routinely required on cryogenic-service items.
