Nickel Alloy Supplier to Jebel Ali, United Arab Emirates

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nickel alloys, titanium, duplex and super duplex stainless and cobalt alloys into Jebel Ali, shipped from our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai with full export documentation.

Jebel Ali combines one of the region's largest container ports with the free zone alongside it, making it the principal distribution point for the Gulf, East Africa and the Indian Ocean, with aluminium smelting and engineering fabrication on the same site.

What Jebel Ali Service Demands

Free zone traders and fabricators buy across the range for onward supply, with the heaviest movement in duplex, Inconel 625 and 6Mo austenitics for regional oil, gas and desalination projects.

Specification and Conformity

Free zone arrangements are widely used for re-export across the Gulf and East Africa. Conformity for the UAE market is assessed under the Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme.
