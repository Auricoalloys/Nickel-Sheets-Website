Nickel Alloy Supplier to Ruwais, United Arab Emirates

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nickel alloys, titanium, duplex and super duplex stainless and cobalt alloys into Ruwais, shipped from our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai with full export documentation.

Ruwais in the Al Dhafra region is Abu Dhabi's refining and petrochemical centre, combining one of the world's larger single-site refineries with polyolefin, aromatics and fertiliser production and a dedicated industrial port.

What Ruwais Service Demands

Crude and residue processing, sulphur recovery and polymer production put Inconel 625, Incoloy 825 and 800HT and Hastelloy C-276 in continuous use, with 6Mo austenitics and titanium in seawater cooling.

Specification and Conformity
