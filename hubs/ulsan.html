Nickel Alloy Supplier to Ulsan, South Korea

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nickel alloys, titanium, duplex and super duplex stainless and cobalt alloys into Ulsan, shipped from our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai with full export documentation.

Ulsan is South Korea's industrial capital, combining the world's largest single shipyard with major refining and petrochemical complexes, automotive assembly and a dense base of heavy fabrication.

What Ulsan Service Demands

Shipbuilding and offshore module fabrication is the largest draw - super duplex and duplex for topsides and ballast systems, nickel 200/201 for LNG cargo containment, Inconel 625 for cryogenic and sour service - alongside refinery grades for the petrochemical complexes.

Specification and Conformity

Korean yards typically order to the end client project specification rather than to KS, with full traceability and third-party inspection standard on export fabrication.
