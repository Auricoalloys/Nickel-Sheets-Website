    Monel K-500 (UNS N05500, ASTM B865, W.Nr. 2.4375) is a nickel-copper alloy strengthened by precipitation hardening with precise additions of aluminum and titanium. This superalloy maintains excellent mechanical performance (tensile ≥965 MPa, yield ≥690 MPa) and resists cracking, distortion, or corrosion in sour gas, high-velocity seawater, acid lines, and cryogenic media. Unlike standard stainless and bronze grades, Monel K-500 remains durable at sub-zero and elevated temperatures (up to 480°C), and is non-magnetic, making it ideal for oil survey components, valve trims, and offshore drilling hardware.
