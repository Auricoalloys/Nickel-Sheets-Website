Monel K-500 (UNS N05500, ASTM B164) is a nickel-copper alloy strengthened by discrete aluminum and titanium additions via precipitation hardening, greatly enhancing its tensile and yield strength while maintaining corrosion resistance comparable to Monel 400. The alloy is highly resistant to chloride stress corrosion cracking (SCC), hydrogen embrittlement, and pitting corrosion, performing excellently in challenging marine, chemical, and sour gas environments with service temperature capabilities up to 315°C.
