Monel R-405 (UNS N04405)

Monel R-405 (UNS N04405) is Monel 400 with sulphur deliberately added — 0.025 to 0.060%, stated as a minimum rather than a ceiling. The sulphide inclusions break the chip, and that is the whole point of the grade: it is bought to be screw-machined at rates plain Monel 400 will not sustain. Every other element matches 400, so the seawater, hydrofluoric acid and caustic resistance comes with it unchanged.

Key Properties

  • Free-machining by design. Sulphur 0.025-0.060% is the only difference from Monel 400, and it is specified as a minimum because the inclusions are wanted.
  • Monel 400's chemistry otherwise — nickel 63% minimum, copper 28-34% — so corrosion behaviour follows the parent grade.
  • Bar and wire only. Special Metals publishes ASTM B164 for rod, bar and wire and no flat-product standard: added sulphur has no purpose in plate or sheet, and would only harm hot workability.
  • Monel 400 is the alternative wherever the part is welded rather than machined — sulphur is what makes R-405 the poorer choice for welding.

Applications

  • Valve stems and seats machined in volume
  • Fasteners, studs and screw-machined fittings
  • Meter, instrument and pump components
  • Marine and chemical hardware where Monel 400 corrosion resistance is needed on a turned part
