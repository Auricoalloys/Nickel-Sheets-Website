    Monel K400 (UNS N04400, ASTM B164, W.Nr. 2.4360/2.4361) is a solid-solution nickel-copper alloy prized for resistance to acidic and alkaline media, stress corrosion cracking, and high-velocity seawater. Unlike cheaper stainless and bronze, Monel K400 bars retain strength, conductivity, and non-magnetic stability even in fast-flowing brine, hydrofluoric acid, sour gas, and cryogenic service. Its proven reliability in chemical plants, marine hardware, heat exchangers, and food or pharma tanks is why engineers trust it where asset failure isn’t an option.

    Aurico Alloys LLP provides Monel K 400 Round Bars in sizes up to 800mm diameter, precision-cut and certified to ASTM, EN, DIN, and NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 requirements. Each batch comes with a full mill test certificate for global projects, available in rough-turned, bright-drawn, or custom-machined finishes, enabling seamless deployment in industrial supply chains and urgent spares.
