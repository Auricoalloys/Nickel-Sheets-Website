Monel 400 Wire Supplier, Stockist and Exporter

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies monel 400 wire (N04400, Werkstoff 2.4360) from ready stock in Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets. Wire is bought on diameter and temper, and those two decide whether a coil is any use for the job long before anything on the certificate does.

Monel 400 is a single-phase nickel-copper alloy, so it cannot be hardened by heat treatment and takes its strength from cold work - which is what the temper on a wire order is buying. It is the standard choice for seawater and hydrofluoric acid service.

Industry Applications

  • MIG and TIG welding, as filler wire matching the parent alloy
  • Fasteners — bolts, studs, rivets and cold-headed parts

  • Mesh, wire cloth and general fabrication
