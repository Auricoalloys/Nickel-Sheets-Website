Monel 400 (UNS N04400, ASTM B164) is a solid-solution nickel-copper alloy engineered for corrosion resistance to seawater, sulfuric acid, and hydrogen sulfide environments. Its nickel-copper matrix imparts superior resistance to chloride stress corrosion cracking while retaining ductility and weldability. The alloy offers tensile strength from about 480 MPa annealed, higher in cold-worked tempers, and continuous service to around 480°C, making it ideal for valves, pumps, compressors, subsea equipment, and heat exchangers in the harshest industrial settings.
