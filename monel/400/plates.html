What Sets Monel K 400 Plates Apart?

Monel K 400 (UNS N04400, ASTM B164) is a nickel-copper alloy enhanced with small additions of aluminum and titanium to provide excellent strength and corrosion resistance, even in highly aggressive environments like seawater and acid. It resists pitting, stress corrosion cracking, and biofouling, offering superior durability in marine hulls, pump shafts, and chemical reaction vessels.

This alloy’s excellent fatigue and impact resistance, combined with its weldability and machinability, enable fabricators to produce complex components with precision and reliability. Its ability to withstand temperatures up to 427°C (800°F) without loss of mechanical strength makes it invaluable for many industrial applications.
