Supply Locations Across India

Our head office is in Mumbai and we hold stock at warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai, supplying customers across India's process, energy and engineering industries. The pages below set out the industrial profile of each location and the grades most requested there.

Material can be sheared, laser or waterjet cut, slit or sawn to length before despatch, and every consignment carries an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate.

Andhra Pradesh

LocationLocal industry
VisakhapatnamVisakhapatnam combines an integrated steel plant, a coastal refinery, shipbuilding and naval dockyards, and the pharmaceutical cluster around the city, served by one of India's major eastern ports.

Delhi NCR

LocationLocal industry
New DelhiDelhi and the surrounding National Capital Region host the head offices of most of India's major EPC contractors, public sector engineering companies and power utilities, making it the country's centre of gravity for project procurement.

Gujarat

LocationLocal industry
AhmedabadAhmedabad anchors the Gujarat chemical and pharmaceutical industry, with dye and dye-intermediate manufacture, bulk drug production and textile processing concentrated in and around the city and the Naroda and Odhav estates.
VadodaraVadodara sits at the centre of Gujarat's petrochemical and heavy engineering belt, with fertiliser, petrochemical and industrial gas production nearby and a substantial concentration of process plant fabricators and power equipment manufacturers.
BharuchThe Bharuch district - taking in the Ankleshwar, Dahej, Jhagadia and Panoli estates - is among the most concentrated chemical manufacturing areas in India, covering bulk and speciality chemicals, agrochemicals, dyes and petrochemical derivatives.
JamnagarJamnagar hosts the largest refining complex in the world by single-site capacity, together with a second major refinery and associated petrochemical and aromatics units, making it the densest concentration of refining assets in India.
SuratThe Hazira industrial area outside Surat concentrates natural gas processing, heavy engineering and steel making, while the city itself is a major centre for textile processing and dyeing.
RajkotRajkot is an engineering and casting centre, with a large concentration of foundries, forging shops, diesel engine builders and pump and valve manufacturers supplying industry across India.

Haryana

LocationLocal industry
PanipatPanipat hosts a large refinery and integrated petrochemical complex, together with a naphtha cracker and associated polymer and aromatics units, and a long-established textile processing industry.

Jharkhand

LocationLocal industry
JamshedpurJamshedpur is built around integrated steel making and the heavy engineering, automotive and machinery businesses that developed alongside it, giving the city an unusually deep base of fabrication and machining capability.

Karnataka

LocationLocal industry
BengaluruBengaluru is India's aerospace and defence manufacturing centre, with airframe and engine production, space launch programmes, gas turbine work and a dense supporting base of precision machining and special-process suppliers.

Maharashtra

LocationLocal industry
Navi MumbaiNavi Mumbai holds two of Maharashtra's largest industrial estates - the TTC belt at Rabale, Turbhe and Mahape, and Taloja MIDC - covering bulk and speciality chemicals, pharmaceutical manufacture, engineering and electronics.
MumbaiMumbai and the surrounding Thane-Belapur and Taloja belts form one of India's densest concentrations of chemical, pharmaceutical and engineering manufacture, alongside the refineries at Mahul and the nuclear research establishment at Trombay.
PunePune and the Pimpri-Chinchwad and Chakan belts form one of India's largest automotive and engineering clusters, extending into defence manufacture, forging and precision machining.

Tamil Nadu

LocationLocal industry
ChennaiChennai combines a large automotive and auto component industry with the Manali petrochemical and refining complex to the north of the city, plus fertiliser production and a substantial engineering base.
CoimbatoreCoimbatore is India's principal centre for pump and motor manufacture, with a deep base of foundries, machine shops and textile machinery builders supplying process industries across the country.
SalemSalem is a stainless steel town, built around a major stainless plant and the rolling, processing and fabrication businesses that grew up alongside it, together with magnesite and refractory production.

Telangana

LocationLocal industry
HyderabadHyderabad combines one of the world's largest bulk drug and API manufacturing clusters with aerospace, defence and heavy electrical equipment manufacture.

West Bengal

LocationLocal industry
HaldiaHaldia is an integrated refining and petrochemical centre on the Hooghly, combining a refinery, a petrochemical complex and associated chemical units with a working river port.
KolkataKolkata is the commercial and engineering hub for eastern India, with a long-established base of heavy engineering, boiler and pressure vessel fabrication, and trading houses serving the mineral and steel belt inland.
DurgapurDurgapur is a steel and heavy engineering town, with an integrated steel plant, an alloy steel plant and a cluster of foundries, forges and engineering works serving the eastern industrial belt.
