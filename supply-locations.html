Supply Locations Across India

Our head office is in Mumbai and we hold stock at warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai, supplying customers across India's process, energy and engineering industries. The pages below set out the industrial profile of each location and the grades most requested there.

Material can be sheared, laser or waterjet cut, slit or sawn to length before despatch, and every consignment carries an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate.

Andhra Pradesh

LocationLocal industry
KakinadaKakinada serves the Krishna-Godavari basin gas fields from a deep-water port on the Andhra coast, with onshore gas processing, fertiliser production and edible oil refining in the district.
RajahmundryRajahmundry sits on the onshore Krishna-Godavari basin, with gas production and processing, power generation, paper manufacture and pharmaceutical intermediates in the surrounding district.
VijayawadaVijayawada is the commercial and logistics centre of coastal Andhra Pradesh, serving thermal power generation, cement production, and the gas and fertiliser industry of the Krishna-Godavari districts.
VisakhapatnamVisakhapatnam combines an integrated steel plant, a coastal refinery, shipbuilding and naval dockyards, and the pharmaceutical cluster around the city, served by one of India's major eastern ports.

Chhattisgarh

LocationLocal industry
RaipurRaipur is the commercial centre of the Chhattisgarh steel belt, surrounded by sponge iron plants, rolling mills, ferro-alloy production and power generation.
BhilaiBhilai is built around one of India's largest integrated steel plants, with coke ovens, blast furnaces, rolling mills and an extensive base of engineering and maintenance contractors alongside.

Delhi NCR

LocationLocal industry
New DelhiDelhi and the surrounding National Capital Region host the head offices of most of India's major EPC contractors, public sector engineering companies and power utilities, making it the country's centre of gravity for project procurement.

Gujarat

LocationLocal industry
VapiVapi holds one of Asia's largest chemical manufacturing estates, running north from the Daman Ganga along the GIDC belt, with dyes and dye intermediates, agrochemicals, pharmaceutical intermediates and speciality chemicals concentrated in a few square kilometres.
UmbergaonUmbergaon sits at the southern tip of Gujarat on the Maharashtra border, with GIDC estates covering chemicals, engineering, plastics processing and glass, and forms a continuous industrial belt with Vapi and Sarigam immediately to the north.
NavsariNavsari lies between the Vapi and Hazira industrial belts, with GIDC estates covering chemicals, textile processing and engineering, and sits close enough to the Surat and Hazira complexes to serve as a supply point for both.
AnandAnand and the adjoining Vallabh Vidyanagar and Vithal Udyognagar estates form a long-established engineering cluster, best known for submersible pump and electrical equipment manufacture, alongside chemical and food processing plant.
MundraMundra combines India's largest commercial port with a special economic zone covering thermal power generation, edible oil refining, petrochemicals and copper smelting, on the Gulf of Kutch.
HalolHalol, east of Vadodara, hosts automotive assembly and component manufacture alongside a substantial pharmaceutical and formulations cluster, with engineering estates serving both.
AhmedabadAhmedabad anchors the Gujarat chemical and pharmaceutical industry, with dye and dye-intermediate manufacture, bulk drug production and textile processing concentrated in and around the city and the Naroda and Odhav estates.
VadodaraVadodara sits at the centre of Gujarat's petrochemical and heavy engineering belt, with fertiliser, petrochemical and industrial gas production nearby and a substantial concentration of process plant fabricators and power equipment manufacturers.
BharuchThe Bharuch district - taking in the Ankleshwar, Dahej, Jhagadia and Panoli estates - is among the most concentrated chemical manufacturing areas in India, covering bulk and speciality chemicals, agrochemicals, dyes and petrochemical derivatives.
JamnagarJamnagar hosts the largest refining complex in the world by single-site capacity, together with a second major refinery and associated petrochemical and aromatics units, making it the densest concentration of refining assets in India.
SuratThe Hazira industrial area outside Surat concentrates natural gas processing, heavy engineering and steel making, while the city itself is a major centre for textile processing and dyeing.
RajkotRajkot is an engineering and casting centre, with a large concentration of foundries, forging shops, diesel engine builders and pump and valve manufacturers supplying industry across India.

Haryana

LocationLocal industry
FaridabadFaridabad is one of north India's largest engineering centres, covering automotive components, tractors and agricultural machinery, machine tools, switchgear and refrigeration equipment, with a deep base of forging, casting and precision machining.
HisarHisar is a stainless steel town.
YamunanagarYamunanagar is a pulp, paper and plywood centre, with some of India's larger paper mills, sugar processing and a metal fabrication base supplying both.
PanipatPanipat hosts a large refinery and integrated petrochemical complex, together with a naphtha cracker and associated polymer and aromatics units, and a long-established textile processing industry.

Jharkhand

LocationLocal industry
BokaroBokaro is an integrated steel city in Jharkhand, with blast furnaces, coke ovens and hot and cold rolling, alongside power generation and a base of engineering and maintenance contractors.
RanchiRanchi hosts one of India's principal heavy engineering works, building equipment for steel plants, mining and power generation, with a supporting base of foundries and fabrication shops.
JamshedpurJamshedpur is built around integrated steel making and the heavy engineering, automotive and machinery businesses that developed alongside it, giving the city an unusually deep base of fabrication and machining capability.

Karnataka

LocationLocal industry
MangaluruMangaluru hosts a coastal refinery and petrochemical complex, a fertiliser plant, strategic crude storage and a deep-water port on the Karnataka coast.
BengaluruBengaluru is India's aerospace and defence manufacturing centre, with airframe and engine production, space launch programmes, gas turbine work and a dense supporting base of precision machining and special-process suppliers.

Kerala

LocationLocal industry
KochiKochi combines a large coastal refinery and petrochemical complex with India's biggest shipyard, a rare earths and chemicals plant, and a major container port on the Arabian Sea.

Madhya Pradesh

LocationLocal industry
IndoreIndore and the adjoining Pithampur industrial area form central India's largest manufacturing belt, covering automotive assembly and components, pharmaceuticals, and engineering and process equipment fabrication.
BhopalBhopal hosts one of India's major heavy electrical equipment works, producing turbines, generators, transformers and switchgear, with a supporting base of engineering and fabrication suppliers.

Maharashtra

LocationLocal industry
TarapurThe Tarapur and Boisar industrial belt north of Mumbai concentrates bulk and speciality chemicals, pharmaceutical intermediates and engineering, alongside nuclear power generation on the coast.
AmbernathAmbernath and the adjoining Badlapur estates form part of the Mumbai chemical and engineering hinterland, covering speciality chemicals, dyes, engineering fabrication and defence ordnance manufacture.
MahadThe Mahad and Roha MIDC estates in Raigad district are a concentrated chemical manufacturing belt south of Mumbai, covering bulk chemicals, dyes and intermediates, agrochemicals and pharmaceutical intermediates.
PatalgangaThe Patalganga and Nagothane belt in Raigad district hosts petrochemical production, polymer and intermediates plant and engineering fabrication, within reach of both Mumbai and the JNPT port.
NagpurNagpur sits at the centre of India's thermal power and coal belt, with generation, mining equipment manufacture, defence and aerospace work at MIHAN, and the Butibori industrial area to the south.
NashikNashik combines aircraft manufacture and overhaul with a large automotive and engineering base across the Satpur and Ambad estates, plus defence ordnance production and currency printing.
Chhatrapati SambhajinagarThe Waluj, Shendra and Chikalthana estates around Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, formerly Aurangabad, cover automotive components, pharmaceutical manufacture, brewing and beverage plant, and precision engineering.
KolhapurKolhapur is one of India's largest foundry clusters, with several hundred casting units supplying automotive, pump, valve and engineering customers, alongside sugar processing and textile machinery manufacture.
Navi MumbaiNavi Mumbai holds two of Maharashtra's largest industrial estates - the TTC belt at Rabale, Turbhe and Mahape, and Taloja MIDC - covering bulk and speciality chemicals, pharmaceutical manufacture, engineering and electronics.
MumbaiMumbai and the surrounding Thane-Belapur and Taloja belts form one of India's densest concentrations of chemical, pharmaceutical and engineering manufacture, alongside the refineries at Mahul and the nuclear research establishment at Trombay.
PunePune and the Pimpri-Chinchwad and Chakan belts form one of India's largest automotive and engineering clusters, extending into defence manufacture, forging and precision machining.

Odisha

LocationLocal industry
RourkelaRourkela is an integrated steel town in western Odisha, with coke ovens, blast furnaces, a fertiliser plant and a pipe works, supported by a cluster of engineering and fabrication businesses.
AngulAngul concentrates aluminium smelting, integrated steel making, coal-based power generation and coal gasification in a single district of central Odisha.
ParadipParadip combines a deep-water port on the Odisha coast with a large refinery, petrochemical units and fertiliser production, plus bulk handling for the mineral belt inland.
BhubaneswarBhubaneswar is the commercial and engineering centre for Odisha's mineral belt, serving the steel, aluminium and power projects across the state from a base of engineering, procurement and fabrication businesses.

Punjab

LocationLocal industry
LudhianaLudhiana is Punjab's manufacturing centre, with forging, casting, fastener and auto component works, bicycle and machine tool manufacture, and a large concentration of heat treatment and plating capacity.
BathindaBathinda hosts a large refinery in southern Punjab, with associated petrochemical units, a fertiliser plant and thermal power generation in the district.

Tamil Nadu

LocationLocal industry
TiruchirappalliTiruchirappalli is India's boiler manufacturing centre, with a major high-pressure boiler works and seed plant supported by a dense cluster of fabrication, tube bending and pressure vessel businesses.
HosurHosur sits on the Tamil Nadu side of the Bengaluru industrial corridor, covering automotive and two-wheeler manufacture, electronics assembly, precision engineering and aerospace component work.
ChennaiChennai combines a large automotive and auto component industry with the Manali petrochemical and refining complex to the north of the city, plus fertiliser production and a substantial engineering base.
CoimbatoreCoimbatore is India's principal centre for pump and motor manufacture, with a deep base of foundries, machine shops and textile machinery builders supplying process industries across the country.
SalemSalem is a stainless steel town, built around a major stainless plant and the rolling, processing and fabrication businesses that grew up alongside it, together with magnesite and refractory production.

Telangana

LocationLocal industry
HyderabadHyderabad combines one of the world's largest bulk drug and API manufacturing clusters with aerospace, defence and heavy electrical equipment manufacture.

Uttar Pradesh

LocationLocal industry
GhaziabadGhaziabad forms part of the Delhi NCR engineering belt, with heavy electrical equipment, pipe and tube manufacture, auto components and process equipment fabrication concentrated across its industrial areas.
KanpurKanpur is an old industrial city with fertiliser and chemical manufacture, defence ordnance production, leather and tanning chemistry, and a base of engineering and fabrication works along the Ganga.
NoidaNoida and Greater Noida cover electronics and appliance manufacture, automotive components, and a growing base of precision engineering and process equipment fabrication within the Delhi NCR belt.

West Bengal

LocationLocal industry
HaldiaHaldia is an integrated refining and petrochemical centre on the Hooghly, combining a refinery, a petrochemical complex and associated chemical units with a working river port.
KolkataKolkata is the commercial and engineering hub for eastern India, with a long-established base of heavy engineering, boiler and pressure vessel fabrication, and trading houses serving the mineral and steel belt inland.
DurgapurDurgapur is a steel and heavy engineering town, with an integrated steel plant, an alloy steel plant and a cluster of foundries, forges and engineering works serving the eastern industrial belt.
