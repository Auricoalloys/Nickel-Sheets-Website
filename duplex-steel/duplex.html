Introduction: Refinery Heat Exchanger Failures

In a South Korean refinery's crude distillation unit, duplex stainless steel (DSS) tubes in overhead exchangers suffered chloride-induced stress corrosion cracking (Cl-SCC) at 148°C. MEA hydrochloride salts formed on surfaces, combined with chlorides and low pH, causing transgranular cracks from the outer surface—despite DSS's reputation. Proper duplex steel wire per ASTM A240 and UNS S31803 prevents such issues in chemical processing, oil & gas, and marine sectors.

Specifications & Standards

Duplex wire conforms to ASTM A240 for plates/sheets (applicable chemistry/mechanics), A276 for bars/wire, and A790 for pipes, covering UNS S31803/S32205 (2205). DIN EN 1.4462 and NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 ensure sour service compliance for oil & gas.

Ferrite 35-65% verified metallographically, hardness ≤320 HV10 per NACE MR0103.

Product Forms & Availability

Offered in round (0.5-10 mm Ø), flat, square, shaped wire; spools, coils, straightened lengths per ASTM A276. Used for springs, welds (ER2209), cables in marine and power apps. Custom draws for OEM precision needs.

Chemical Composition

2205 (UNS S31803): Cr 21-23%, Ni 4.5-6.5%, Mo 2.5-3.5%, N 0.08-0.20%, Fe balance. 2507 (S32750): Cr 24-26%, Ni 6-8%, Mo 3-5%, N 0.24-0.32%, Cu ~0.5%—enhanced pitting resistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are key standards for duplex steel wire?

ASTM A240/A276/A790 for UNS S31803 (2205), S32750 (2507); DIN 1.4462, EN X2CrNiMoN22-5. NACE MR0175 for sour oil & gas service.

2205 vs 2507 duplex wire differences?

2205 (UNS S31803): Yield 450 MPa, PREN ~35 for general use; 2507 (S32750): 550 MPa, PREN >40 for severe chlorides. Both per ASTM A240.

Why choose duplex wire over austenitic?

Duplex doubles yield strength, higher PREN resists pitting/SCC in marine/oil. Market to $6B by 2030.

Does duplex come in hex bar form?

Yes, duplex hex bar in 2205/2507 grades (DIN 1.4462) for machining fasteners, pairing with wire in OEM kits per ASTM specs.
