Aurico Alloys LLP supplies hastelloy c4 plates (N06455, Werkstoff 2.4610) from ready stock in Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets.

Plate is specified where pressure containment or structural load governs, so thickness tolerance, flatness and through-thickness testing are what buyers check first.

Hastelloy C4 holds its structure through the 650-1040°C range, so welded fabrications resist the grain-boundary attack that catches other C-family alloys.

Chemical Composition

Nickel balance with 14-18% chromium and 14-17% molybdenum, with iron and tungsten held deliberately low and titanium added for stabilisation.

Mechanical and Physical Properties

Annealed tensile strength around 690 MPa. The restricted iron, cobalt and tungsten give outstanding thermal stability, so it resists sensitisation in the weld heat-affected zone.

Testing and Certification

Every consignment ships with an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate stating heat number, chemical analysis and mechanical properties. Depending on the specification we can add positive material identification, ultrasonic and dye-penetrant testing, intergranular corrosion testing to ASTM G28 or A262, hydrostatic testing, and third-party inspection release from Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV.

Aurico Alloys LLP operates an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system, and can certify material to NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 for sour service where the grade permits.
