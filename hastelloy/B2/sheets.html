Hastelloy B2 (UNS N10665, ASTM B333) is a nickel-molybdenum alloy devoid of chromium, specifically engineered to resist hydrochloric acid and other highly reducing environments at various temperatures. Its exceptional tolerance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking, paired with excellent weldability and mechanical properties, make it an essential choice for demanding chemical processing applications.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy B2 is a nickel-molybdenum alloy carrying about 28% molybdenum and essentially no chromium. That composition makes it the strongest performer the family offers in reducing acids — hydrochloric at all concentrations up to the boiling point, plus sulphuric, acetic and phosphoric. The same absence of chromium means it must not be put into an oxidising stream, or one carrying ferric or cupric ions. For new construction B3 is the usual specification; B2 is ordered to match existing plant.

  • Chemical processing involving hydrochloric and sulfuric acids
  • Oil & gas pipelines and equipment in sour gas service conditions
  • Marine desalination and saltwater system components
  • Pharmaceutical reactors and sterilizers requiring strong corrosion resistance
  • Power generation equipment facing aggressive chemical atmospheres
