FAQ: Hastelloy B2 and B3 Foil Essentials

Hastelloy B2 (UNS N10665, ASTM B575, DIN 2.4617) with 26–30% Mo and <1% Cr provides unmatched resistance to all HCl concentrations up to boiling (corrosion <0.1 mm/y), far superior to C-276 which suffers transpassive attack above 5% HCl; ideal for evaporators, pumps, and piping.
