    Hastelloy B2 (UNS N10665, ASTM B335) is a nickel-molybdenum alloy with added iron that offers improved resistance to hydrochloric acid in reducing conditions and excellent resistance in oxidizing environments. It withstands severe thermal and chemical shocks experienced in reactors, heat exchangers, and piping systems. Its versatility and toughness make it superior to many stainless and nickel alloys when exposed to mixed acids and hot brines.

    Applications & Benefits

    Why this grade

    Hastelloy B2 is a nickel-molybdenum alloy carrying about 28% molybdenum and essentially no chromium. That composition makes it the strongest performer the family offers in reducing acids — hydrochloric at all concentrations up to the boiling point, plus sulphuric, acetic and phosphoric. The same absence of chromium means it must not be put into an oxidising stream, or one carrying ferric or cupric ions. For new construction B3 is the usual specification; B2 is ordered to match existing plant.

    • Chemical: Strong acid reactors, hydrofluoric acid service, evaporators, FGD process, pickling tanks.
    • Marine: Heat exchangers, seawater cooling, condensers, pump parts.
    • Oil & Gas: Sour wells, acidizing tools, piping, valve seats.
    • Pharma: High purity reactors, sterilizers, mixing tanks.
    • Power: Furnace components, boilers, superheaters, turbines.
    • Hardware/Industrial Supply: Trusted stock, OEM builds, urgent repairs.
