    Hastelloy N (UNS N10003, ASTM B573) is a nickel-based alloy that offers excellent stability, oxidation resistance, and corrosion resistance in high-temperature, chloride-rich environments. Its chemistry excludes nickel molybdenum alloys, favoring a unique formulation that resists stress corrosion cracking even in the harshest chemical streams such as hydrochloric acid and strongly oxidizing media. Its thermal stability and high strength make this alloy ideal for heat exchangers, reactor liners, and piping systems that handle aggressive chemicals under extreme conditions.

    Applications & Benefits

    Why this grade

    Hastelloy N is a nickel-molybdenum-chromium alloy developed for molten fluoride salts at the Oak Ridge Molten-Salt Reactor Experiment, and it is still the grade specified for molten fluoride salt and hot hydrofluoric acid service. It is not a general-purpose aqueous corrosion alloy — for acid plant the B and C grades apply.

    • Chemical: Reactors for acids, distillation columns, and corrosion-resistant piping.
    • Marine: Heat exchangers, seawater piping, offshore platforms.
    • Oil & Gas: Hydrogen sulfide environments, sour gas pipelines, oil refining equipment.
    • Pharma: High-purity reaction vessels, sterilizer components, corrosion-resistant process equipment.
    • Power: Exhaust manifolds, furnace components, heat exchangers for high-temperature applications.
