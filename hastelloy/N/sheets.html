Hastelloy N (UNS N10003, ASTM B434) is uniquely formulated for resistance to molten fluoride salts (the fuel and coolant of next-generation molten salt reactors), achieving documented corrosion rates below 0.0025 mm per year in LiF-BeF₂ fuel salt at 700°C. Its exceptional composition 71% nickel, 16% molybdenum, 7% chromium, 5% iron, plus trace elements delivers outstanding oxidation, carburization, and thermal creep resistance across 705–870°C, with capability extending to thermal storage and concentrated solar power applications.

Aurico Alloys LLP offers Hastelloy N Sheets in various thicknesses and finishes, fully certified to ASTM B434 and ASME SB434 standards with complete mill test documentation and traceability.
