Hastelloy N (UNS N10003, ASTM B434) is uniquely formulated for resistance to molten fluoride salts (the fuel and coolant of next-generation molten salt reactors), achieving documented corrosion rates below 0.0025 mm per year in LiF-BeF₂ fuel salt at 700°C. Its exceptional composition 71% nickel, 16% molybdenum, 7% chromium, 5% iron, plus trace elements delivers outstanding oxidation, carburization, and thermal creep resistance across 705–870°C, with capability extending to thermal storage and concentrated solar power applications.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy N is a nickel-molybdenum-chromium alloy developed for molten fluoride salts at the Oak Ridge Molten-Salt Reactor Experiment, and it is still the grade specified for molten fluoride salt and hot hydrofluoric acid service. It is not a general-purpose aqueous corrosion alloy — for acid plant the B and C grades apply.

  • Molten salt reactors (MSR) fuel and coolant circuit components primary application
  • Concentrated solar power (CSP) thermal storage systems
  • Chemical process equipment exposed to extreme oxidation and creep conditions
  • Containers and piping for molten fluoride salt processing
  • Nuclear thermal systems and next-generation energy infrastructure
  • Marine and industrial desalination facilities requiring corrosion resistance
