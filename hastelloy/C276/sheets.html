Hastelloy C276 (UNS N10276, ASTM B574, ASME SB574) is a nickel-molybdenum-chromium alloy designed for extreme resistance to oxidizing and reducing environments, including strong mineral and organic acids. Its impressive chemical composition, including molybdenum and chromium, ensures outstanding protection against chloride ion attacks, while maintaining high mechanical strength and manufacturability across a wide temperature range.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy C276 carries roughly 16% chromium, 16% molybdenum and 4% tungsten, and it is the general-purpose member of the C family: it holds up in oxidising and reducing media alike, in wet chlorine, hypochlorite, seawater and flue-gas desulphurisation. It is the default C grade — move to C22 or C2000 only where a specific environment defeats it.

  • Chemical reactors, piping, and vessels handling oxidizing and reducing acids
  • Oil & gas processing, sour environment piping, and subsea equipment
  • Marine seawater systems, desalination plants, and heat exchangers
  • Pharmaceutical equipment requiring corrosion resistance in complex chemical media
  • Power plant pollution control and heat recovery systems
