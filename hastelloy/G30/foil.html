Specifications & Standards

Hastelloy G30 foil adheres to ASTM B575 for strip and sheet, ASME SB575 Section II, DIN/EN 2.4603 (NiCr30FeMoW), excelling in wet phosphoric acid up to 105°C. Hastelloy N (UNS N10003) supports high-temperature fluoride service, tested via ASTM G36 for SCC and G28 for intergranular attack. Global standards ensure interoperability for marine and pharma.

Full traceability includes heat analysis per ASTM E1019, hardness ASTM E18, and non-destructive ET/RT for fabricator confidence.

Mechanical & Physical Properties

G30 offers 35% elongation, UTS 655 MPa min, density 8.1 g/cm³, CTE 16.3 µm/m·°C to 538°C. Excellent fabricability for pharma bellows.

N maintains strength above 700°C, with superior creep rupture for power gen turbine seals.

Why this grade

Hastelloy G30 carries about 30% chromium and is stabilised with niobium. It was developed for phosphoric acid and remains the usual choice for wet-process phosphoric acid and fertiliser plant, as well as for mixed acid streams containing nitric and hydrofluoric acid.
