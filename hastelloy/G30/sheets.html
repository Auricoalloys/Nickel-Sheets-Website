Hastelloy G30 (UNS N06030, DIN 2.4603, ASTM B574) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy engineered specifically for resistance to "wet process" phosphoric acid—an industry-critical application processing millions of tons annually for agricultural fertilizers. Its high chromium content delivers outstanding oxidation resistance to nitric acid and mixtures, while molybdenum and copper provide moderate resistance to reducing acids like hydrochloric and sulfuric. The alloy's structured composition resists grain boundary precipitate formation, enabling robust welded service.
