Hastelloy G30 (UNS N06030, DIN 2.4603, ASTM B582) is a nickel-chromium-iron alloy engineered specifically for resistance to "wet process" phosphoric acid—an industry-critical application processing millions of tons annually for agricultural fertilizers. Its high chromium content delivers outstanding oxidation resistance to nitric acid and mixtures, while molybdenum and copper provide moderate resistance to reducing acids like hydrochloric and sulfuric. The alloy's structured composition resists grain boundary precipitate formation, enabling robust welded service.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy G30 carries about 30% chromium and is stabilised with niobium. It was developed for phosphoric acid and remains the usual choice for wet-process phosphoric acid and fertiliser plant, as well as for mixed acid streams containing nitric and hydrofluoric acid.

  • Wet process phosphoric acid evaporators and concentration equipment—primary industrial use
  • Nitric acid processing and pickling operations
  • Chemical reactors and heat exchangers in oxidizing environments
  • Oil & gas refinery and petrochemical processing equipment
  • Fertilizer manufacture and agrichemical production
  • Nuclear fuel reprocessing and nuclear waste processing systems
