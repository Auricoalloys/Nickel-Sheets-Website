HASTELLOY® G-30® alloy (UNS N06030) is a nickel-chromium-iron material with alloying additions of molybdenum, tungsten, copper and niobium, developed as an improved version of alloy G-3 for superior resistance to “wet process” phosphoric acid and other oxidizing acid media. Technical summaries emphasise that G-30 shows superior corrosion resistance over most other nickel and iron-base alloys in commercial phosphoric acids, provides high resistance in nitric and nitric-containing mixtures due to its high chromium, and retains moderate resistance to reducing acids such as hydrochloric and sulfuric acids because of its molybdenum and copper contents.
