Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy C2000 combines the high molybdenum of C276 with the high chromium of C22 and adds about 1.6% copper. It is specified where one alloy has to cope with both oxidising and reducing conditions — process swings, upset conditions, or mixed acid streams — rather than changing material partway through a plant.

  • Chemical processing equipment handling strong oxidizers and reducers
  • Oil & gas process piping and pressure vessels in aggressive conditions
  • Marine applications exposed to saltwater and corrosive atmospheres
  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing and sterilization systems
  • Power generation pollution control and heat exchange components
