Hastelloy B-2

Hastelloy® B-2 (UNS N10665, W.Nr. 2.4617) is a nickel–molybdenum alloy — molybdenum 26-30% with chromium held below 1% — built for hydrochloric acid at all concentrations. The near-absence of chromium is the point rather than an omission: it is a reducing-acid alloy, and it has no useful resistance in oxidising conditions.

Key Properties

  • Molybdenum 26-30% with chromium capped at 1.0% and iron at 2.0%.
  • Hydrochloric acid at every concentration and temperature, and sulphuric and phosphoric acid besides.
  • No oxidising resistance. Ferric and cupric ions attack it; where the service is mixed or not fully known, the C series is the family to quote.
  • Hastelloy B-3 is the thermally stable successor, with the same molybdenum content and controlled chromium and iron.

Applications

  • Hydrochloric acid reactors, piping and vessels
  • Acetic acid and acetic anhydride plant
  • Hydrogenation and alkylation equipment
  • Sulphuric and phosphoric acid handling

B-2 is a hydrochloric acid alloy only while the acid stays free of oxidising species: ferric ions, cupric ions and dissolved air all raise its corrosion rate. Why, and which grades take over when they are present, is set out in our acid and alkali service guide.
