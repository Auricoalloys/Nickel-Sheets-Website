Why Hastelloy C22 Plates Are a Superior Choice

Hastelloy C22 (UNS N06022, ASTM B575) is a nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy augmented with iron and small quantities of cobalt, optimized for resistance against pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking. It maintains excellent strength and corrosion resistance in highly aggressive oxidizing and reducing environments, including hot chloride, sulfuric, and nitric acid media.

Its alloy chemistry and microstructure ensure remarkable oxidation resistance at temperatures up to 1149°C (2100°F). The alloy also demonstrates excellent weldability and fabrication versatility, supporting complex vessel and component manufacturing with minimal performance loss.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy C22 raises chromium to about 22% at the expense of tungsten. The extra chromium widens the margin in oxidising conditions — nitric acid, mixed acids, and oxidising chlorides such as ferric and cupric chloride — which is exactly where C276 is most likely to pit. It is the standard step up from C276 when the oxidising side of a mixed stream is the problem.

  • Chemical reactors, heat exchangers, and piping systems carrying strong oxidizers and reducers
  • Pollution control systems like flue gas scrubbers and waste incinerators
  • Marine desalination plants, seawater pipelines, and offshore equipment
  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing requiring highly corrosion-resistant materials
  • Power generation boilers, superheaters, and high-temperature ducting
