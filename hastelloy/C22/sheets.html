Hastelloy C22 (UNS N06022, ASTM B574, ASME SB574) is a nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy enhanced with tungsten and iron to provide superior resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking, winning favor for its versatility in both oxidizing and reducing media. Its chemical makeup, a delicate balance of chromium (20-22.5%), molybdenum (12.5-14.5%), tungsten (2.5-3.5%), and nickel (balance) enables this alloy to resist the harshest conditions encountered in industrial applications.
