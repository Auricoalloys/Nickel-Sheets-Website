Hastelloy C22 (UNS N06022, ASTM B575, ASME SB575) is a nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy enhanced with tungsten and iron to provide superior resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking, winning favor for its versatility in both oxidizing and reducing media. Its chemical makeup, a delicate balance of chromium (20-22.5%), molybdenum (12.5-14.5%), tungsten (2.5-3.5%), and nickel (balance) enables this alloy to resist the harshest conditions encountered in industrial applications.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy C22 raises chromium to about 22% at the expense of tungsten. The extra chromium widens the margin in oxidising conditions — nitric acid, mixed acids, and oxidising chlorides such as ferric and cupric chloride — which is exactly where C276 is most likely to pit. It is the standard step up from C276 when the oxidising side of a mixed stream is the problem.

  • Chemical processing plants handling oxidizing and reducing acids
  • Oil & gas sour gas pipelines, valves, and processing equipment
  • Marine heat exchangers, desalination plants, and saltwater exposure applications
  • Pharmaceutical reactors and sterile manufacturing lines
  • Power generation pollution control and high-temperature components
