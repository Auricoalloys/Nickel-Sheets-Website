    Hastelloy C22 (UNS N06022, ASTM B574, W.Nr. 2.4602) is a nickel-chromium-molybdenum-tungsten superalloy with a unique capacity to defeat pitting, crevice attack, and stress corrosion cracking in oxidizing and reducing acids, chlorine, and seawater. Outperforming C276 and high-end stainless grades, C22 offers peace of mind for industrial engineers specifying hardware or pressure vessels exposed to mixed chemical media, wet process streams, marine brines, or high-purity pharma lines.

    Applications & Benefits

    Why this grade

    Hastelloy C22 raises chromium to about 22% at the expense of tungsten. The extra chromium widens the margin in oxidising conditions — nitric acid, mixed acids, and oxidising chlorides such as ferric and cupric chloride — which is exactly where C276 is most likely to pit. It is the standard step up from C276 when the oxidising side of a mixed stream is the problem.

    • Chemical: Acid lines, reactors, evaporators, FGD, fertilizer, and mixed-media digesters.
    • Marine: Piping, fittings, seawater heat exchangers, coastal plant upgrades.
    • Oil & Gas: Downhole lines, sour service, chlorinated or acidic wells, refinery units.
    • Pharma: High-purity process vessels, reactor rods, cleanroom supports.
    • Power: Boiler tubes, condensers, flue-gas scrubbers, heat exchangers.
    • Fabrication/Supply: Fast spares for process, urgent OEM projects, major repairs in harsh chemical or marine settings.
