    Hastelloy alloys, especially grades such as B2 (UNS N10665), C276 (UNS N10276), and X (UNS N06002), are nickel-chromium-molybdenum superalloys engineered to excel where corrosion resistance and thermal stability are paramount. These materials resist pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking even in highly aggressive environments with acids, chlorides, and high-temperature oxidative media, making them ideal for chemical reactors, heat exchangers, and offshore pipelines.

    Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Hastelloy round bars globally in sizes from 6mm to 300mm in various finishes such as bright, polished, or custom-forged forms. All products come with ASTM, DIN, EN certification, and batch traceability, ensuring compliance with international standards for the most critical projects in aerospace, marine, oil & gas, and power markets.

    What sets Hastelloy apart is its unparalleled resistance to oxidation, crevice corrosion, and high-temperature oxidation, enabling OEMs, fabricators, and hardware stores to deliver components that last longer, operate safer, and perform better under demanding conditions. This makes Hastelloy the material of choice for industries that cannot compromise on safety and durability.
