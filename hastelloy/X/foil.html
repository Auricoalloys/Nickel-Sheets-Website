Specifications and Standards Overview

Hastelloy X (UNS N06002, DIN 2.4665) is specified under ASTM B435/ASME SB435 for sheet, plate, and strip (including foil), with EN 10095 (NiCr22MoNb) for high-temperature structural use. Hastelloy C2000 (UNS N06200, Werkstoff 2.4675) follows ASTM B575/ASME SB575 and EN 10088-5 (NiCr23Mo16Cu), optimized for sheet and foil in corrosive media. Both alloys ship with EN 10204 3.1 certificates, including PMI, tensile data, and grain size verification, compliant with NACE MR0175 for sour service and ASME VIII for pressure boundaries.

Industry-Specific Applications and Advantages

Why this grade

Hastelloy X is a high-temperature alloy rather than a wet-corrosion one. It holds oxidation resistance to around 1200°C and is equally at home in reducing, carburising and oxidising furnace atmospheres, which is why it has long been the choice for gas turbine combustors, transition ducts and industrial furnace hardware.

Power Generation & Gas Turbines

Hastelloy X foil forms combustion liners and transition ducts, resisting oxidation up to 1200 °C where Inconel 718 creeps prematurely extending intervals 30–50%.

Chemical Processing

C2000 foil seals reactors handling H2SO4-HCl mixes, outperforming C-276 in oxidizing shifts; X foil shields crackers from carburization.

Oil & Gas

C2000 in sour wet gas (NACE compliant); X in flare stacks and HT ducts, reducing failures vs. RA333.

Marine & Pharma

C2000 foil for seawater evaporators and sterile vessels (FDA); X for exhaust heat exchangers.

FAQ: Key Insights on Hastelloy X and C2000 Foil

Hastelloy X (UNS N06002, ASTM B435) excels in static oxidation and carburization up to 1204 °C with good fabricability, ideal for gas turbine liners, furnace muffles, and reformer baffles—chosen over Inconel 718 when PWHT is impractical and creep strength at 1100 °C suffices without age-hardening.

C2000 (UNS N06200, ASTM B575) adds 1.3–1.9% Cu to high Mo-Cr for superior sulfuric acid and oxidizing-reducing resistance (PREN ~55), resisting pitting where C-276 sensitizes; weldable without PWHT, per EN 10088-5, for chemical reactors and FGD ducts.

EN 10204 3.1 MTCs detail chemistry, RT/HT tensile, hardness, grain size; PMI (XRF); NACE MR0175 sour service; ASME IX weld quals; optional ASTM G48 pitting, G28 crevice tests, helium leak for pharma. Full melt traceability and RoHS/REACH for exports.

Both offer excellent cold formability in annealed state (elongation >35%); X foil bends sharply for combustion baffles; C2000 stamps easily for gaskets. GTAW/GMAW welding with matching fillers (ERNiCrMo-14 for X, ERNiCrMo-22 for C2000) without cracking or sensitization.
