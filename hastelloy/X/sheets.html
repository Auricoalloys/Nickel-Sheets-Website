Hastelloy X (UNS N06002, ASTM B435, ASME SB435) is a nickel-chromium-iron-molybdenum alloy strengthened against heat and corrosion up to 2200°F. Its balanced composition gives it excellent oxidation, carburization, and nitriding resistance, as well as notable resistance to chloride-induced stress corrosion cracking. This combination makes Hastelloy X a favorite for combustion zone components in gas turbines, chemical reactor parts, and industrial furnace equipment.
