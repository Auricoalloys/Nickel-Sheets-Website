Hastelloy B3 (UNS N10675, ASTM B333) is a nickel-molybdenum superalloy with improved structural stability compared to similar alloys, formulated to withstand hydrochloric, sulfuric, acetic, phosphoric, and formic acids across a wide temperature range. Its chemical composition and heat treatment reduce unwanted precipitates like Ni4Mo, enhancing weldability and component life.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Hastelloy B3 resists the same reducing acids as B2, but its composition was reformulated to suppress the intermetallic precipitation that embrittles B2 in the 550–900°C range. The result is far better thermal stability, so welded and formed sections keep their ductility in service. Specify B3 wherever B2 chemistry is wanted in new fabrication.

  • Chemical reactors and heat exchangers processing oxidizing and reducing acids
  • Oil & gas components exposed to highly aggressive sour environments
  • Marine desalination and seawater cooling systems
  • Pharmaceutical process vessels and sterilization equipment
  • Power generation heat exchangers and emission control equipment
