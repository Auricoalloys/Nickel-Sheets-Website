Should I use maraging steel or H13 powder?

Maraging 18Ni300 prints soft, machines easily, then ages at 490 C to over 1900 MPa with minimal distortion, which suits conformal-cooled injection mould inserts. H13 is the better choice when the tool sees sustained heat and thermal cycling, as in die casting, because it keeps hardness at temperature.
