H13 Tool Steel Powder Supplier and Exporter

H13 Tool Steel powder (T20813) is a argon gas atomised hot-work tool steel feedstock for additive manufacturing, supplied to ASTM A681 for the wrought equivalent in 15-45 µm, 15-53 µm, 45-106 µm, 53-150 µm size fractions.

Holds hardness at temperature and resists thermal fatigue, so it survives the heat-and-quench cycling that destroys ordinary tool steel in die casting.

What Is H13 Powder Used For?

Die-casting dies, hot forging tooling, extrusion dies, and repair cladding of worn tool surfaces.

Post-processing

Preheated build plate to control cracking, then austenitise, quench and double temper.

Should I use H13 or maraging steel powder?

Use H13 where the tool runs hot and cycles, as in die casting, hot forging and extrusion, because it holds hardness at temperature and resists thermal fatigue. Use maraging 18Ni300 for injection mould inserts, where you want extreme hardness, easy machining before ageing and almost no distortion during it. H13 for heat, maraging for hardness and dimensional stability.
