Export Markets

Aurico Alloys LLP ships nickel alloys, titanium, duplex and super duplex stainless and cobalt alloys from our warehouses in Mumbai and Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai to customers worldwide. Each market below has its own conformity requirements, discharge ports and industrial profile, and the pages set out what applies where.

Every consignment carries an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate as standard, with 3.2 certification and third-party inspection by Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, SGS or TUV available on request. We quote ex-works, FOB or CIF to your named port.
