Incoloy 903

Incoloy® 903 (UNS N19903) is a controlled-expansion superalloy — nickel 36-40%, cobalt 13-17%, niobium 2.40-3.50% and titanium 1.00-1.85% on an iron balance. It combines low thermal expansion with a near-constant modulus across its working range, which is what gas turbine sealing and casing hardware is chosen for.

Key Properties

  • Low, controlled thermal expansion with a near-constant modulus — the property the grade exists for.
  • Nickel 36-40%, cobalt 13-17%, niobium 2.40-3.50%, titanium 1.00-1.85%, iron balance.
  • Age-hardenable, with aluminium 0.30-1.15%.
  • No ASTM standard is published for it. Special Metals supplies it to its own specification, which is what the specification table below records as mill.

Applications

  • Gas turbine casings and seal rings
  • Shafts and housings needing dimensional stability
  • Rings and spacers where clearance must hold with temperature
