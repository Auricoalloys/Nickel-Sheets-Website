Incoloy 825

Incoloy® 825 (UNS N08825, W.Nr. 2.4858) is a nickel–iron–chromium alloy with molybdenum, copper and titanium added — molybdenum 2.5-3.5%, copper 1.5-3.0% and titanium 0.6-1.2%. The molybdenum and copper are what give it resistance to sulphuric and phosphoric acids, and the titanium stabilises it against sensitisation when welded.

Key Properties

  • Molybdenum 2.5-3.5% with copper 1.5-3.0% — the pair is what makes it a sulphuric and phosphoric acid alloy rather than a general-purpose one.
  • Nickel 38-46%, chromium 19.5-23.5%, giving resistance in reducing and oxidising conditions alike.
  • Titanium 0.6-1.2% stabilises against intergranular attack in the weld heat-affected zone.
  • Incoloy 925 is the age-hardenable version of this same corrosion chemistry.

Applications

  • Pickling tank heaters and equipment
  • Phosphoric acid evaporators
  • Chemical process piping and vessels
  • Oil and gas production hardware

Incoloy 825 is excellent in pure phosphoric acid up to boiling 85% and in nitric acid up to boiling 65%, and in dilute sulphuric acid Special Metals ranks it ahead of Hastelloy C-276. Its limits, and the grades to move to when chlorides are present, are in our acid and alkali service guide.
