Incoloy 825 Wire Supplier, Stockist and Exporter

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies incoloy 825 wire (N08825, Werkstoff 2.4858) from ready stock in Mumbai, to customers across India and in more than 40 export markets. Wire is bought on diameter and temper, and those two decide whether a coil is any use for the job long before anything on the certificate does.

Incoloy 825 is stabilised with titanium and carries copper and molybdenum, which is why it holds up in sulphuric and phosphoric acid service where a plain austenitic stainless will not. As wire it is used where the filler has to match that resistance across a weld.
