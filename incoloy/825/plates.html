This nickel‑iron‑chromium alloy (UNS N08825) gives procurement heads, design engineers, and fabricators a stable, code‑approved solution where 316L, 904L, even Alloy 20 struggle with mixed acids, chlorides, and elevated temperature.

Incoloy 825 combines 38–46% nickel with chromium, molybdenum, copper, and titanium to resist general corrosion, pitting, crevice attack, and chloride‑ion stress‑corrosion cracking in both oxidizing and reducing media such as sulfuric, phosphoric, and nitric acids plus seawater and sour gas.
