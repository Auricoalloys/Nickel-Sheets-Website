Why this grade

Incoloy 825 is not a high-temperature grade at all. It takes the 800 base and adds about 3% molybdenum, 2% copper and titanium stabilisation, producing the wet-corrosion member of the family: sulphuric and phosphoric acid duty, and the resistance to chloride stress-corrosion cracking that 316L does not have.

  • Chemical: Sulfuric/phosphoric acid reactors, FGD scrubbers, pickling tanks
  • Oil & Gas: Sour gas piping, downhole tubing, H2S service
  • Marine: Seawater piping, desalination plants, offshore platforms
  • Pharma: Mixing vessels, sterile processing equipment
  • Power: Pollution control stacks, flue gas desulfurization

FAQ 825 vs 316L/625 Corrosion

825 offers 30x better pitting resistance (PREN 31 vs PREN 19) and resists SCC in chloride/sulfuric acid where 316L fails rapidly.

825 costs 40% less with equivalent sulfuric/phosphoric acid resistance; 625 needed only for extreme HF/HCl service.
