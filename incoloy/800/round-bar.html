    Incoloy 800 (UNS N08800, ASTM B408) is a nickel-iron-chromium alloy specially formulated for excellent oxidation resistance and microstructural stability up to 1100°C. Its balanced chemistry delivers superb resistance to carburization and thermal fatigue, while maintaining adequate ductility and weldability. Commonly used in heaters, reformers, heat exchangers, and steam generators, it outperforms standard stainless steels in severe industrial conditions.

    Applications & Benefits

    Why this grade

    Incoloy 800 is a 32% nickel, 21% chromium alloy on an iron balance. It is the general-purpose member of the family for heat and corrosion resistance up to about 650°C, and the least expensive of the 800 series. Above that temperature its creep strength falls away and 800H or 800HT applies instead.

    • Chemical: Heat exchangers, furnace components, reformers operating in moderately oxidizing atmospheres.
    • Marine: Seawater pumps, condensers, piping with moderate corrosion resistance requirements.
    • Oil & Gas: Equipment exposed to sour gas with good corrosion resistance and mechanical properties.
    • Pharma: Process vessels requiring resistance to heat and corrosion.
    • Power: Boilers, superheater tubes, and exhausts needing thermal and oxidation resistance.
    • OEM & Fabricators: Ready stock with certifications, ensuring high quality for industrial maintenance and fabrication.
