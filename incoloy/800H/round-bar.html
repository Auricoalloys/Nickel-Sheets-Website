    Incoloy 800H (UNS N08810, ASTM B408) is a heat-resistant nickel-iron-chromium alloy with enhanced carbon content for improved creep strength. It maintains excellent oxidation resistance and mechanical stability at temperatures up to 1150°C, making it ideal for heat exchangers, furnace components, and high-temperature piping. Compared to Incoloy 800, grade 800H provides greater tensile and yield strengths, addressing the demands of high-stress environments.

    Applications & Benefits

    Why this grade

    Incoloy 800H shares the chemistry of 800 with two deliberate changes: carbon is controlled to 0.05–0.10% and the solution anneal is carried out at a higher temperature to give a coarser grain. Both raise creep and stress-rupture strength, which is why 800H rather than 800 is the grade for ASME pressure service above 650°C.

    • Chemical: Heat exchangers, reformers, furnace parts with oxidation and corrosion resistance.
    • Marine: Saltwater piping, condensers, cooling systems.
    • Oil & Gas: Hydrocarbon processing equipment requiring thermal and corrosion resilience.
    • Pharma: Sterilization vessels and clean room processing hardware.
    • Power: Boiler tubes, superheater components exposed to high temperature and stress cycling.
    • OEM & Fabrication: Certified quality for urgent supply, precision machining, and critical projects.
