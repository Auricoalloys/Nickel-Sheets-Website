Why this grade

Incoloy 800H shares the chemistry of 800 with two deliberate changes: carbon is controlled to 0.05–0.10% and the solution anneal is carried out at a higher temperature to give a coarser grain. Both raise creep and stress-rupture strength, which is why 800H rather than 800 is the grade for ASME pressure service above 650°C.

  • Chemical: Ethylene pyrolysis tubes, acetic acid plants, FGD systems
  • Power: Superheater/reheater tubing, boiler components up to 1100°C
  • Oil & Gas: Hydrocarbon cracking furnaces, reformer tubing
  • Marine: Exhaust systems, heat exchanger plates
  • Pharma: High-temp reaction vessels, sterilization equipment
