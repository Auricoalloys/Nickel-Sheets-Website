    Incoloy alloys, including grades 800 (UNS N08800, W.Nr. 1.4876) and 825 (UNS N08825, W.Nr. 2.4858), offer balanced nickel, chromium, and iron compositions that bridge the gap between standard stainless steels and expensive nickel alloys. Incoloy 800 provides excellent high-temperature strength up to 1500°F (816°C), while Incoloy 825 adds molybdenum and copper for superior resistance to pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress corrosion cracking in acidic and chloride environments. These alloys resist oxidation, carburization, sulfidation, and chloride attack, ideal for heat exchangers, pressure vessels, piping, and chemical processing equipment.

    Incoloy round bars are supplied globally per ASTM B408, ASTM B425, EN 10295, and DIN 17470 standards, available in diameters from 3mm to 800mm, with bright, polished, peeled, and custom finishes. Full mill test certificates, NACE MR0175 compliance, and batch traceability support demanding projects in petrochemical, oil & gas, marine, and pharmaceutical sectors. These bars are easily welded, machined, and fabricated, making them the preferred choice for OEMs, fabricators, and hardware suppliers worldwide.

    As a trusted Incoloy Round Bars supplier, manufacturer, and stockist, Aurico Alloys LLP provides affordable, best-quality, authentic materials for chemical, oil & gas, marine, pharma, and power generation industries, along with hardware and industrial supply stores ensuring certified reliability and global compliance.
