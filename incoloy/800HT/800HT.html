Incoloy 800HT

Incoloy® 800HT (UNS N08811, W.Nr. 1.4959) takes 800H's controlled carbon and adds a tightened aluminium and titanium window — 0.25 to 0.60% each, against 0.15 to 0.60% on 800 and 800H — with their combined total controlled as well. That is what gives it the highest creep-rupture strength of the three.

Key Properties

  • Aluminium 0.25-0.60% and titanium 0.25-0.60%, both with a minimum, and the sum of the two controlled rather than each alone.
  • Carbon 0.06-0.10%, on the same basis as 800H.
  • The highest creep-rupture strength of Incoloy 800, 800H and 800HT.
  • Same standards as the rest of the family — ASTM B409 / B906 flat, B408 bar, B163 / B407 / B829 tube.

Applications

  • Ethylene furnace quench boilers
  • Hydrocarbon cracker tube supports and hangers
  • High-temperature process piping
  • Radiant tubes and furnace internals

Available Forms

  • Incoloy 800HT Plates
  • Incoloy 800HT Round Bars
  • Incoloy 800HT Sheets
