Incoloy 800HT surpasses 800H with higher creep rupture strength (20% better at 1000°C) due to stabilized microstructure from elevated aluminum+titanium and precise carbon control (0.06-0.10%), preventing grain coarsening that compromises standard alloys above 900°C.

Unlike stainless steels that suffer rapid oxidation (>1mm/year) and carbide precipitation in sulfurous furnace atmospheres, Incoloy 800HT maintains <0.1mm/year corrosion with full mechanical integrity through 1100°C thermal cycling essential for petrochemical reformers and power plant reheaters.
