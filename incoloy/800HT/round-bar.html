    Incoloy 800HT (UNS N08811, ASTM B407) is a nickel-iron-chromium alloy with controlled carbon content developed to enhance creep and stress rupture properties at elevated temperatures up to 1100°C. This makes it a preferred choice for high-temperature furnace components, heat exchangers, and pressure vessels where oxidation resistance, stability, and mechanical strength are crucial. Compared to the Incoloy 800, the 800HT variant offers improved tensile strength and creep resistance without compromising corrosion protection.

    Aurico Alloys LLP supplies Incoloy 800HT Round Bars in diameters from 6 to 350mm, available in bright, ground, and polished finishes.
