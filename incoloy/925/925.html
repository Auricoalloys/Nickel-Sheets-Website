Incoloy® 925 (UNS N09925, W.Nr. 2.4852) is Incoloy 825's corrosion chemistry made age-hardenable — titanium raised to 1.9-2.4% and aluminium to 0.1-0.5%, which precipitate on ageing to give strength 825 cannot reach. It is qualified for sour service under NACE MR-01-75.

Key Properties

  • Age-hardenable. Titanium 1.9-2.4% and aluminium 0.1-0.5% are the additions Incoloy 825 does not have.
  • 825's corrosion base retained — nickel 42-46%, chromium 19.5-22.5%, molybdenum 2.5-3.5%, copper 1.5-3.0%.
  • NACE MR-01-75 for sour service, with ASME Code Case 2218.
  • Incoloy 825 is the alternative where the solution-annealed condition is enough and strength is not the constraint.

Applications

  • Downhole hangers, packers and completion hardware
  • Valves and fasteners in sour service
  • Wellhead components
  • Chemical process equipment needing strength with 825 corrosion resistance
