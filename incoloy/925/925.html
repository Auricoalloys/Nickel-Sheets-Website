Why This Grade

Incoloy 925 is the corrosion chemistry of 825 made age-hardenable with titanium and aluminium, so it delivers the same acid and chloride resistance at far higher strength. It is the sour-service oilfield grade — hangers, valves and packers. Its ageing response is deliberately slow, which lets heavy sections be heated and cooled during annealing without cracking.
