Why Incoloy 330 Sheets Are the Industry Standard for Extremes

Incoloy 330 (UNS N08330, ASTM B536) is a high-performance nickel-iron-chromium alloy explicitly designed for its formidable resistance to oxidizing and reducing media. It features excellent stability in high-temperature environments, especially in sulfuric acid and hydrochloric acid, making it an ideal choice for coolant systems, heat exchangers, and chemical reactor lining.

This alloy’s composition includes approximately 33% nickel, 20% chromium, and 8-10% iron, with minor additions of molybdenum and copper to bolster resistance to general corrosion, stress corrosion cracking, and high-temperature oxidation. Its proven reliability is backed by ASTM B536 standards, with certifications ensuring compliance for critical sectors such as chemical processing, marine, pharmaceutical, and power plants.

Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Incoloy 330 is a 35% nickel, 19% chromium alloy carrying about 1.2% silicon, and the silicon is the point — it confers the carburisation and thermal-shock resistance that 800 lacks. That makes it the furnace-hardware grade: radiant tubes, retorts, muffles and heat-treatment fixtures cycling to around 1150°C.

  • Furnace components in high-temperature oxidizing atmospheres
  • Heat exchangers, reformers, and pressure vessels in power plants
  • Sour and alkali-resistant piping for chemical industries
  • Marine seawater systems, including condenser and cooling pipes
  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing equipment with high corrosion resistance
