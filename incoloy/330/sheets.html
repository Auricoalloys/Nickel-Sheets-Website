Why Incoloy 330 Sheets Are the Industry Standard for Extremes

Incoloy 330 (UNS N08330, ASTM B163) is a high-performance nickel-iron-chromium alloy explicitly designed for its formidable resistance to oxidizing and reducing media. It features excellent stability in high-temperature environments, especially in sulfuric acid and hydrochloric acid, making it an ideal choice for coolant systems, heat exchangers, and chemical reactor lining.

This alloy’s composition includes approximately 33% nickel, 20% chromium, and 8-10% iron, with minor additions of molybdenum and copper to bolster resistance to general corrosion, stress corrosion cracking, and high-temperature oxidation. Its proven reliability is backed by ASTM B163 standards, with certifications ensuring compliance for critical sectors such as chemical processing, marine, pharmaceutical, and power plants.
