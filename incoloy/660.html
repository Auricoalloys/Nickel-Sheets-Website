Incoloy® 660

Incoloy® 660, better known as A-286, is an age-hardenable iron-nickel-chromium superalloy that keeps useful strength to around 700 °C. It is widely used for turbine fasteners, discs and non-rotating engine hardware where a fully nickel-based alloy is not warranted.
