Incoloy® 660

Incoloy® 660 (UNS S66286), better known as A-286, is an age-hardenable iron-base superalloy — chromium 13.5-16%, nickel 24.0-27.0%, titanium 1.90-2.35% and molybdenum 1.0-1.5%. The titanium is what hardens it, and it keeps useful strength to around 700 °C on an iron base, which is why it is specified where a fully nickel-based alloy is not warranted.

Key Properties

  • Iron base, not a nickel alloy. Chromium 13.5-16% with nickel 24.0-27.0% is an austenitic stainless composition, and it prices accordingly.
  • Age-hardened by titanium 1.90-2.35%, with aluminium capped at 0.35%.
  • Useful strength to about 700 °C, above which the nickel-based grades take over.
  • AMS 5525 / 5858 for sheet and plate; ASTM A638 and AMS 5731 / 5732 / 5734 / 5737 for bar.

Applications

  • Turbine fasteners, bolting and studs
  • Turbine discs and non-rotating engine hardware
  • Springs and retaining rings at temperature
  • Fasteners for automotive and aerospace exhaust

Available Forms

  • Incoloy® 660 Plates
  • Incoloy® 660 Round Bars
  • Incoloy® 660 Sheets
