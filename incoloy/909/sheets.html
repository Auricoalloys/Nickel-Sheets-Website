Why this grade

Incoloy 909 is the silicon-bearing development of 903. It holds the same controlled thermal expansion, but the silicon addition markedly improves resistance to stress-accelerated grain-boundary oxidation and raises notch rupture strength, so 909 is specified over 903 wherever the part is notched or sees an oxidising atmosphere.

  • Incoloy 909 for gas turbine casings: Outer casings matching rotor CTE to 730°C, eliminating blade tip interference
  • High-strength alloy for steam turbine bolts: HP/IP turbine casing bolts maintaining preload through 720°C cycles
  • Incoloy 909 rocket engine thrust chambers: Cryogenic fuel jackets to 800°C combustion with zero distortion
  • Incoloy 909 for ordnance hardware: Missile guidance fins surviving launch to re-entry thermal shock
  • Chemical: High-pressure autoclaves cycling -50°C to 700°C
  • Oil & Gas: Deepwater riser tensioner cylinders
