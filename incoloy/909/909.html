Incoloy 909

Incoloy® 909 (UNS N19909) is a controlled-expansion superalloy strengthened with niobium at 4.3-5.2%, with aluminium deliberately held to 0.15% maximum. That low aluminium is what distinguishes it from Incoloy 903, and it is supplied to AMS 5892 and AMS 5893.

Key Properties

  • Niobium 4.3-5.2% with titanium 1.3-1.8% and cobalt 12-16%.
  • Aluminium capped at 0.15%. Incoloy 903 carries up to 1.15%, and that difference is the reason to specify one over the other.
  • Low, controlled thermal expansion with a constant modulus.
  • AMS 5893 for flat product and AMS 5892 / 5884 for bar.

Applications

  • Gas turbine casings, rings and seals
  • Steam turbine rotor components
  • Shafts and spacers requiring dimensional stability

Available Forms

  • Incoloy 909 Plates
  • Incoloy 909 Round Bars
  • Incoloy 909 Sheets
