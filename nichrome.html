Nichrome Alloys

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nichrome resistance alloys from Mumbai in a full range of forms — sheet, plate, coil, strip, foil, bar, wire and tube. Held in stock or arranged with the manufacturer, depending on grade and quantity.

Nichrome is nickel-chromium wound or formed into something that is meant to get hot and stay intact. Its usefulness rests on three properties at once: high electrical resistivity, so a manageable length gives the wanted resistance; a resistance that barely changes as the element heats; and a tight, adherent chromium oxide skin that stops the element burning away.

Grades are named by their nickel-to-chromium ratio, and the ratio is the whole specification. More nickel means a higher usable temperature and a longer life, and costs more. The lower-nickel grades substitute iron, which is cheaper and works perfectly well at lower temperatures.

What Is Nichrome? Composition, Melting Point and Properties

What is nichrome made of?

Nichrome is a family of nickel-chromium alloys made for electrical resistance heating, and its grades are named by their nickel-to-chromium ratio. The nickel-based grades are NiCr 80/20 (chromium 19–21%, nickel the balance), NiCr 70/30 (chromium 29–31%, nickel the balance) and NiCr 60/15 (chromium 14–17%, at least 57% nickel, iron the balance). The iron-bearing grades trade nickel for iron: NiCr 40/20 (chromium 18–22%, at least 37% nickel, iron the balance), NiCr 30/20 (chromium 18–22%, at least 28% nickel, iron the balance) and NiCr 20/25 (chromium 23–27%, at least 18% nickel, iron the balance).

What is the melting point of nichrome?

NiCr 80/20 and NiCr 70/30 melt at about 1,400 °C, and NiCr 60/15 melts at about 1,390 °C, according to the manufacturer's data sheets. The data sheet for NiCr 40/20, NiCr 30/20 and NiCr 20/25 publishes no melting point, so none is stated here. The temperature an element can be run at in air is lower than the melting point: the data sheets give 1,200 °C for NiCr 80/20 as a heating element, 600 °C for NiCr 80/20 as a resistance alloy and approximately 1,250 °C for NiCr 70/30.
