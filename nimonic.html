Nimonic Alloys Supplier, Stockist and Exporter

Aurico Alloys LLP supplies nine Nimonic® grades from Mumbai, in sheet, plate, round bar and foil. Held in stock or arranged with the manufacturer, depending on grade and quantity.

Nimonic is a family of nickel-chromium superalloys developed for service where the load is carried at temperature rather than in spite of it — gas turbine hot sections above all. The family divides cleanly in two. The solid-solution grades take their strength from the alloying elements in the matrix and stay easy to form and weld. The age-hardened grades add titanium and aluminium, which precipitate a strengthening phase during heat treatment and buy a great deal of creep strength at the cost of being harder to fabricate.

Where Nimonic Is Used

Almost everything Nimonic does comes back to holding strength where a steel or a stainless would have crept or scaled away:

  • Gas turbines — blades, discs, rings and casings in the hot section
  • Combustion systems — combustion chambers and flame tubes, usually in 263 where the part is fabricated from sheet and welded
  • Automotive and diesel — exhaust valves, turbocharger components
  • Hot working tooling — dies and tools that stay loaded while hot
  • Aerospace fasteners — bolts and rings that must not relax at temperature
  • Furnace and heat treatment — fixtures, retorts and muffles
