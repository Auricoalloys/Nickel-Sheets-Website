Combustion Chamber Oxidation Forces Costly Retrofit

A petrochemical reformer in the Middle East faced emergency shutdown when oxidation scaled through liner foils after 15,000 hours at 1050°C. Standard alloys embrittled under carburizing gases and thermal cycles, costing $3.2 million in replacements and lost production. Metallurgy review highlighted inadequate Cr/Mo for scale adherence leading fabricators to Nimonic 86, 263, and 901 foils. These standards-compliant superalloys with optimized chemistry ensure prolonged integrity in power, chemical, and gas turbine service.

Such resilience addresses the 45% of reformer failures tied to material degradation.

Equivalent Grades Across Global Standards
