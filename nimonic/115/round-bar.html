    Nimonic 115 (W. Nr. 2.4636/2.4969, BS HR4) is a high-strength nickel-chromium-cobalt alloy, further strengthened by molybdenum, aluminum, and titanium additions. It offers exceptional stability at >1000°C, holding a tensile strength around 1300 MPa, yield >850 MPa, and elongation at break near 25%. Its microstructure prevents embrittlement and guarantees outstanding performance during continuous thermal cycling, making it essential for gas turbines, reactor cores, and marine propulsion.

    Round bars come in sizes from 6mm to 200mm diameter, as bright, ground, or rough-turned, with supplies meeting BS HR 4, AECMA, DIN and NACE MR0175 standards.     Applications & Benefits

    Why this grade

    Nimonic 115 is the strongest of the wrought Nimonic series, taking blade service to roughly 1000°C. It is ordered where nothing lower in the range will hold the stress, and it is correspondingly the most difficult of the family to work and to weld.

    • Chemical: Reformers, digester hardware, heat exchangers.
    • Marine: Turbine shafts, seawater heat exchanger tubes, anti-corrosion fasteners.
    • Oil & Gas: Gas turbine blades, hot valve parts, wellhead hardware.
    • Pharma: Sterile plant hardware, incinerator fasteners.
    • Power: Boiler tubes, combustor linings, high-temperature rods.
    • Hardware: Fasteners, die cast inserts, support rods.
