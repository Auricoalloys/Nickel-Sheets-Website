Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Nimonic 115 is the strongest of the wrought Nimonic series, taking blade service to roughly 1000°C. It is ordered where nothing lower in the range will hold the stress, and it is correspondingly the most difficult of the family to work and to weld.

  • Chemical Processing: High-temperature reactors, catalyst supports, and reformer tubes requiring superior hot corrosion resistance.
  • Oil & Gas Industry: Gas turbine blades, combustion chamber liners, and heat exchanger components enduring cyclic thermal fatigue.
  • Marine Applications: Turbine blades and high-stress fasteners with outstanding oxidation resistance.
  • Pharmaceutical & Power: Precision components for high-temperature furnaces and gas turbines ensuring reliable performance over long durations.
  • Hardware & OEMs: Stock for high-temperature fasteners, precision cut sheet blanks, and custom fabrication needs.

FAQ & Search Trends

Nimonic 115 has increased cobalt and titanium levels delivering enhanced creep resistance and tensile strength at elevated temperatures compared to Nimonic 80A and 81. It is best suited for demanding gas turbine and chemical process applications.

Welding typically uses matching filler metals with controlled heat input, followed by solution annealing at 1040-1080°C and precipitation aging around 700°C to optimize mechanical properties and corrosion resistance.
