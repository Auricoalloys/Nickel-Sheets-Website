Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Nimonic 80A is the same 80/20 nickel-chromium base made age-hardenable with titanium and aluminium, which precipitate gamma prime. It carries far more strength at temperature than 75 and has long been the choice for automotive and diesel exhaust valves and for turbine bolting to around 815°C. The trade-off is fabrication: it needs solution treatment before welding and a post-weld age, or it strain-age cracks.

  • Power & Turbines: ASTM B637 Nimonic 80A plates for high-stress exhaust valves and spindle bolts; proven in gas turbine blades, rings and discs where sustained 700–815°C service rules out stainless steels.
  • Oil, Gas & Marine: Corrosion resistant Nimonic 80A plates for offshore platform gas turbine hot-sections, UNS N07080 nickel alloy plates for oilfield downhole tools in high-temperature wells, and marine-grade engine valve components.
  • Chemical & Pharma: Nimonic 80A plates for chemical reactors handling high-temperature oxidizing media and high-purity Alloy 80A plates for pharmaceutical sterilization equipment fabrication where oxidizing steam cycles can exceed 650°C.
  • Furnaces & Tooling: Custom cut-to-size Nimonic 80A plates for industrial furnace hearth furniture and die-casting inserts, providing better thermal fatigue resistance than tool steels or Inconel 600 in repeated heat cycles.
