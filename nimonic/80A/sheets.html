Applications & Why Choose Nimonic 80A

Why this grade

Nimonic 80A is the same 80/20 nickel-chromium base made age-hardenable with titanium and aluminium, which precipitate gamma prime. It carries far more strength at temperature than 75 and has long been the choice for automotive and diesel exhaust valves and for turbine bolting to around 815°C. The trade-off is fabrication: it needs solution treatment before welding and a post-weld age, or it strain-age cracks.

  • Chemical Processing: Reaction vessels, heat exchangers – superior to Nimonic 75 in oxidizing acids
  • Oil & Gas: Downhole components, sour service – exceptional Nimonic 80A creep resistance
  • Marine: Exhaust systems, turbine housings – better than Inconel X-750 at cyclic thermal loads
  • Pharma: Sterilization equipment – maintains Nimonic 80A high tensile strength in cleanroom conditions
  • Power Generation: Gas turbine blades, combustion chamber components – industry standard
  • Hardware Stores/OEM: Die casting inserts, high-temperature fasteners from ready stock
