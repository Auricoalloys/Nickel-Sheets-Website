Why This Grade

Nimonic 263 is the one grade in the range designed to be welded. Its aluminium plus titanium content is deliberately held below that of 105 and 115, so a fabricated assembly can be aged without cracking while still carrying useful strength to about 900°C. That is why combustion liners, transition pieces, casings and exhaust rings — anything built up from sheet and welded — are specified in 263 rather than in a stronger grade.
