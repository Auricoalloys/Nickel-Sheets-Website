Applications & Benefits

Why this grade

Nimonic 263 is the one grade in the range designed to be welded. Its aluminium plus titanium content is deliberately held below that of 105 and 115, so a fabricated assembly can be aged without cracking while still carrying useful strength to about 900°C. That is why combustion liners, transition pieces, casings and exhaust rings — anything built up from sheet and welded — are specified in 263 rather than in a stronger grade.

  • Aerospace: AMS 5872 Nimonic 263 plates for aircraft engine exhaust ducts, transition liners, high-stress sheet fabrications.
  • Power/Oil & Gas: High-ductility Alloy 263 plates for welded assemblies in land-based turbines, low-temperature combustors, seals; corrosion-resistant for oil refinery waste heat recovery, offshore flare tips.
  • Marine: Marine grade Nimonic 263 plates for offshore platform turbine hot-sections, subsea propulsion components.
  • Chemical & Pharma: Nimonic 263 plates for chemical reactors requiring high-temperature weld integrity, durable pharma high-heat incineration, custom cut for heat exchangers.
  • Why Superior: Nimonic 263 vs Waspaloy easier fabrication/welding, better ductility for OEMs/hardware stores.
