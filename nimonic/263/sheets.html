FAQ & Search Trends

Nimonic 263 offers higher cobalt and molybdenum contents, delivering improved creep resistance and oxidation resistance especially at temperatures above 850°C, making it more suitable for advanced turbine and chemical applications.

Use compatible nickel-based filler metals with preheat and post-weld heat treatment to restore age-hardened properties and prevent cracking. Controlled heat input is crucial to maintain weld integrity.
