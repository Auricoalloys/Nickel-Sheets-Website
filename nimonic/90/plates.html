Nimonic 90 (UNS N07090, W.Nr. 2.4632, NiCr20Co18Ti) is a wrought, age‑hardened nickel‑chromium‑cobalt alloy strengthened by titanium and aluminum additions, designed for high stress‑rupture strength and creep resistance up to 920°C with good oxidation resistance.
