Applications & Why Choose Nimonic 90

Why this grade

Nimonic 90 is 80A with roughly 16–20% cobalt added. The cobalt lifts useful service temperature by about 100°C over 80A, which is what makes it the grade for turbine blades and discs in the cooler stages, and for hot-working tooling — duty where 80A has run out of creep strength but the cost and difficulty of 105 or 115 is not justified.

  • Chemical: Reactor internals, cracking furnace tubes – Nimonic 90 creep resistance 2x better than 800H
  • Oil & Gas: Steam reformer tubesheets, cracking coils – superior to Nimonic 81 vs Nimonic 80A in sustained load
  • Marine: Gas turbine blades, propulsion components – exceptional fatigue resistance
  • Pharma: High-purity heat treatment baskets – maintains strength post-age hardening
  • Power: Nimonic 90 gas turbine blades, rotor discs – industry proven
  • Hardware/OEM: Precision sheet for high-stress fasteners from ready stock
