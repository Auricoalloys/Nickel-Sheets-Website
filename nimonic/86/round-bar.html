    Nimonic 86 (UNS N07086, W. Nr. 2.4961) stands apart as a solid-solution strengthened nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy fortified by rare earth (cerium), cobalt, and titanium additions for maximum oxidation, scaling, and cyclic fatigue resistance at up to 1100°C. Unlike age-hardenable grades, its performance is built into its chemistry, making it a first-choice for burner liners, piping, and furnace hardware exposed to sustained thermal and corrosion cycles.

    Available in diameters from 6mm to 200mm, Nimonic 86 round bars arrive in bright, rough-turned, or ground finish. These bars meet ASTM, AMS 5608, BS HR201, and NACE MR0175 criteria, accompanied by full mill test certificates and global traceability. All Nimonic 86 Round Bars come with rapid shipment options and expert support for urgent projects and routine overhauls.

    Industrial buyers prefer Nimonic 86 for affordable, high-quality, and authenticated supply protecting installations in chemical digesters, marine pump shafts, high-temp process tubes, and high-pressure energy lines, while achieving real savings through fewer shutdowns and repairs.
