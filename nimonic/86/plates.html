Nimonic 86 is a solid‑solution Ni‑Cr‑Mo alloy with cerium and magnesium, designed for exceptionally good cyclic oxidation resistance at 1050°C, while Nimonic 75 and 80A offer lower Mo and less optimized oxide behavior, so they scale faster and spall more under severe thermal cycling.
