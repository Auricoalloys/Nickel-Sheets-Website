FAQ & Search Trends

Nimonic 86 is a solid‑solution Ni‑Cr‑Mo alloy with cerium and magnesium, designed for exceptionally good cyclic oxidation resistance at 1050°C, while Nimonic 75 and 80A offer lower Mo and less optimized oxide behavior, so they scale faster and spall more under severe thermal cycling.

Published price tables show Nimonic 86 plate in India at roughly ₹1,200/kg for Indian origin, ~₹1,400–₹1,600/kg for Japanese, European, or US origin, and some local offers around ₹2,999/kg for 5 mm plate in Mumbai, with final price depending on thickness, finish, and documentation.

Nimonic 86 is a solid‑solution strengthened alloy rather than an age‑hardenable grade; its strength comes from Ni‑Cr‑Mo plus Ce and Mg rather than gamma‑prime precipitation, which simplifies weldability and gives stable properties under repeated high‑temperature exposure.
