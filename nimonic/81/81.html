Nimonic 81

Aurico Alloys LLP: Premier Nimonic 81 sheets supplier, stockist & exporter in India to abroad.

Key Properties

  • Chromium 30% nominal, well above the 18-21% of the 75 / 80A / 90 line, giving markedly better hot corrosion resistance.
  • Titanium 1.8% and aluminium 0.9%, nominal — Special Metals publishes a nominal composition for this grade rather than limits.
  • No UNS number is published for it. The identity table below records a dash, which is the correct published answer and not a gap.
  • Supplied to mill specification in every form.

Applications

  • Exhaust valves in high-sulphur fuel service
  • Gas turbine hot-section hardware
  • Components exposed to hot corrosion

Available Forms

  • Nimonic 81 Plates
  • Nimonic 81 Round Bars
  • Nimonic 81 Sheets
