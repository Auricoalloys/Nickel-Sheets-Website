The turning point came when the boiler OEM switched tube shields and baffles from low‑alloy steels to Nimonic 81 Plates a 30% chromium nickel‑base alloy whose descaled weight loss in 75% Na₂SO₄ + 25% NaCl at 800°C is several times lower than Nimonic 80A, giving dramatically longer life in molten‑salt and vanadium pentoxide attack from high‑sulfur, high‑vanadium fuels.

Nimonic 81 (often listed as a precipitation‑hardenable nickel‑chromium alloy) was developed specifically for hot‑corrosion environments such as gas turbines, piston‑engine exhaust valves, and heat‑exchange plant contaminated by alkali metal sulfates and chlorides in flue gas. Compared with Nimonic 80A, it keeps similar elevated‑temperature strength but adds higher chromium (~30%) and optimized chemistry for much better resistance in SO₂, fuel ash, and salt‑shower tests at 800°C, where 80A shows several‑fold higher descaled weight loss.
