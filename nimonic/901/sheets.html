Applications & Why Choose Nimonic 901

Why this grade

Nimonic 901 substitutes a large iron fraction for nickel and is age-hardened with titanium, which makes it materially cheaper than the fully nickel-base grades. It is a forging alloy for turbine discs, shafts and rings running to around 600°C, where the higher-temperature capability of 90 or 105 would simply go unused.

  • Chemical: High-pressure reactor vessels, heat exchanger tubesheets – superior to stainless in reducing environments]
  • Oil & Gas: Compressor discs, turbine shafts – 30% higher creep life than 718
  • Marine: Propulsion shafts, gear components – excellent forgeability
  • Pharma: Sterile processing autoclaves – maintains strength post-aging
  • Power: Gas turbine discs & rings, casings/seals – industry standard
  • OEM/Hardware: Precision forgings from ready sheet stock
