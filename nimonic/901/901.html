Nimonic 901

Aurico Alloys LLP: Trusted Nimonic 901 sheets supplier, stockist & exporter in India to abroad.

Key Properties

  • Iron balance, not nickel — nickel 42.5% nominal, which is what makes it the economical grade in this family.
  • Titanium 2.9% nominal with molybdenum 5.75% and chromium 12.5%, all nominal.
  • Special Metals publishes this table as nominal, not for specification purposes; the figures above are quoted on that basis.
  • AMS 5660 / 5661 for bar.

Applications

  • Gas turbine discs and shafts
  • Compressor rotors and spacers
  • High-strength bolting at moderate temperature

Available Forms

  • Nimonic 901 Plates
  • Nimonic 901 Round Bars
  • Nimonic 901 Sheets
