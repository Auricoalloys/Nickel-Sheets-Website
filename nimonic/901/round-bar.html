    Nimonic 901 (UNS N09901, Werkstoff 2.4662, AMS 5660/5661, ASTM B463) is an age-hardenable nickel-iron-chromium superalloy with substantial molybdenum, titanium, and aluminum. It is valued for ultimate yield strength, toughness, creep resistance up to 600°C, and excellent corrosion protection in jet engine, refinery, turbine, and marine settings. Its structure, reinforced by Ti and Al, delivers predictable hardening and stress resistance where other alloys fade.

    Available in round bars from 5 to 200mm diameter, each batch is supplied to ASTM, AMS, DIN, and ISO standards with full MTC documentation for vital traceability. Providers of Nimonic 901 Round Bars and Inconel 718 Round Bars offer rapid global supply, certified packaging, and customized sizes for urgent projects and plant shutdowns.

    Applications & Benefits

    Why this grade

    Nimonic 901 substitutes a large iron fraction for nickel and is age-hardened with titanium, which makes it materially cheaper than the fully nickel-base grades. It is a forging alloy for turbine discs, shafts and rings running to around 600°C, where the higher-temperature capability of 90 or 105 would simply go unused.

    • Chemical: gas reaction rings, furnace tubes, refinery seals.
    • Marine: shaft seals, engine disc rings, corrosion-exposed fasteners.
    • Oil & Gas: turbine engine discs, casing, shafts.
    • Pharma: process plant springs, sterilizer valve bars.
    • Power Generation: generator rings, steam turbine casings, high-temperature bolting.
    • Hardware/OEM: custom nonmagnetic fasteners, high-strength round rods.
