NiCr 70:30

NiCr 70:30 raises the chromium to 30%, which lifts oxidation and carburisation resistance to 1300 °C. Reformers, incinerators and kiln furniture are the usual applications.

Key Properties

  • Chromium 29-31%, half again as much as 80:20, which is what lifts carburisation and sulphur resistance.
  • Nickel balance with iron under 1%.
  • Carbon 0.07% maximum, tighter than 80:20's 0.15%.
  • Specified where the atmosphere attacks the scale, not merely where the temperature is high.

Applications

  • Reformer and incinerator internals
  • Kiln furniture in sulphur-bearing atmospheres
  • Heating elements in carburising service
