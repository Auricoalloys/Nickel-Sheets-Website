NiCr 80:20

NiCr 80:20 is the classic 80% nickel, 20% chromium resistance heating alloy, with oxidation resistance to 1200 °C. It is used for furnace muffles, heating elements and reformer internals.

Key Properties

  • Nickel balance with iron held under 1% — it is a genuinely nickel-based alloy, which is what puts it at the top of the range for service temperature.
  • Chromium 19-21%, forming the chromia scale the alloy depends on.
  • Silicon 0.75-1.75%, a range with a minimum, aiding scale formation.
  • As nickel falls through the range — 60:15, 40:20, 30:20 — iron replaces it and the cost falls with the temperature rating.

Applications

  • Furnace heating elements and muffles
  • Reformer and kiln internals
  • Industrial heaters and load banks
