NiCr 60:15

NiCr 60:15 is a nickel-iron-chromium resistance alloy holding oxidation resistance and mechanical stability to 1150 °C, specified where reformer and boiler components need more than an iron-based grade without the cost of 80:20.

Available Forms

  • NiCr 60:15 Plates
  • NiCr 60:15 Round Bars
  • NiCr 60:15 Sheets
