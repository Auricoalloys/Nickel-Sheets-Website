NiCr 40:20

NiCr 40:20 is a cost-effective resistance alloy rated to 1050 °C, chosen for its high electrical resistivity and form stability in heating elements and furnace components.

Key Properties

  • Nickel 37% minimum on an iron balance, with chromium 18-22%.
  • Silicon 1.8-3.0%, markedly higher than the nickel-rich grades — silicon does part of the work chromium and nickel do in 80:20.
  • Carbon 0.10% maximum.
  • No UNS number is published for it; the identity table below records a dash, which is the correct published answer.

Applications

  • Heating elements and furnace components
  • Resistor banks and load banks
  • Industrial heaters at moderate temperature

Available Forms

  • NiCr 40:20 Plates
  • NiCr 40:20 Round Bars
  • NiCr 40:20 Sheets
