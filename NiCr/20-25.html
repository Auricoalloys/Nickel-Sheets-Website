NiCr 20:25

NiCr 20:25 is the chromium-rich member of the range at 20% nickel and 25% chromium, giving oxidation and carburisation resistance to 1100 °C for reformer and furnace components.

Key Properties

  • Chromium 23-27% with nickel only 18% minimum — the one grade in the range where chromium exceeds nickel, and it is the chromium doing the work.
  • Iron balance, the highest iron content of the six.
  • Silicon 1.0-1.5% and carbon 0.10% maximum.
  • No UNS number is published for it; the identity table records a dash accordingly.

Applications

  • Reformer and furnace components
  • Heating elements in oxidising atmospheres
  • Kiln and calciner internals

Available Forms

  • NiCr 20:25 Plates
  • NiCr 20:25 Round Bars
  • NiCr 20:25 Sheets
