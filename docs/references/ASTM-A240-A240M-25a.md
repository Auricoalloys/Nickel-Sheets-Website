# ASTM A240/A240M-25a — Table 1 Chemical Composition Requirements

Source: ASTM A240/A240M Standard Specification for Chromium and
Chromium-Nickel Stainless Steel Plate, Sheet, and Strip for Pressure
Vessels and for General Applications. Edition 25a (latest as of 2026-08).

Supplied as screenshot by the business owner, 2026-08-27.
Used as the chemistry source for grades where no separate mill bulletin
has been read. Cited in docs/chemistry.csv as "ASTM A240/A240M-25a".

## Footnotes (relevant)

A  Maximum, unless range or minimum is indicated. Ellipses (…) mean
   no requirement — the element need not be determined or reported.
D  Carbon reported to nearest 0.01% except for low-carbon types
   (nearest 0.001%).
E  Columbium (Cb) and niobium (Nb) refer to the same element.
G  Common name, not a trademark. Not associated with any one producer.
K  Cr + 3.3 Mo + 16 N = 41 min  (applies to S32750 / 2507)
L  Cr + 3.3(Mo + ½W) + 16 N = 41 min  (applies to S32760 / Zeron 100)
