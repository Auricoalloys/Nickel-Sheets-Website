Alloy 926 foil, also known as Cronifer 1925 hMo (Werkstoff 1.4529), bridges the gap between costly nickel alloys and standard austenitics, delivering unmatched resistance to chloride pitting, crevice corrosion, and stress cracking in sulfuric/phosphoric acids, brine, and sour gas—environments where 904L or 316L fail prematurely. With 6-7% Mo, 24-26% Ni, and nitrogen stabilization, its high PREN (≈42–45) ensures superior performance in hot brine, outperforming Alloy 926 vs 904L foil comparisons for pitting resistance.

Tailored for chemical processing units, offshore oil & gas instrumentation, marine pump seals, pharma reactors, and power FGD systems, these thin gauge foils (0.1mm to 0.5mm) form gaskets, liners, shims, and bipolar plates.
