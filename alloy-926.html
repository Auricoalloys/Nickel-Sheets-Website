Alloy 926 (UNS N08926)

Alloy 926 (UNS N08926, W.Nr. 1.4529) is a 6% molybdenum super-austenitic stainless with a copper addition and nitrogen strengthening. Its pitting resistance places it alongside AL-6XN and 254 SMO, and it is specified for seawater handling, desalination, offshore process piping, pulp bleaching and flue-gas desulphurisation.
